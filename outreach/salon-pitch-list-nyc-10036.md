## Pitch playbook (quick)

- **Best window:** Tue–Thu, 10:00am–12:30pm — after open, before the lunch/matinee rush. Never Fri–Sat.
- **Ask for:** the owner or salon manager by name; if absent, leave the one-pager and get their name + best time.
- **Bring:** a small sample tray (5–8 pieces), a one-page partnership sheet, counter cards with a QR code to roomysjewelery.store, and a simple commission table.
- **Offer models (pick per venue):**
  1. Countertop display case on consignment — salon earns 15–25% of sales, zero inventory risk.
  2. Stylist referral code — stylist earns a flat fee or % on redeemed purchases.
  3. Event night — "jewels & blowouts" trunk show; you bring bubbly, they bring the client list.
  4. Bridal package tie-in (RK Bridal, blow dry bars, hotel spas).
- **Route suggestion:** Day 1: W 46th "Salon Row" (144 → 352 → 429 → 451) + Elite Day Spa + Nail WAH. Day 2: 8th/9th/10th Ave sweep (CityStar → Renew York → Hell's Kitchen Barbers → HK Best → Drybar 42nd → NAMS + 10th Ave nail cluster). Day 3: 10018/10019 outliers + RK Bridal.
