India's gold imports fall 81.27% to $1.8bn in September

Silver imports fell 49.11% to $665.08m, on Commerce Ministry data released 15 October. From April to September, gold imports were down 27.32% at $19.27bn against the same months of 2025.

By Carat Capital

19 October 2026

What changed

Gold imports fell for a second month. India imported $1.8 billion of gold in September, 81.27% less than a year earlier, Commerce Ministry data released 15 October show. Silver imports roughly halved.

What it means

  • If you import bullion: The duty has been 15% since 13 May, up from 6%.
  • If you make for India's market: Less metal is entering the trade, but retail prices still follow the world price and the rupee.
  • What this is not: Proof the duty did it. Neither named cause is quantified.

A second month of steep falls

August fell first. India's August gold imports had dropped 45% to $2.3 billion. September's $1.8 billion sits about a fifth below that.

The half-year confirms it. From April to September, gold imports fell 27.32% on the same months of 2025, to $19.27 billion. Silver fell 25.17% to $2.4 billion over the same six months.

Two causes named, neither measured

The duty rose. The government raised the import duty on precious metals to 15% from 6%, effective 13 May.

An appeal followed. Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged buyers to defer gold purchases amid the West Asia crisis, Kotak reports. The data do not separate the appeal from the duty.

Switzerland still supplies most

Supply is concentrated. Switzerland supplied about 40% of India's gold imports, the UAE more than 16% and South Africa about 10%, Kotak reports.

What to watch

  • Mid-NovemberOctober's trade data. A third fall would extend the pattern into the wedding season; a rebound would test the duty reading.
  • Around 20 OctoberSwiss customs' September gold exports by destination, the supply side of India's figure.
  • OngoingAny change to the 15% duty. None is announced.

What would change this call

A rebound in October with the duty unchanged. That would point to timing, or to the purchase appeal fading, rather than a lasting fall in India's gold demand.

How India's import figure is built

The Commerce Ministry publishes monthly merchandise trade by commodity, in US dollars, around the middle of the following month. The figures are provisional and are revised. A value series moves with the gold price as well as with tonnes, so a fall in dollars is not by itself a fall in weight.
