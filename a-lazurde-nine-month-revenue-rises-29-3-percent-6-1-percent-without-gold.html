Lazurde nine-month revenue rises 29.3%, 6.1% without gold

Net profit was SAR 0.2 million against a SAR 16.8 million loss in the nine months to 30 September, preliminary results show. Third-quarter retail revenue fell 4.4%.

By Carat Capital

11 November 2026

What changed

Without gold, 6.1%. Lazurde's nine-month revenue rose 29.3% to SAR 2,940.4 million. Its operating revenue, which strips out the gold's value, rose 6.1%, preliminary results show.

What it means

  • If you supply Saudi retailers: Lazurde's third-quarter retail revenue fell 4.4%, though most like-for-like Saudi stores grew 3%.
  • If you sell wholesale: its wholesale revenue rose 12% over nine months, on higher making charges, it says.
  • What this is not: a large profit. Nine-month net profit was SAR 0.2 million.

Gold swelled the top line.

Total up 29.3%. Revenue reached SAR 2,940.4 million against SAR 2,274.0 million. Lazurde credits better operations and higher world gold prices.

Ex-gold up 6.1%. Operating revenue, the company's measure without the gold's value, rose to SAR 448.0 million from SAR 422.3 million.

Retail slipped in the quarter.

Q3 retail down 4.4%. Lazurde attributes the third-quarter fall to geopolitical conditions in the region. Most of its like-for-like Saudi stores grew 3%, it says.

Nine months up 1%. Retail revenue rose 1% over nine months and makes 50% of the group's operating revenue. E-commerce sales grew 10%.

Net profit returned, thinly.

Q3 net profit returned. Lazurde made a net profit of SAR 1.8 million in the third quarter against a SAR 4.3 million loss a year earlier.

A one-off gold loss. Nine-month profit absorbed a SAR 5.7 million unrealised loss on the fair value of gold stock, the company says.

Equity fell. Shareholders' equity was SAR 236.3 million against SAR 269.1 million. Excluding a currency translation reserve, it was SAR 603 million, Lazurde says.

What to watch

  • 31 December 2026Lazurde's financial year ends. The test: whether retail revenue rises in the fourth quarter after the third quarter's 4.4% fall.

What would change this call

If the full-year statements show operating revenue growth below 6.1% for the year, the nine months' rise would read as the gold price alone.

How Lazurde's two revenue lines differ

Total revenue includes the value of the gold in each piece, so it rises with the metal price. Operating revenue, the company's own measure, excludes the gold's value and its price rise, leaving making charges and margin.
