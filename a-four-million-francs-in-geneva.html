Watches

Patek Philippe split-seconds carries CHF 4m estimate at Phillips

The 1945 chronograph heads Phillips' Geneva Watch Auction XXIV on 7 and 8 November, a sale of more than 200 lots. Phillips gives it CHF 2m to 4m and a Paul Newman Daytona CHF 800,000 to 1.6m.

By The Watches Desk

18 September 2026 Corrected

What changed

Phillips set the estimate. A 1945 Patek Philippe split-seconds chronograph, lot 95, carries an estimate of CHF 2 million to 4 million at Phillips' Geneva Watch Auction XXIV.

What it means

  • If you own a vintage Patek: Phillips' sale page gives lot 95 CHF 2 million to 4 million. It sold on 7 November for CHF 6,267,000, premium included.
  • If you consign to the autumn season: this sale opens it, on 7 and 8 November.
  • What this is not: a price. An estimate is the house's opinion before the room bids.

Source: Phillips' sale page for Geneva Watch Auction XXIV, read 8 November 2026, and Rapaport, 16 September 2026 · estimates in Swiss francs, excluding buyer's premium

Patek leads the catalogue

Two to four million. The watch has a silver-toned dial with steel hour markers and a tachymeter scale. Rapaport reported only the CHF 4 million upper figure on 16 September. Phillips' sale page gives CHF 2,000,000 to 4,000,000.

Two Rolexes and two Journes

Journe carries a floor. An F.P. Journe Résonance with a hand-engraved pink-gold dial, lot 7, is estimated by Phillips at in excess of CHF 1 million. Rapaport reported CHF 1 million to CHF 2 million.

Rolex takes two places. Lot 20, a yellow-gold Daytona with a Paul Newman dial, carries CHF 800,000 to 1.6 million.

Stelline carries a floor. Lot 129, a 1952 Rolex with a Stelline dial, carries in excess of CHF 600,000. Rapaport reported CHF 1.2 million.

The autumn season opens here

Two days, 200 lots. The sale runs on 7 and 8 November with more than 200 timepieces, held with Bacs & Russo, and opens the autumn live season.

The last Geneva sale. Phillips' Geneva Watch Auction XXIII realised $96.3 million in May.

What to watch

  • 8 November 2026The sale's second session, with lots 129 and 161. The test: whether each sells inside or above the house's estimate.

What would change this call

The room. Lot 95's hammer of CHF 5.1 million on 7 November already sits above its CHF 4 million high estimate; the second session decides lots 129 and 161.

How a Phillips estimate is read

Phillips publishes a band, a low and a high, for most lots, and a floor ("in excess of") for a few. Estimates exclude buyer's premium; the Sold For price includes it. Rapaport's 16 September report gave the upper figures, which is why this page first printed four of five lots as upper only.

Method

Rapaport's report of 16 September 2026 is the source for the lots, the estimates and the dates, and it attributes them to Phillips' own announcement of Tuesday 15 September. The dollar figures are Rapaport's conversion and are printed at its own precision rather than recomputed at a rate this desk cannot date. The $96.3 million comparison for the previous Geneva sale comes from this paper's own coverage of 9 July 2026 and is linked where it is used.
