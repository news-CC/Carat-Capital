Richard Mille takes GB1 timekeeping for the 38th America's Cup

The British challenger races the second preliminary regatta at Naples from 24 to 27 September, and will launch a reconfigured AC75 by the end of 2026. No terms were disclosed.

By The Watches Desk25 September 2026Brief 30 sec · Full read 1 min
What changed

No price was published. Richard Mille has become Official Timekeeping Partner of GB1, the British challenger for the 38th America's Cup in Naples in 2027. The announcement carried no financial terms.

What it means · The Desk’s View

If you retail the brand: A sailing tie gives a counter a story with dates on it. The first regatta window closes on 27 September.

If you track sponsorship value: Nothing is published. No fee, term or activation budget appears in the announcement.

What this is not: A watch launch. No model, reference or price is attached to the partnership, only the naming of a timekeeping partner.

Who wears what the announcement says Fletcher "alongside key members of the team" will wear Richard Mille watches through the campaign, and separately names four more sailors in the squad; it does not say which of them wear the watches, and no model or reference is given. Counting the squad the five in the table are the sailors the announcement names by name. It does not state a full crew size, and the AC75's rebuilt layout is described as accommodating five sailing crew cockpits, which is a boat specification and not a headcount of the squad. The dateline the announcement is dated 24 September and says it was made ahead of the 24–27 September regatta, so the event and the announcement share a window rather than a single day. A weekly digest on another outlet carried it the following day; that later wrapper date is not used here.

IThe boat is a rebuild

The hull is kept. GB1 retains the core structural hull from its 2024 campaign, with a rebuilt internal layout for five sailing cockpits.

The systems are new. The team lists battery-based energy storage, upgraded control electronics, appendage interfaces and foil systems for the 2027 rule cycle.

IISix nations race at Naples

The regatta is one-design. Six nations race AC40 foiling yachts at Naples from 24 to 27 September, GB1's first racing on those waters.

A fourth attempt. The British team returns for its fourth America's Cup campaign, representing the Royal Yacht Squadron as Challenger of Record.

IIIThe brand's reason, in its words

Precision is the pitch. Peter Harrison, chief executive of Richard Mille EMEA, said the "38th America's Cup rewards precision, relentless innovation and resilience".

The sport is old. The trophy dates to 1851, when the schooner America sailed to Britain to race the British fleet.

01What would change this call+

A published figure. This partnership is worth nothing on paper until a fee, a term or a product commitment is disclosed, and none is; a later filing or interview that puts a number on it would make it measurable either way. A change of Challenger of Record or a withdrawal from the 2027 event would end the association before it produced anything a counter could sell against.

02A timekeeping partnership is a naming right, not a supply contract+

Official Timekeeping Partner is a category sponsorship: the brand buys exclusivity in the timing category for an event or a team, and usually the right to place logos, host guests and put product on the wrists of named participants. It is not the same as supplying the instruments that actually time the racing, which in modern America's Cup competition is done by the event's own tracking and telemetry systems rather than by a wristwatch. It is also not a licensing deal, so it does not by itself produce a commemorative model, although brands frequently follow one with a limited edition. The value to a retailer is therefore narrative rather than allocation: the story has dates, names and a location, and it ends in a scheduled event. Whether it moves stock depends on whether a product follows, which nothing in this announcement promises.
