Il Ponte's jewellery sale totals over €3 million, 98% of lots sold

The Milan house's off-calendar Important Jewellery auction ran on 7 and 8 October. A Nardi coral necklace and a Cartier pearl-and-diamond choker each sold for €88,200, among the highlights it names.

By Carat Capital

27 October 2026 Corrected

What changed

Almost every lot sold. Il Ponte's off-calendar Important Jewellery auction in Milan, held on 7 and 8 October, sold 98% of its lots for over €3 million, the house says. Every lot in the first session sold.

What it means

  • If you consign signed jewels in Europe: Il Ponte's regular November sale follows. Ask for its estimates against these prices.
  • If you buy at Italian sales: Signed coral, jadeite and Central European gold made the named results, €37,800 to €88,200.
  • What this is not: A market index. One house reports one sale, and the release prints no estimates.

Coral and Cartier led.

Two at €88,200. A Nardi three-strand coral bead necklace, lot 77, and a Cartier pearl and old-cut diamond choker, lot 92, each sold for €88,200.

Central Europe sold. A Krauss cabochon tourmaline necklace made €44,100. A Lährm gold chain hung with 17 pocket watches made €41,580, the house says.

Jadeite drew bidders.

Jade and onyx. A carved jadeite, onyx and diamond belt sold for €41,580. A pair of diamond, coral and jadeite pendant earrings made €37,800.

Italian names held. Necklaces by Buccellati and Bulgari each made €37,800. The Bulgari chain carries a 12.81-carat yellow sapphire.

November sale is next.

A further test. Il Ponte says its regular November jewellery auction will offer another reading of demand. The release gives no date or estimates for it.

What to watch

  • November 2026Il Ponte's regular Important Jewellery sale. The test: sell-through near 98% again.

Corrections1 note

Clarified, 30 October 2026: a key-figure label said the over-€3 million total came from 'two sessions'. Il Ponte's release names the sale dates, 7 and 8 October, and a first session; it does not say there were two. The label now reads '7–8 Oct'. The Keep-reading line also called a 10 October unsold lot the auctions desk's last result; two later results had run, on 19 and 24 October. It now points to the 24 October Lyon & Turnbull sale. What was right as published: every figure, including over €3 million and 98%. Found by Carat Capital's Standards desk on 27 October 2026.

What would change this call

A November sale well below 98% sold would make the October result an off-calendar exception, not a reading of Milan demand.

What the totals count

The house gives a total 'over €3 million' and a 98% share of lots sold. It prints no lot count, no estimates and no premium basis. Lot prices are as printed.
