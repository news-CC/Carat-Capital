Carat Capital · Brand Book03 · The idea

The idea

A diamond that is also a pen

Printers set type in points. Dealers weigh diamonds in points: one point is a hundredth of a carat. The mark sits where the two trades meet.

The stone

A round brilliant seen from the side. The crown is cut at 34.5°, the angle Marcel Tolkowsky published in Diamond Design in 1919.

The pen

Its pavilion is a nib. The slit runs down the centre facet, and the two pavilion mains become the tines.

The point

The nib’s breather hole holds a red point. The same point closes the name, so the symbol and the wordmark end on one mark.

Carat Capital · Brand Book04 · The symbol

The symbol

Six parts, all from the stone

Every line in the mark is a facet edge or the slit of a nib. Nothing is there for decoration, which is why it holds together at every size.

The symbol is always drawn with the table up and the point red. It never sits in a circle, badge or frame.

Carat Capital · Brand Book06 · Optical sizes

Optical sizes

Three cuts for three distances

Fine knockouts disappear on small screens, so the mark comes in three cuts. Each keeps the silhouette and the point and drops detail as it shrinks.

L cut

96 px and up · 25 mm and up

All facets. The master drawing.

M cut

32–96 px · 9–25 mm

Crown facets removed, knockouts doubled.

S cut

16–32 px · under 9 mm

Girdle, slit and point only. Favicons and app bars.

Carat Capital · Brand Book07 · The engraved cut

The engraved cut

For print that invites a closer look

Each facet is hatched at its own angle and density, lit from the upper left, so the stone reads in pure line. Use it on certificates, the Almanac cover, foil and blind stamping, and letterpress.

Use it at 40 mm or 400 px and larger. Below that, use the L cut.

Carat Capital · Brand Book08 · The wordmark

The wordmark

Instrument Sans Bold, and one red point

The name is set in Instrument Sans Bold capitals, the face the paper’s nameplate has always used. The full stop is drawn as the point.

Display cut

Tracking −20, word space +0.06 em. For every lockup and the masthead, at a cap height of 24 px or 6 mm and up.

Text cut

Tracking 0, word space +0.06 em. A little more air for small sizes, navigation bars and live web text. Carat Capital

Artwork, not type

Use the files; don’t retype the name. The website is the one exception: it sets the name live in Instrument Sans Bold and draws the point in CSS to the same measurements.

Clear space and minimum size

Give it one cap height

Keep everything else at least x away from the lockup on every side, where x is the cap height of the C.

Lockup with tagline180 px · 45 mm wide
Lockup, no tagline120 px · 30 mm
Stacked lockup100 px · 25 mm
Wordmark90 px · 22 mm
Symbol, S cut16 px · 5 mm

Below 180 px the tagline stops being legible, so drop it rather than shrink it.

Carat Capital · Brand Book16 · On screens

On screens

Small, and still the stone

The S cut carries the favicon and app bars. Avatars and app icons use the L and M cuts on Black or Pearl White, with the stone at about 62% of the width so circular crops never touch it.

In print

Cards, letters and the brief

Stationery uses the engraved cut in pearl foil on Black stock, and the lockup in Black on Pearl White stock. The Morning Brief opens with the lockup and a date line in Plex Mono.

Carat Capital · Brand Book18 · Files

Files

Everything in brand/logo

SVG for anything that scales, PNG where a platform asks for pixels. Type is outlined and knockouts are transparent, so every file sits on any background.

The drawings are generated from brand/source/point.js. Change a proportion there and every file, and this book, rebuilds to match.

svg/

Lockups (horizontal, stacked, wordmark) in Black, reverse, pearl foil and one-colour; the symbol in L, M and S cuts, pearl foil and engraved. (Files named gold and paper keep their names and now carry the pearl lustre and Pearl White.)

png/

Transparent PNGs of the lockups at 2400 px, the symbol at 1024 px, the engraved cut at 2048 px.

favicon/

favicon.svg (switches to Pearl White in dark browsers), favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), apple-touch-icon, 192 and 512 px icons.

social/

Avatars on Pearl White and on Black at 1024 px, and the 1200 × 630 share card.
