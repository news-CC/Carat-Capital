Royal Gold's Q3 revenue rises 84% to $464.8m, debt cleared in October

Operating cash flow reached a record $344.4m on 109,000 gold-equivalent ounces sold, against 72,900 a year earlier. The last $150m on its credit facility was repaid on 19 October.

By Carat Capital

5 November 2026 Corrected

What changed

Revenue rose 84%. Royal Gold's Q3 revenue was $464.8m against $252.1m a year earlier. Gold brought in $340.3m of it.

What it means

  • If you price gold jewellery: Gold averaged $4,262 an ounce in the quarter. The tape's $4,123.20 bid is 3.3% lower.
  • If you hold Royal Gold: Earnings per share rose to $2.68 from $1.92, on 84.6m weighted shares against 65.8m.
  • What this is not: Growth on the same assets. The year-ago quarter preceded the Sandstorm and Horizon deal of October 2025.

Key figures

RevenueQ3 2026, against $252.1m in Q3 2025$464.8m
Operating cash flowa record, it says; against $174.0m$344.4m

Credit facility undrawnafter the last $150m, 19 October$1.4bn

Source: Royal Gold, third-quarter 2026 results, Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K, 4 November 2026; quarter to 30 September 2026.

Price and volume both rose.

Gold up 23%. Gold averaged $4,262 an ounce in the quarter against $3,457 a year earlier, the company's table shows. GEOs sold rose 50% to what Royal Gold calls a record.

The deal is inside. The year-ago quarter preceded the Sandstorm and Horizon transaction, closed in October 2025. Revenue from South and Central America rose to $111.5m from $32.2m. The release does not split the growth between the deal and the price.

CEO: repaid in under a year.

Cash flow nearly doubled. Operating cash flow was $344.4m against $174.0m a year earlier, a record, the company says.

Two years became one. Royal Gold had $1.225bn of debt after closing the deal and expected to repay it within two years, its chief executive says. It repaid $250m in the quarter.

The last $150m. The remaining $150m went on 19 October, leaving the $1.4bn credit facility undrawn. Its chief executive says the balance was fully repaid just under a year after closing; the release does not itemise earlier repayments.

Gold guidance stays unchanged.

Gold range holds. Royal Gold keeps its 2026 gold sales guidance at 290,000 to 320,000 ounces, with 224,265 sold through September. It expects silver and copper at or above the top of their ranges.

Other metals raised. Guidance for other metals revenue rises to $53m to $56m, from a top of $38m.

Shares bought back. It repurchased 190,045 shares in the quarter at an average $259.83, for $49m.

What to watch

  • Q4 2026 results, date not announcedFull-year gold sales. The test: whether they reach the 290,000 to 320,000 ounce range, which needs 65,735 to 95,735 more ounces.
  • In the coming days, Royal Gold saysThe final $50m Warintza advance, after its security documents are registered. The test: whether the facility stays undrawn.

Corrections1 note

Clarified, 6 November 2026: a sub-head on this page read "Debt repaid in under a year." in this paper's voice, while the page printed only the month of the deal's closing and two of the repayments. The sub-head now attributes the claim, and the paragraph gives the chief executive's own wording, "just under one year" after closing in October 2025, and says the release does not itemise earlier repayments. "Record" is now attributed to the company. No printed figure changed. Raised by Carat Capital's Standards desk on 5 November 2026.

What would change this call

If Royal Gold splits out the interests it acquired and they explain little of the $212.7m rise in revenue, the quarter was the gold price on the old portfolio, not the deal.

How a GEO is counted

Royal Gold defines gold-equivalent ounces as revenue for a period divided by the average LBMA PM gold fixing price for that period. $464.8m over $4,262 gives about 109,057, against the printed 109,000.

Revenue table: 'Gold $ 340.3 $ 196.6 73 %', 'Total revenue $ 464.8 $ 252.1 84 %', 'GEOs 109,000 72,900 50 %'. Average prices: 'Gold ($/oz) $4,262 $3,457 23%'. Guidance table: gold 290,000–320,000 oz, 224,265 through 30 September; other metals $34–$38m revised to $53–$56m. Region table: South and Central America $111.5m against $32.2m. Arithmetic, ours: 464.8 / 252.1 = 1.844, the release's 84%; 340.3 / 464.8 = 73.2% of revenue; 464.8 - 252.1 = 212.7; 4,123.20 / 4,262 = 0.967, so the tape is 3.3% below the quarter's average; 290,000 - 224,265 = 65,735 and 320,000 - 224,265 = 95,735.
