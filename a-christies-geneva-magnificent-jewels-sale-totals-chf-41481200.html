Christie's Geneva Magnificent Jewels sale totals CHF 41,481,200

The 10 November sale included the Bulgari Pink at CHF 12,086,000. Christie's sale page gives totals as hammer price plus buyer's premium; a connoisseur's collection sale, part I, made CHF 20,916,480.

By Carat Capital

14 November 2026

What changed

The sale total is in. Christie's Magnificent Jewels sale in Geneva on 10 November totalled CHF 41,481,200 including buyer's premium, its sale page shows.

What it means

  • If you consign to Geneva: the two jewel sales that day made CHF 62,397,680, on Carat Capital's addition.
  • If you value pink diamonds: the Bulgari Pink's price would be 29.1% of the sale total if both are on the same basis.
  • What this is not: a result against estimate. Christie's estimates exclude the premium and its totals include it.

Key figures

Magnificent Jewels, totalincluding buyer's premiumCHF 41,481,200
Connoisseur's Collection part I, totalincluding buyer's premiumCHF 20,916,480
Bulgari Pinkbasis not stated by Christie'sCHF 12,086,000

Source: Christie's sale pages, read 14 November 2026 · sales held 10 November 2026 in Geneva · totals are hammer price plus buyer's premium, per the pages

Two jewel sales, one day.

Magnificent Jewels: CHF 41.5m. The Magnificent Jewels sale, which included the Bulgari Pink, totalled CHF 41,481,200, Christie's sale page shows.

Part I: CHF 20.9m. Exceptional Jewels & Objects of Elegance, part I of a connoisseur's collection, made CHF 20,916,480 the same day.

The pink's share is uncertain.

Totals include premium. Both sale pages say totals are hammer price plus buyer's premium, and that estimates exclude it.

Lot basis unstated. Christie's 10 November release gave the Bulgari Pink at CHF 12,086,000 without naming a basis. On the same basis as the total, it would be 29.1%.

What to watch

  • From 14 November 2026A lot-level result for the Bulgari Pink. The test: whether Christie's states that its price includes the premium.

The story so far

  1. 13 November 2026The Bulgari Pink sold for CHF 12,086,000; the release did not state the price basis.
  2. 9 October 2026The preview: a CHF 10 million to CHF 15 million estimate.

Go deeper

What would change this call

If the Bulgari Pink's price excludes the premium, its share of the premium-inclusive total is larger than 29.1%.

Hammer and premium

The hammer price is the winning bid. The house adds a buyer's premium on top. Christie's sale totals include it; its estimates do not.

Method

Christie's sale pages, door.py 06:01 EST 14 November 2026: auction 31087, 200, 230,639 bytes, '41,481,200' 2 hits; auction 31493, 200, 237,705 bytes, '20,916,480' 2 hits. Each page's tooltip reads 'Estimates do not include buyer's premium. Sales totals are hammer price plus buyer's premium.' (1 hit on each). The lot-level price is not in either page's bytes. Christie's press release of 10 November, re-fetched 06:05 EST 14 November, 301 to 200, 152,526 bytes, the size read on 13 November: 'CHF12,086,000' 6, 'premium' 0, 'hammer' 0, 'fees' 0. Carat Capital's arithmetic: 41,481,200 + 20,916,480 = 62,397,680; 12,086,000/41,481,200 = 0.2914, so 29.1% of the total on a common basis.
