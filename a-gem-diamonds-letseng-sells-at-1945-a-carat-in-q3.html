Gem Diamonds' Letšeng averages $1,945 a carat in Q3, up 73% on year

Gem Diamonds sold 17,899 carats from Letšeng in the third quarter for $34.8m, against 22,830 carats and $25.7m a year earlier. Four stones above $1m each brought in $22.2m, and the miner raised its 2026 guidance.

By Carat Capital

4 November 2026

What changed

Price rose as volume fell. Letšeng sold 21.6% fewer carats in the third quarter than a year earlier, at $1,945 a carat. Revenue rose to $34.8m.

What it means

  • If you tender large rough: Four stones made 64% of quarterly revenue; the average tracks a few large goods.
  • If you lend to or hold Gem: The credit lines expire on 21 December; Gem says renewal is progressing.
  • What this is not: A market-wide price rise. Gem's chief executive calls prices firm for Letšeng-quality goods only.

Four stones carried the quarter.

Four above $1m. Four diamonds sold for more than $1m each and brought in $22.2m. Gem says the 347-carat Lesotho Jubilee, sold in the quarter, lifted the average.

Up 73% on year. Against $1,124 a carat in Q3 2025, the average is up 73%. Revenue was $25.7m a year earlier.

Recoveries fell too. Carats recovered fell to 19,299 from 22,268 a year earlier. Ore came mainly from the Main Pipe, in line with the mine plan.

Guidance rises before a deadline.

More carats expected. Gem raised its 2026 guidance for carats recovered to 78,000 to 80,000, from 75,000 to 78,000. It cites operating performance and cost control.

Facilities due 21 December. Gem's revolving credit facilities expire on 21 December. It says commercial terms are being finalised with Nedbank and Standard Bank.

Lesotho sets a variable royalty.

Three to six percent. Lesotho approved a royalty of 3% to 6%, set on the six-monthly average price per carat. It applies to sales after 1 October.

What to watch

  • By 21 December 2026Renewal of Gem's revolving credit facilities. The test: whether terms are signed before they expire.
  • Full-year 2026 report, date not announcedThe year's carats recovered. The test: whether they land inside the raised 78,000 to 80,000 range.

What would change this call

A fourth quarter that holds near $1,945 a carat without stones above $1m would mean the run of goods, not a few large stones, is lifting Letšeng's price.

How the average is set

Gem reports the average as revenue divided by carats sold. Here $34.8m over 17,899 carats gives about $1,944, against the printed $1,945.
