What’s moving now

Economics · Global

Lab-grown settles into its true business model

At an 86% discount to natural, LGD is no longer a diamond substitute — it's a fashion-jewelry category with diamond optics. Margins migrate from the stone to the brand and the volume.

Channels · Shenzhen

Live selling jumps the Pacific

The livestream counter that built China's jewelry e-commerce is landing in the West — TikTok gem sales, WhatsApp private clienteling, and jewelers becoming broadcasters.

Technology · London

Traceability tech grows teeth

Blockchain provenance, ledgers, and assay-office digital passports move from pilot to mandate as regulation and retailer policy converge. The tech stack becomes a condition of shelf space.

The working vocabulary

The trade’s terms, plainly told. More in the Field Guide.

Lab-grown (LGD) Term
Diamonds grown in weeks by HPHT or CVD — chemically identical to mined, economically a different universe. Their price curve is a technology curve, which is the whole story.
Clienteling Practice
One-to-one retail relationship management — the private-client WhatsApp thread, remembered anniversaries, first calls on new goods. The oldest luxury skill, now run on software.
Omnichannel Concept
The customer researches at midnight, tries on Saturday, buys by DM on Tuesday. Winning retailers price, stock and staff as one continuous counter across all of it.
Digital product passport Regulation
An EU-led ID standard giving each piece a scannable record of origin, materials and custody. Coming for jewelry the way nutrition labels came for food.

On the record

What this desk filed in the last eight weeks, dated and sourced.

  1. This week, August 20 to September 23

    Natural Diamond Council renames itself The Diamond Collective

    The Natural Diamond Council renamed itself The Diamond Collective on 22 September. It changed its logo and palette the same morning, dropping the word natural from its own name.

    Source: JCK, 22 September 2026; the body's own site; RAPI 1.00ct series, 22 September 2026

  2. This week, August 20 to September 23

    Rings.com puts the lab-grown 1-carat median back at $571

    Rings.com's September index puts the one-carat lab-grown market median at $571 a carat. It is the first one-carat median the index has published since June.

    Source: Rings.com Lab-Grown Diamond Price Index, September 2026 edition, published 18 September 2026

  3. This week, August 20 to September 23

    Stuller puts jewellery stores at 26% of doors, 48% of dollars

    Harold Dupuy of Stuller told the American Gem Society's Conclave in Orlando that jewellery-only stores are 26% of national doors and about 48% of the dollars. No period or denominator was published.

    Source: Rapaport, 17 September 2026

  4. This week, August 20 to September 23

    US luxury store openings fall 46% to 123,334 square feet

    JLL counted 123,334 square feet of US luxury store openings in the first half of 2026, against 227,000 a year earlier. Jewellery and watch retailers took 43.5% of the sub-2,500 square foot band.

    Source: JLL leasing report of 8 September 2026, reported by JCK, 16 September 2026

  5. This week, August 20 to September 23

    Macy's raises full-year sales guidance by $125m at midpoint

    Macy's lifted full-year net sales guidance by $125m at the midpoint and its comparable sales range to 1.0% to 1.5%, from 0.5% to 1.2% in June.

    Source: Macy's Q2 2026 results, Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K, 10 September 2026
