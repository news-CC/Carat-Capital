The working vocabulary

The trade’s terms, plainly told. More in the Field Guide.

Lab-grown (LGD) Term
Diamonds grown in weeks by HPHT or CVD — chemically identical to mined, economically a different universe. Their price curve is a technology curve, which is the whole story.
Clienteling Practice
One-to-one retail relationship management — the private-client WhatsApp thread, remembered anniversaries, first calls on new goods. The oldest luxury skill, now run on software.
Omnichannel Concept
The customer researches at midnight, tries on Saturday, buys by DM on Tuesday. Winning retailers price, stock and staff as one continuous counter across all of it.
Digital product passport Regulation
An EU-led ID standard giving each piece a scannable record of origin, materials and custody. Coming for jewelry the way nutrition labels came for food.

  3. This week, August 20 to September 23

    A company's 'broadly flat' margin was printed as a fact

    Clarified 6 November 2026: pandora-reports-6-percent-q3-organic-growth.html (No. 104, 5 November 2026) carried the lead-in "Underlying margin flat." Pandora says its underlying EBIT margin was "broadly flat"; taking its 3.7-point one-off from the 15.9% margin leaves about 12.2%, against 14.0% a year earlier, and the release does not reconcile the two. The lead-in now attributes the claim and the arithmetic is printed beside it. No printed figure changed. Raised by Carat Capital's Standards desk on 5 November 2026.

    Source: Carat Capital, a-pandora-reports-6-percent-q3-organic-growth.html; Pandora A/S, Q3 2026 company announcement
