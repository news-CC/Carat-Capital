Bulgari Pink diamond sells for CHF 12,086,000 at Christie's Geneva

The 24.00-carat Fancy Intense Pink ring, unseen for more than seven decades according to Christie's, was the last lot of the 10 November sale. The house gave the price as US$14,809,761.

By Carat Capital

13 November 2026

What changed

The last lot sold. Christie's sold the 24.00-carat Bulgari Pink for CHF 12,086,000 at its Geneva Magnificent Jewels sale on 10 November.

What it means

  • If you hold pink diamonds: compare this price with others only on the same basis. The release does not say whether it includes the premium.
  • If you consign: Christie's says the stone was fresh to the market, held by one family since the 1950s.
  • What this is not: a sale total. Christie's says its full Geneva results follow.

The sale's final lot.

Sold for CHF 12,086,000. The emerald-cut ring by Bulgari sold for US$14,809,761, Christie's says. It was the final lot of the Magnificent Jewels sale.

Basis not stated. The release does not say whether the price includes the buyer's premium. Christie's says the full Geneva Luxury Week results follow later.

A family stone since the 1950s.

Bought in India. The consignor's great-grandparents acquired the stone in India in the 1950s, and Bulgari set it in Rome, Christie's says.

Type IIa. Christie's classes the diamond as Type IIa, a category it puts at fewer than 2% of gem-quality diamonds.

Estimated at CHF 10m-15m. Christie's estimated the ring at CHF 10 million to CHF 15 million when it announced the sale on 9 October.

What would change this call

If Christie's results show CHF 12,086,000 is a premium-inclusive price, the hammer was lower, and the ring's place against its CHF 10 million low estimate would need restating.

Hammer and premium

The hammer price is the winning bid. Houses add a buyer's premium on top. The release gives one price without naming its basis, and the 9 October estimate did not name one either.
