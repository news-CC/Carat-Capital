Sotheby's sells HK$267.5m in Hong Kong as rubies take top three

A Mauboussin ruby necklace led at HK$16 million, 1.45 times its high estimate. Cartier tutti frutti earrings made HK$7.9 million on an HK$850,000 high estimate, and over 180 lots were offered.

By The Auctions Desk18 September 2026Brief 25 sec · Full read 1 min
What changed

Rubies took the top. Sotheby's Hong Kong High Jewelry sale made HK$267.5 million, or $34.1 million. Ruby lots took the three highest prices, led by a Mauboussin necklace at HK$16 million.

What it means · The Desk’s View

If you bid on Burmese ruby: the top three lots were ruby. Two cleared HK$12.8 million each.

If you consign coloured stones: small estimates moved furthest. A Cartier pair made 9.3 times its high estimate.

What this is not: a diamond market reading. No diamond-led lot reached the top ten.

IRubies took the three highest prices

Mauboussin led. A Mauboussin necklace of tumbled rubies and ruby beads made HK$16 million, or $2 million, against a HK$11 million high estimate.

Two at 12.8. A pair of Burmese ruby earrings and a 5.06-carat Burmese ruby ring each made HK$12.8 million.

IISmall estimates moved furthest

Cartier multiplied. Cartier tutti frutti earrings carrying an HK$850,000 high estimate made HK$7.9 million, 9.3 times that figure.

Van Cleef followed. Van Cleef & Arpels mystery-set ruby earrings made HK$5.1 million on a HK$1.4 million high estimate, 3.6 times it.

IIIColoured stones filled the top ten

No diamond led. Every one of the ten highest prices went to a lot led by a coloured stone, out of more than 180 offered. A 7.52-carat Paraiba tourmaline ring made HK$10.5 million.

The trailed pair cleared low. The earrings led in the 6 September preview made HK$12.8 million, 64% of their HK$20 million high estimate and 36% below it.

The depthMethod, sources, corrections · open what you need
01What would change this call+

A second September sale at these levels. One session in one city is a single reading, and a house can assemble a ruby-heavy catalogue in a market that is not paying up for ruby anywhere else.

02How a price-over-estimate multiple reads+

A high estimate is the house's public ceiling on what it expects, set by its specialists before the room bids. Dividing the hammer-inclusive sale price by that ceiling gives a single number: above 1 the room outbid the house, below 1 it did not. The multiple is most informative at the small end, where a modest band leaves room for a result many times it, and least informative at the top, where a wide band absorbs almost any outcome. A ratio of 9.29 on an HK$850,000 band and a ratio of 0.64 on an HK$20 million band can sit in the same sale without contradicting each other.

03Method · the desk’s arithmetic+

Rapaport's report of 17 September 2026 is the source for the total, the lot descriptions, the estimates and the prices. Its own attribution for the top lot is the Sotheby's website.
