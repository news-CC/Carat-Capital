Chow Sang Sang gold reads 1,200 yuan a gram, 14.8% below 25 August

Chow Tai Fook quoted 1,202 yuan and Lao Miao 1,196 on 24 October, after cuts of 6 to 12 yuan on the day. Shenzhen's Shuibei market drew weekend crowds, Qianjiang Evening News reports.

By Carat Capital

25 October 2026

What changed

Another step down. Chow Sang Sang's pure-gold counter read 1,200 yuan a gram on 24 October, down 6 yuan on the day. Its 25 August level was 1,408.

What it means

  • If you buy gold jewellery in China: The four big brand counters sit within 10 yuan of each other, near 1,200.
  • If you price against a counter: These are retail boards carrying workmanship and margin, not spot metal.
  • What this is not: A spot price. The tape's gold bid, Friday's close, is $3,994.90 an ounce.

About 100 yuan in a month

Near 1,300 before. Brand counters stood near 1,300 yuan a gram on 24 September, the report says. A month later they are about 100 yuan lower.

Cuts of 6 to 12. Lao Miao cut 12 yuan on 24 October. Chow Sang Sang, Chow Tai Fook and Lao Feng Xiang each cut 6.

Shuibei filled up

Weekend crowds. Shenzhen's Shuibei jewellery market drew crowds on Saturday 24 October, the report says. Buyers posted online that it was busier than a vegetable market.

Counters lag spot. Brand boards price finished jewellery and reset on each brand's own schedule. A move in the metal reaches them later.

What to watch

  • Monday 26 OctoberThe first spot gold session since Friday's close, which the brand boards follow with a lag.
  • DailyThe brand boards. A return above 1,250, the early-October level, would undo the month's fall.

What would change this call

Brand boards back above 1,250 yuan within the week. Then 24 October was a dip, not a new level.

How a brand board works

Chinese jewellery brands post a daily price per gram for pure-gold jewellery. It includes workmanship and margin over the metal, so it runs above the bullion price. Each brand sets its own board.
