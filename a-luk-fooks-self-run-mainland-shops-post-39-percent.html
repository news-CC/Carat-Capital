Luk Fook's self-run Mainland shops post +39% for 1–7 October

Hong Kong and Macao same-store sales rose 15% that week, and licensed Mainland shops were flat. Chow Tai Fook's filing shows Macao up 47.3% in July to September, against Hong Kong's 24.0%.

By Carat Capital

24 October 2026

What changed

October opened hotter. Luk Fook's self-operated Mainland shops posted same-store sales up 39% for 1 to 7 October, against 3% for July to September. Hong Kong and Macao rose 15%.

What it means

  • If you hold Luk Fook: Weigh one week against a quarter in which group same-store sales rose 12%.
  • If you sell gold in Hong Kong: Both chains report Macao outrunning Hong Kong in the quarter.
  • What this is not: A Mainland recovery. Licensed shops, about 90% of Luk Fook's Mainland count, were flat.

Source: Luk Fook HKEX announcement, 'Retail sales performance for the second quarter... and the first week of October 2026', dated 22 October 2026. Chow Tai Fook HKEX operational data, 22 October 2026. Same-store sales, year on year, self-operated shops only.

A wider gap pulls buyers

Luk Fook names tax. Luk Fook says the Mainland's gold value-added tax policy widened the price gap with Hong Kong and Macao. It says that is drawing more gold buyers there.

Macao ran fastest. Chow Tai Fook's filing splits its quarter: same-store sales rose 24.0% in Hong Kong and 47.3% in Macao. Luk Fook's Macao also outran Hong Kong.

The Mainland splits in two

Own shops jumped. Luk Fook's self-operated Mainland shops rose 39% in the week. Its licensed shops, about 90% of its Mainland count, were flat against a high base.

The quarter was slower. For July to September, self-operated Mainland same-store sales rose 3% and licensed shops 1%. Neither company gives a reason for the first week beyond the price gap.

Chow Tai Fook reports in November

Interims due. Chow Tai Fook expects to publish its interim results by the end of November. Its profit alert names a gold-loan revaluation gain first.

What to watch

  • End of NovemberChow Tai Fook's interim results for the six months to 30 September.
  • Not datedLuk Fook's next trading update, which would show whether the first week held.

What would change this call

A later Luk Fook update showing October self-operated Mainland growth back near the quarter's 3%. Then the first week was a one-week spike.

How same-store sales are counted

Luk Fook compares sales at the same self-operated shop open for full days in both periods. Licensed shops and e-commerce are excluded, so its licensed-shop figure is reported separately. Chow Tai Fook also counts self-operated stores only.
