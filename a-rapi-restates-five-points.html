RAPI restates all five daily points read a day earlier

The one-carat series read on 21 September and again on 22 September differs at every overlapping date, by between 12 and 27 cents. The level is barely changed; the dating of a quote is not.

By The Diamond Desk22 September 2026Brief 30 sec · Full read 1 min
What changed

The series moved. Every one of the five daily points published between 16 and 20 September has moved. The series read on 22 September differs from the same page read on 21 September.

What it means · The Desk’s View

If you quote off RAPI: Date the read, not only the point. Two people quoting the same day can hold different numbers.

If you settle contracts on it: The moves are under three-tenths of a dollar, immaterial at one carat.

What this is not: An error. A revised series is ordinary; an undated quote is the risk.

IEvery point moved

Five for five. Each of the five points dated 16 to 20 September reads differently on the two mornings. Three fell by about a quarter of a dollar; two rose by twelve cents.

The shifts cluster. The moves take two values, not five: minus 26 or 27 cents, or plus 12 cents. Random transcription noise would not group that tightly.

IIThe level barely changed

Small in dollars. The largest single restatement is 27 cents against a level near $3,916, or 0.007%. The division is 0.27 over 3,916.46.

A new point arrived. The series also added a point for 21 September at $3,918.53, above every reading in the five days before it.

IIIA quote now needs two dates

Two dates, one number. A RAPI figure needs the date it was read as well as the date of the point. Without both, two correct quotes of the same point can disagree.

01What would change this call+

A stable read. If tomorrow's fetch returns these six points unchanged, what is reported here is a one-off revision rather than a standing property of the series.

02How RAPI is put together+

RAPI averages the asking prices of the ten per cent lowest-priced stones listed on RapNet within a narrow band, D to H colour and IF to VS2 clarity, GIA graded. It is a floor for a top specification rather than a market mean. Until 7 September the page published a monthly series; since then it has published a trailing thirty-day daily series per size. A daily average computed off a moving pool of listings can be recomputed as listings are added, withdrawn or regraded, and that is the ordinary mechanism by which a published point can change after the fact. Rapaport publishes no restatement policy on the page, and this desk is not claiming to know which mechanism applied here.

03Method · the desk’s arithmetic+

The 22 September column was read from the index page's own embedded JSON series, parsed out of the raw bytes of a 200 response whose last-modified header read 22 September 2026 09:15:54 GMT, rather than from a rendered table or a summariser. No third party is credited with this comparison, because none has published one and none was consulted for it. The 16 September point has a third recorded reading, $3,912.13, which is what this paper's tape filed on 17 September and printed on 21 September; it is named here for completeness and is not used in the table, which compares only the two full-series reads. Every difference in the Move column is a subtraction of the two printed cells.
