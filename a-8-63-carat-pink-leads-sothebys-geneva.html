8.63-carat pink leads Sotheby's Geneva with CHF 3.3m-5m estimate

JCK reports the unmounted fancy intense pink heads Sotheby's High Jewelry sale at the Mandarin Oriental on 11 November. A Graff ring with a 2.88-carat fancy deep blue carries an estimate of CHF 1.7 million to 2.5 million.

By Carat Capital

30 October 2026

What changed

Sotheby's priced a pink. JCK reports Sotheby's has estimated an unmounted 8.63-carat fancy intense pink diamond at CHF 3.3 million to CHF 5 million. It leads the house's High Jewelry sale in Geneva.

What it means

  • If you bid on coloured diamonds: The high estimate is about CHF 579,000 a carat, under the CHF 625,000 Christie's put on its 24-carat pink.
  • If you consign before Geneva: Four coloured diamonds here carry low estimates of CHF 850,000 or more.
  • What this is not: A price. These are estimates, relayed by JCK, for a sale on 11 November.

Four coloured diamonds lead.

Blue matches pink. A Graff ring set with a 2.88-carat pear-shaped fancy deep blue carries CHF 1.7 million to 2.5 million. An unmounted 8.11-carat fancy pink, graded VVS1, carries the same range.

The yellow is fourth. An 18.03-carat fancy vivid yellow is estimated at CHF 850,000 to CHF 1.6 million, JCK reports.

Ceylon sapphires listed as unheated.

Ruby from CHF 850,000. A ring with a 5.027-carat ruby described as pigeon blood carries CHF 850,000 to CHF 1.3 million. JCK cites no laboratory report for the description.

Heat treatment is named. Sotheby's lists several large Ceylon sapphires as unheated, JCK reports. The largest named is a 69.44-carat stone in a Harry Winston ring, at CHF 580,000 to CHF 750,000.

Provenance carries older pieces.

Rubinstein, then Wrightsman. An 1883 convertible necklace attributed to Boucheron, centred on a heart-shaped sapphire, belonged to Helena Rubinstein and then Jayne Wrightsman. It carries CHF 330,000 to CHF 650,000.

Bulgari's Burmese bib. A circa 1970 Bulgari necklace of 35 Burmese sapphires hung from a diamond bib carries CHF 130,000 to CHF 200,000.

What to watch

  • 10 November 2026Christie's Magnificent Jewels, Geneva, with its 24-carat pink estimated at CHF 10 million to 15 million. The test: both pinks priced a carat.
  • 11 November 2026Sotheby's High Jewelry, Mandarin Oriental, Geneva. The test: the 8.63-carat pink against its CHF 3.3 million low estimate.

What would change this call

An 8.63-carat pink that fails to reach its CHF 3.3 million low estimate, a day after Christie's offers its 24-carat pink, would mark Geneva's November week as soft for coloured diamonds.

What an estimate is

An estimate is the range the house expects bidding to reach, agreed with the consignor. It is not a price. JCK's page does not say whether these figures include buyer's premium.
