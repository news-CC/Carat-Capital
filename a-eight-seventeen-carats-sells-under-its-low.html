Christie's 8.17-carat D/IF ring sells for $243,200, below estimate

A Graff 7.85-carat D VS1 ring led Jewels Online at $256,000, inside its range. The New York online sale closed 15 October with $9.3m in total, Rapaport reports.

By Carat Capital

19 October 2026

What changed

The headline lot missed its range. The 8.17-carat ring, offered without reserve, sold for $243,200, under its $250,000 low estimate. A Graff ring took the top price.

What it means

  • If you price large D rounds: With no reserve, bidders set $243,200 on a D flawless 8-carat round. A Van Cleef D ring also missed its low.
  • If you consign signed coloured pieces: Two Bulgari lots made 2.56 and 3.66 times their highs.
  • What this is not: A fall in D prices. Two lots is a small sample.

Two D stones missed their lows

No reserve, no floor. The 8.17-carat round sold 2.7% below its $250,000 low estimate. With no reserve, the bidding alone set the price.

A second D missed. A Van Cleef & Arpels ring with a 5.22-carat D stone made $140,800, under its $150,000 low.

The top lot held. Graff's 7.85-carat D VS1 ring sold for $256,000, inside its range. The sale closed 15 October with $9.3 million.

Signed coloured pieces ran ahead

Bulgari led. A Bulgari peridot and sapphire necklace made $153,600, 2.56 times its $60,000 high estimate.

Earrings went further. Bulgari sapphire and emerald earrings sold for $128,000, against a $35,000 high.

A Webb pairing too. A 9.04-carat I VS1 ring sold with a David Webb ring jacket for $166,400, against a $100,000 high.

What to watch

  • DecemberChristie's Magnificent Jewels and Jewels Online sales, which JCK says will offer more from the Joel Arem collection. A second reading on large D stones.
  • 25–26 NovemberChristie's Hong Kong sales, the house's next large test of top-end demand, in watches.
  • Q4 2026Whether other no-reserve D lots clear under their lows, or this stays one result.

What would change this call

Large D rounds clearing above their high estimates at the December sales. Then this result reads as one online room on one day, not a price signal.

How an estimate range works

An auction house sets a low and a high estimate from comparable sales. A reserve, usually at or below the low, is the price under which the lot does not sell. A lot without reserve sells to the top bidder at any price, so a result under the low is a market reading, not a failed sale.
