Watches

Sotheby's expects $9m-plus for 30 watches, nearly all first-owner

A platinum Patek 5374/300P-001 leads at $2.5 million to $5 million in New York on 7 December. Most of the rest are Tiffany-retailed Pateks, several double-signed and new to auction.

By Carat Capital

21 October 2026

What changed

Provenance is the pitch. Sotheby's expects The Original Owner Collection, 30 watches nearly all kept by their first buyers, to make more than $9 million in New York on 7 December.

What it means

  • If you consign modern Patek: Sotheby's is marketing first-owner provenance and Tiffany signatures, not age alone.
  • If you bid in New York in December: Phillips sells on 5 and 6 December, two days earlier. Both houses lead with Patek.
  • What this is not: A result. Estimates exclude the buyer's premium, and nothing sells before 7 December.

Two Pateks make auction debuts

A jewelled grand complication. The platinum Patek 5374/300P-001, a minute repeater perpetual calendar set with baguette diamonds and sapphires, is estimated at $2.5 million to $5 million. Patek unveiled the reference in 2022.

A Rainbow Aquanaut follows. The 5260/355R Aquanaut Luce Rainbow minute repeater, circa 2024, carries $1.4 million to $2.8 million. Sotheby's says both are making their auction debuts.

A third repeater too. A pink gold 5208R-001, which pairs a minute repeater with a chronograph and an instantaneous perpetual calendar, is estimated at $900,000 to $1.8 million.

Tiffany signatures fill the rest

Most came through Tiffany. The remaining watches are mainly Patek references retailed by Tiffany & Co., which Sotheby's calls Patek's first US retail partner since 1851. Several are double-signed and new to auction.

The blue Nautilus. The 5711/1A-018, a 2022 edition of 170 with a Tiffany-blue dial, is estimated at $1 million to $1.6 million.

Several firsts at auction. Sotheby's calls its Tiffany-signed Cubitus 5821/1AR-001 and Aquanaut Travel Time 5164G-001 the first of each offered at auction. Both carry $150,000 to $300,000.

Phillips sells two days earlier

A crowded week. Phillips holds its New York Watch Auction on 5 and 6 December, led by a pink gold Patek 2499 estimated above $3 million.

What to watch

  • 5–6 DecemberPhillips' New York sale, two days before, and how far its Patek lots clear estimate.
  • 7 DecemberSotheby's Important Watches sale in New York, where the collection is offered.

What would change this call

First-owner Tiffany pieces selling near their low estimates. Then provenance adds little to a modern Patek's price.

How an auction estimate is quoted

Sotheby's says its estimates exclude the buyer's premium, while prices achieved include hammer price, buyer's premium and overhead premium. A sale-day result is therefore higher than a hammer price set against the same estimate.
