$4,000 is the floor — tested three times, held three times

Gold stabbed below $4,000 on war nerves and snapped back within a day. Manufacturers have stopped waiting for a retreat: product architecture is being redesigned around a $4,000-plus planning price.

B-02

The lightweighting revolution

Hollow forms, electroforming, 9k and 10k revivals, silver-gilt hybrids — the craft of making less metal look like more is the decade's quiet growth industry. Vicenza and Shenzhen lead.

Manufacturing · Vicenza

Recycling becomes a supply line

At these prices, the scrap drawer is a mine. Old-gold buybacks are now a strategic sourcing channel for refiners and brands alike — with its own pricing, logistics and fraud problems.

Talk like the trade — the working vocabulary

The trade's terms, plainly told
HallmarkTerm

The assay office's stamped guarantee of fineness — 750 for 18k, 916 for 22k. The oldest consumer-protection system in the world, and the inspiration for this paper's own mark.

Making chargePractice

The labor and design fee added over the metal's melt value — the jeweler's actual margin. When gold spikes, making charges get squeezed first; watch them to see who holds pricing power.

Fineness ladderConcept

24k is pure; 22k, 18k, 14k, 10k and 9k trade purity for durability and price. Bull markets in bullion push whole countries down a rung — India's 18k boom is this cycle's signature.

Melt valueMetric

What a piece is worth as raw metal, ignoring craft entirely. The gap between melt and retail is where brand, design and trust live — and it's the number every buyback desk starts from.

Gold & Metals This week - August 19 to September 9

Pudong Development Bank ends retail Shanghai Gold Exchange trading after 25 September

Shanghai Pudong Development Bank will discontinue its agency service for Shanghai Gold Exchange personal precious-metals business at a time of its choosing after 25 September 2026, closing mobile banking, online banking and branch counter channels, across 9 contract codes from Au99.99 to Ag(T+D). ICBC and China Construction Bank closed the same business on 24 July 2026; Postal Savings Bank, Ping An Bank and Guangfa Bank filed earlier suspensions in the first half of 2026, making 6 banks out of the channel this year. Bank staff told the 21st Century Business Herald that the exchange had decided to shut individual-client trading altogether, citing market instability and policy requirements - attributed to those staff, not to the exchange.

Source — Jiemian News, 8 September 2026; 21st Century Business Herald, 27 June 2026
Gold & Metals This week - August 19 to September 9

Palladium sheds $36.00 to $1,335.00 against the 8 September mark

On Kitco's bid, read at 10:04 UTC on 9 September 2026, palladium fell $36.00 to $1,335.00, a fall of 2.6258% and the only move of the four metals past one percent. Gold eased to $4,389.30, platinum rose to $1,841.00 and silver to $66.13. Palladium's bid-ask spread widened to 2.9963% of the bid from 2.9176%, and the gold-silver ratio narrowed to 66.4 ounces from 66.7. Changes are measured against the 8 September marks in this paper's own record, not against Kitco's change field, which runs from Kitco's prior close and prints all four metals higher. No source read named a cause.

Source — Kitco, 9 September 2026; livepriceofgold.com; TradingEconomics
Gold & Metals This week - August 19 to September 9

Platinum adds $6.00 to $1,829.00, the only one of four metals higher

On Kitco's bid, read at 10:04 UTC on 8 September 2026, platinum was the only one of the four metals higher on the board's own session change, at $1,829.00 against the $1,815.00 mark this paper printed on 7 September. Gold & Metals This week - August 19 to September 9

DMCC tokenises a 1,971kg silver bar, its first commodity asset under the VARA framework

Tokinvest, regulated by Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority, began issuing fractional digital interests in the bar on 7 September 2026 as an Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset deployed on BNB Chain. Brink's holds the physical metal and DMCC Tradeflow registers possession. SAM Precious Metals cast the bar in the UAE at 99.9% purity, sized to 1971, the year the United Arab Emirates was founded, and it was unveiled at DMCC's Dubai Precious Metals Conference in November 2025 after Guinness World Records recognised it. At 63,369 troy ounces and Kitco's $65.946 bid, the metal is worth about $4.18m, a figure DMCC does not publish. Regulated secondary trading opens after the initial issuance.

Source — DMCC, 7 September 2026; Kitco, 8 September 2026
