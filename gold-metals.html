Talk like the trade — the working vocabulary

The trade's terms, plainly told
HallmarkTerm

The assay office's stamped guarantee of fineness — 750 for 18k, 916 for 22k. The oldest consumer-protection system in the world, and the inspiration for this paper's own mark.

Making chargePractice

The labor and design fee added over the metal's melt value — the jeweler's actual margin. When gold spikes, making charges get squeezed first; watch them to see who holds pricing power.

Fineness ladderConcept

24k is pure; 22k, 18k, 14k, 10k and 9k trade purity for durability and price. Bull markets in bullion push whole countries down a rung — India's 18k boom is this cycle's signature.

Melt valueMetric

What a piece is worth as raw metal, ignoring craft entirely. The gap between melt and retail is where brand, design and trust live — and it's the number every buyback desk starts from.

Gold & Metals This week — August 17 to September 3

India's mandatory gold hallmarking regime reaches 392 districts across 26 of 36 states and union territories

The Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery and Gold Artefacts (Third Amendment) Order, 2026, issued 3 August 2026 by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution on the advice of the Bureau of Indian Standards under the BIS Act 2016, adds twelve districts and takes the count from 380 to 392. The twelve: Kokrajhar and Lakhimpur (Assam), Panipat (Haryana), Bhind, Khandwa and Vidisha (Madhya Pradesh), Bhandara (Maharashtra), Angul (Odisha), Kotputli-Behror (Rajasthan), Kancheepuram (Tamil Nadu), Udham Singh Nagar (Uttarakhand), Jhargram (West Bengal). The parent order dates from 15 January 2020; the previous amendment was 28 April 2026. This paper did not carry the order when it was reported on 10-11 August and records it here 28 days late, as a live regulation rather than as news. No district-level percentage is printed: published totals for India's district count range from 784 to about 802 depending on source and date, and this desk will not build a percentage on a denominator it cannot pin down.

Source — Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery and Gold Artefacts (Third Amendment) Order, 2026, issued 3 August 2026; The Retail Jeweller India, 11 August 2026; Business Standard, 10 August 2026; Zee Business
