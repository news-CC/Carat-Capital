Talk like the trade — the working vocabulary

The trade's terms, plainly told
HallmarkTerm

The assay office's stamped guarantee of fineness — 750 for 18k, 916 for 22k. The oldest consumer-protection system in the world, and the inspiration for this paper's own mark.

Making chargePractice

The labor and design fee added over the metal's melt value — the jeweler's actual margin. When gold spikes, making charges get squeezed first; watch them to see who holds pricing power.

Fineness ladderConcept

24k is pure; 22k, 18k, 14k, 10k and 9k trade purity for durability and price. Bull markets in bullion push whole countries down a rung — India's 18k boom is this cycle's signature.

Melt valueMetric

What a piece is worth as raw metal, ignoring craft entirely. The gap between melt and retail is where brand, design and trust live — and it's the number every buyback desk starts from.

Gold & Metals This week — August 3

Every precious metal rises for a third session as gold adds $109 an ounce in a day

At 5:58am New York time on 5 August, Kitco's spot page had gold bid at $4,157.80 an ounce, up $81.20 or 1.99% against Tuesday's close; silver $61.35, up $1.94 or 3.27%; platinum $1,747.00, up $19.00 or 1.10%; and palladium $1,355.00, up $26.00 or 1.96%. It was the third consecutive session in which all four rose. Against the $4,048.80 print this paper carried on 4 August, gold added about $109 an ounce in twenty-four hours. The gold to silver ratio tightened to 67.8 from about 69.0 and platinum stood at 42.0% of the gold price; fine gold at $4,157.80 an ounce is $133.68 a gram against $130.17 the previous session. The move followed reports that the United States, Iran and Oman were closing on an interim agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with an announcement targeted for Wednesday; lower oil cut the inflation path and traders trimmed the odds of a September Federal Reserve increase to about 57% from about 67% a day earlier. Intraday prints were materially higher than the mark carried here, at $4,170.90 at 3:08am and $4,174.70 at 2:44am. Trading Economics printed platinum at $1,760.10 and palladium at $1,372.00, spreads of $13.10 and $17.00 against Kitco, which remains this paper's designated source. NAT1 and LGD1 both carried unchanged; the lab-grown reference was not printed because two trackers stood 28% apart, at $709 and $552, on the same week.

Source — Kitco / FXStreet / GoldSilver
Gold & Metals This week — August 3

Indian futures rise with the global bid rather than behind it

October 2026 gold futures on India's Multi Commodity Exchange traded at ₹1,45,359 per 10 grams on 5 August, up ₹1,060 or 0.73%, and September 2026 silver at ₹2,24,109 a kilogram, up ₹2,494 or 1.13%. The same gold contract held above ₹1,43,500 in the previous session. ₹1,45,359 per 10 grams is ₹14,536 a gram of futures-grade metal, a duty-inclusive domestic price that cannot be compared directly with the $133.68 a gram international spot mark. Context: Indian jewellery demand fell 19% in the first quarter and to 75.1 tonnes in the second from 88.8 tonnes, a 15% fall, on World Gold Council figures filed by this paper on 31 July, with the council reporting demand beginning to recover in late June after a month-long lull. Chinese gold jewellery demand fell 28% in the same quarter to 50 tonnes, the weakest since 2004. Third-quarter Indian tonnage will not be published for months; the futures print is a price expectation, not an offtake figure.

Source — NewsX / Business Today / Goodreturns
Gold & Metals This week — August 3

The white metals lead again as gold slips 0.14% ahead of Friday's payrolls

At 5:59am New York time on 4 August, Kitco's spot page had gold bid at $4,048.80 an ounce, down $5.70 or 0.14% against Monday's close; platinum $1,655.00, up $28.00 or 1.72%; palladium $1,266.00, up $22.00 or 1.77%; and silver $58.63, up 56.3 cents or 0.97%. The gold to silver ratio tightened to about 69.0 from 69.8 the previous session and platinum stood at 40.9% of the gold price. Trading Economics, quoting a different print time on the same session, showed platinum up 2.56% and silver up 1.83% while agreeing with Kitco on gold at minus 0.14%; it put gold down about 2.8% over the past month and up 19.8% on the year, silver down 5.3% on the month and up 55.3% on the year, and platinum up 26.8% on the year. The ADP national employment report is due Wednesday and non-farm payrolls Friday, the first full read on American hiring since the Federal Reserve held rates on 29 July with three dissents. Gold at $4,048.80 an ounce is $130.17 a gram of fine metal.

Source — Kitco / Trading Economics
Gold & Metals This week — August 3

Chinese gold jewellery demand falls to its lowest second quarter since 2004

The World Gold Council's Gold Demand Trends for the second quarter of 2026, published 30 July, put mainland Chinese gold jewellery demand at 50.0 tonnes, down 28% from 69.2 tonnes a year earlier and the lowest second-quarter figure since 2004. First-half Chinese demand was 136 tonnes, down 30%, while the value of that demand rose 11% to about $21 billion. India took 75.1 tonnes in the quarter, down 15% and its lowest second quarter since the pandemic, on first-half demand of 141 tonnes worth about $21 billion, up 26% by value. The Middle East fell 19% to 32.2 tonnes and the United States fell 25% to 22.2 tonnes, the sharpest percentage decline among the major markets. Global jewellery fabrication was 278.2 tonnes against 335.3 tonnes a year earlier, which the council described as one of the weakest second quarters in its data series. First-half global jewellery value reached about $86 billion, up 22% from $71 billion.

Source — World Gold Council
