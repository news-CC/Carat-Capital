Talk like the trade — the working vocabulary

The trade's terms, plainly told
HallmarkTerm

The assay office's stamped guarantee of fineness — 750 for 18k, 916 for 22k. The oldest consumer-protection system in the world, and the inspiration for this paper's own mark.

Making chargePractice

The labor and design fee added over the metal's melt value — the jeweler's actual margin. When gold spikes, making charges get squeezed first; watch them to see who holds pricing power.

Fineness ladderConcept

24k is pure; 22k, 18k, 14k, 10k and 9k trade purity for durability and price. Bull markets in bullion push whole countries down a rung — India's 18k boom is this cycle's signature.

Melt valueMetric

What a piece is worth as raw metal, ignoring craft entirely. The gap between melt and retail is where brand, design and trust live — and it's the number every buyback desk starts from.

Gold & Metals This week — August 17

Ten Chinese brand gold counters priced between 1,403 and 1,410 yuan a gram on 25 August, a spread of 0.499%

On 25 August, ten Chinese jewellery brands posted gold counter prices inside seven yuan a gram. Chow Tai Fook, Chow Tai Seng, Chow Hong Ji and Tse Sui Luen read 1,410 yuan; Chow Sang Sang and Luk Fook 1,408; Lao Miao 1,407; Lao Feng Xiang 1,406; Zhou Liu Fu 1,405; China Gold 1,403, the lowest. The spread is 7 yuan, or 0.499% of the low, this desk's arithmetic. On 24 August, Chow Tai Fook read 1,393, Chow Sang Sang 1,390 and Lao Miao 1,393, so in one session Chow Sang Sang added 18 yuan or 1.295%, Chow Tai Fook 17 yuan or 1.220%, and Lao Miao 14 yuan or 1.005% - a daily move two to three times the distance across the whole field. These are retail counter prices for finished jewellery, each carrying its own workmanship charge above the metal, not spot quotations, and the per-gram labour charge is not disclosed on these boards. A 26 August pullback reported for four of these brands reached this desk single-source with a back-calculated day-over-day change; it could not be confirmed against a second independent source and is therefore not printed, and no 25-to-26 August change is stated.
