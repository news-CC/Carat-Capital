Newmont reports $1,454 an ounce all-in cost on $4,317 gold

Its third-quarter all-in sustaining cost fell from $1,621 in the second quarter but rose from $1,303 a year earlier. Newmont improved its full-year cost guidance to $1,520 an ounce.

By Carat Capital

23 October 2026

What changed

Costs fell on the quarter. Newmont's all-in sustaining cost fell 10% to $1,454 an ounce in the third quarter. Its realised gold price slipped to $4,317 from $4,414 in the second quarter.

What it means

  • If you model miners against spot: Spot gold sat at $4,020.90 at 05:01 EDT, 6.9% under Newmont's third-quarter realised price.
  • If you track cost inflation: All-in cost is 11.6% above a year earlier, on 8.5% fewer ounces.
  • What this is not: A profit margin. All-in sustaining cost leaves out growth capital and tax.

Cadia's recovery lowered costs

Cadia drove it. Newmont says the 10% fall came mainly from lower other expense, chiefly second-quarter costs at Cadia. Higher sustaining capital partly offset it.

Guidance improved. It improved full-year guidance to $925 an ounce for costs applicable to sales and $1,520 for all-in sustaining cost.

The gold price slipped

Lower than Q2. The $4,317 realised price is $97 below the second quarter, Newmont says. It is below the nine-month average of $4,546.

Spot is lower still. The tape's 05:01 EDT gold bid of $4,020.90 is 6.9% under the quarter's realised price, on Carat Capital's division.

Cash went back out

Returns matched cash. Free cash flow was $1.9 billion. Newmont says it returned $1.9 billion through dividends and buybacks since its last earnings call.

Net income rose. Reported net income was $2.5 billion, against $2.2 billion in the second quarter. Newmont declared a dividend of $0.26 a share.

What to watch

  • Fourth quarterWhether all-in cost holds near $1,454 or moves to the $1,520 full-year guide.
  • 21 DecemberThe $0.26 dividend is paid to holders of record on 25 November.
  • Early 2027Newmont's full-funds decision on the Red Chris block cave.

What would change this call

A fourth quarter with all-in cost back above $1,600. Then the third quarter's fall was Cadia's one-off, not a trend.

How all-in sustaining cost is built

All-in sustaining cost adds sustaining capital, overheads and other sustaining costs to the cost of mining and processing an ounce. By-product treatment credits copper, silver and other metal sales against gold. It excludes growth projects, interest and tax. Newmont reports it as a non-GAAP measure.
