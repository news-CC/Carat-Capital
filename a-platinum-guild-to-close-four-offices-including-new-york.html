Platinum Guild cuts four offices, including New York

PGI USA and PGI South Korea shut on 31 December and Japan on 30 December, JCK reports. Hong Kong ends in the same window, leaving China and India from 2027.

By Carat Capital

26 October 2026

What changed

Two markets kept. Platinum Guild International will close its offices in the US, Japan, South Korea and Hong Kong, JCK reports. Dedicated operations continue in China and India from 2027.

What it means

  • If you sell platinum bridal in the US: PGI USA closes on 31 December; plan 2027 platinum marketing without its New York office.
  • If you work with PGI in Japan or Korea: PGI says it will still support platinum jewelry marketing where offices close.
  • What this is not: A PGI exit from platinum. It calls the move structural.

New York closes in December.

Luker stays through 2026. The 4 closures include New York, where PGI USA president Jenny Luker keeps her role through 2026, JCK reports. PGI has had a US presence since 1992.

Structure, not confidence. PGI says it will keep its education and market-intelligence work. It called the move “a change in operating structure, not a change in confidence in these markets.”

North America had grown.

Demand rose 6%. Platinum jewelry demand in North America rose 6% in 2025, PGI chief executive Tim Schlick said in June. Some brands switched designs from white gold, he said.

The metal is steady. The tape's platinum bid at 05:00 ET on 26 October was $1,544 an ounce, $4 below Friday's close.

What to watch

  • 30 and 31 December 2026Japan closes, then PGI USA and PGI South Korea.
  • 2027PGI's China and India operations. The test is whether PGI still publishes a US demand figure without a US office.
  • June 2027PGI's next annual Platinum Jewellery Business Review, if it keeps June's timing.

What would change this call

A PGI statement naming a US partner or successor to carry its marketing would make this a handover, not a withdrawal from the US trade.

What PGI is

Platinum Guild International was founded as a marketing organisation in 1975, JCK reports. It focused on four jewelry markets: China, Japan, India and the US.
