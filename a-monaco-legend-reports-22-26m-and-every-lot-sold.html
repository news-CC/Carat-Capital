Monaco Legend reports €22.26m and 100% sell-through at its watch sale

Monaco Legend Group says every lot sold at its Exclusive Timepieces auction in Monaco. A Rolex 6062 led at €1,858,000 with premium; the house's April sale made €26.47m across 288 lots.

By Carat Capital

4 November 2026

What changed

Every lot sold. Monaco Legend Group reports total sales of €22.26m at its Exclusive Timepieces auction, with 100% of lots sold. Its April sale sold 98.3%.

What it means

  • If you consign vintage watches: Every lot found a buyer. The house gives no lot count or estimate total, so depth against April cannot be judged.
  • If you price Patek complications: A 2499/100 made €1,424,000 and a 3974 €1,144,000, both with premium.
  • What this is not: A like-for-like fall. The total is 15.9% below April's, on an unstated lot count.

A Rolex led the weekend.

Goldberger provenance led. Lot 50, a steel Rolex 6062 Triple Calendar Moonphase, made €1,858,000 with premium. It was bought new in 1955 and joined John Goldberger's collection in 2017.

Two Pateks above €1m. A Patek Philippe 2499/100 closed the sale at €1,424,000, and a Patek 3974 made €1,144,000.

April made more on 288 lots.

Spring was larger. The April sale made €26.47m across 288 lots, with 98.3% sold, the house said then.

Totals only compare. November's total is 15.9% below April's. The house gives no lot count or estimate for November, so only the totals can be set side by side.

What would change this call

A November lot count well below April's 288 would make the lower total a smaller sale rather than a weaker one.

How the total is reported

The house publishes its own total and sell-through rate. The lot prices it names include buyer's premium; the release does not say whether the total does.
