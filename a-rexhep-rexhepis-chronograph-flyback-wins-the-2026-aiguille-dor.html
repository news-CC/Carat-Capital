Rexhep Rexhepi's Chronograph Flyback wins the 2026 Aiguille d'Or

The GPHG gave twenty prizes in Geneva on 7 November. Audemars Piguet, Chopard and Ferdinand Berthoud each won two, and Patek Philippe took the Special Jury Prize for its Watch Art Grand Exhibition.

By Carat Capital

8 November 2026

What changed

Rexhepi took the top prize. The Aiguille d'Or Grand Prix, the GPHG's highest award, went to Rexhep Rexhepi for the Chronograph Flyback. The 2026 ceremony, the 26th, was held in Geneva on 7 November.

What it means

  • If you stock the winners: they show at Watches of Switzerland in Knightsbridge, London, from 18 to 23 November.
  • If you track brand awards: Audemars Piguet, Chopard and Ferdinand Berthoud each won two of the twenty prizes.
  • What this is not: a sales figure or a price. The prizes are the judgement of an Academy and a jury.

Rexhepi wins the Aiguille d'Or.

A jury chose. An Academy of more than 1,000 members took part in the selection, and a jury chaired by Wei Koh awarded 20 prizes, the GPHG says.

Three houses win twice.

Two prizes each. Audemars Piguet, Chopard and Ferdinand Berthoud are the 3 double winners. Audemars Piguet won Iconic and Calendar and Astronomy, Chopard won Time Only and Jewellery. Ferdinand Berthoud won Chronometry and Tourbillon.

Patek's prize is cultural. Patek Philippe took the Special Jury Prize for its Watch Art Grand Exhibition, an exhibition rather than a watch.

The winners travel to London.

Geneva, then Knightsbridge. The 84 nominated watches stay at Geneva's Musée d'Art et d'Histoire until 15 November, after showings in Mumbai and Bangkok. The winners go to Watches of Switzerland in Knightsbridge from 18 to 23 November.

What to watch

  • Until 15 November 2026The nominees' exhibition at the Musée d'Art et d'Histoire, Geneva.
  • 18 to 23 November 2026The winners at Watches of Switzerland, Knightsbridge. The test: whether the retailer reports enquiries for the prize models.
  • 8 November 2026Phillips' second session in Geneva. The test: whether prize-winning makers' lots hammer above their high estimates.

The story so far

  1. 18 July 2026Sotheby's watch bidding moved toward independents, Rexhep Rexhepi among them.

What would change this call

Nothing in a prize list moves a price by itself. The call that matters is at auction: if Rexhepi and the double winners' pieces sell inside their estimates over the coming season, the prize is recognition, not demand.

How the GPHG is chosen

An Academy of more than 1,000 members takes part in the selection and a jury of international experts awards the prizes. The GPHG was created in 2001 and has been overseen since 2011 by a foundation recognised as a public-interest organisation, it says.
