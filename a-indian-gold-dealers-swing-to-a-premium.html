Indian gold dealers swing to premiums of up to $7 an ounce

Lower prices drew festive and wedding buyers, Reuters reported on 16 October; dealers had offered discounts of up to $6 the week before. Several large Indian funds suspended bulk gold ETF subscriptions.

By Carat Capital

20 October 2026 Corrected

What changed

Discount became premium. Indian dealers quoted gold at premiums of up to $7 an ounce in the week to 16 October, against discounts of up to $6 a week earlier, Reuters reported.

What it means

  • If you buy bullion in India: A Mumbai dealer told Reuters imports have run below their usual pre-festival level, tightening supply.
  • If you hold Indian gold ETFs: Several large funds suspended bulk subscriptions. Reuters names none.
  • What this is not: Investor buying. CapsGold says investors are still holding back.

Lower prices brought buyers back

The price eased. Domestic gold was quoted near ₹148,200 per 10 grams on the Friday, after a two-week low of ₹145,627 earlier in the week.

Jewellers led demand. Chanda Venkatesh of Hyderabad bullion merchant CapsGold told Reuters that jewellery demand is picking up with the festive and wedding season. Investors are still holding back, Venkatesh said, as prices continue to fall.

A dealer sees tight supply. Imports usually rise before the festivals but have run lower this year, tightening supply, a Mumbai dealer at a private bank told Reuters. Trade ministry data showed September imports of $1.8 billion against $9.6 billion a year earlier.

Funds and neighbours moved too

ETF inflows paused. Several large Indian mutual funds issued notices late on 15 October suspending bulk subscriptions to gold exchange-traded funds. Reuters gave no fund names and no reason.

China stayed premium. Chinese bullion traded at premiums of $4 to $13 an ounce over the global benchmark. MKS PAMP's Bernard Sin said physical buyers were cautious in a volatile range.

Elsewhere, near flat. Singapore premiums ran $0.50 to $1.70, and Hong Kong flat to $1.50.

What to watch

  • Next weekly surveyWhether India's premium holds through the festival weeks or slips back to a discount.
  • Festival and wedding seasonWhether the jewellery demand CapsGold describes holds as the season runs.
  • Next monthly trade dataOctober gold imports, which show whether dealers restocked.

What would change this call

A return to discounts in the next survey. Then the premium was a one-week squeeze in supply, not a turn in demand.

How the Indian premium is quoted

Dealers quote a premium or discount against the official domestic price, which already includes the 15% import levy and 3% sales tax. A premium means buyers pay above landed cost, usually because local stock is short. A discount means dealers are clearing metal that buyers will not take at the official price.
