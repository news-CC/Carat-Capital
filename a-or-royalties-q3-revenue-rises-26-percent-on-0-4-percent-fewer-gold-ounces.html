OR Royalties Q3 revenue rises 26% on 0.4% fewer gold ounces

Revenue reached $90.2 million as the realised gold price averaged $4,304 an ounce, against $3,434. Net earnings were $55.4 million, against a 2025 quarter that included a $54.4 million one-off gain.

By Carat Capital

13 November 2026

What changed

Price, not ounces. OR Royalties' third-quarter revenue rose 26% to $90.2 million while gold equivalent ounces earned fell 0.4%, its results show.

What it means

  • If you buy gold for manufacture: the quarter's realised price averaged $4,304 an ounce. The 05:00 ET tape on 13 November reads $3,899.10.
  • If you follow mining finance: the company kept 96.8% of revenue as cash margin, its own measure.
  • What this is not: a fall in underlying earnings. Adjusted earnings, a company measure, rose 28%.

Same ounces, higher price.

Revenue up 26%. Revenue was $90.2 million in the quarter to 30 September, against $71.6 million a year earlier.

Ounces 0.4% lower. Gold equivalent ounces earned were 20,237 against 20,326. The company converts silver and copper into gold ounces at each period's prices.

Gold up 25%. The realised gold price, on the LBMA PM fix, averaged $4,304 an ounce against $3,434.

Net earnings fell on a one-off.

Net down 33%, one-off. Net earnings were $55.4 million against $82.8 million. The 2025 quarter included a $54.4 million non-cash gain on the deemed disposal of an associate.

Adjusted earnings up 28%. Adjusted earnings, a company measure, rose to $53.9 million from $42.3 million.

2026 guidance reaffirmed.

63,734 ounces so far. OR Royalties earned 63,734 gold equivalent ounces in nine months and expects about the mid-point of its 80,000 to 90,000 guidance.

Malartic is the cornerstone. The company calls a 3-5% net smelter return royalty on Agnico Eagle's Canadian Malartic complex its cornerstone asset.

Dividend of $0.065. The board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.065 a share, payable on 15 January 2027.

What to watch

  • By February 2027Gold Fields expects its investment decision on Windfall, where OR Royalties holds a 2.0% to 3.0% net smelter return royalty. The test: whether construction is approved.
  • Full-year 2026 results, early 2027Ounces earned for the year. The test: whether deliveries land near 85,000, the guidance mid-point.

What would change this call

If the gold price falls back towards last year's $3,434 average, revenue falls with it, because ounces earned are flat.

What a gold equivalent ounce is

OR Royalties is a precious metals royalty and streaming company. It converts the silver and copper it earns into gold ounces at each period's average prices, so one figure covers the portfolio.
