Surat diamond unit shuts before Diwali with ₹10 lakh of pay owed

The owner of Marutinandan Diamonds in Varachha is untraceable, police said on 23 October. The union says he paid ₹4.50 lakh of ₹14.50 lakh in wages, then stopped answering.

By Carat Capital

25 October 2026

What changed

The owner left. Marutinandan Diamonds, a Varachha cutting and polishing unit, has shut and its owner is untraceable, police said on 23 October. On the union's figures, ₹10 lakh of wages is unpaid.

What it means

  • If you run a Surat unit: Workers are taking unpaid wages to the union and the police before the Diwali break.
  • If you buy from Surat: The unit cut and polished both natural and lab-grown diamonds, the newspaper's sources say.
  • What this is not: Evidence of wider closures. This is one unit, and no count of others exists.

Part-paid, then gone

One payment came. The union says owner Kiritbhai Vagadiya paid ₹4.50 lakh in the second week of October and promised the rest on 19 October.

Then silence. He did not come, and his phone was off on 20 October, the union says. Workers found his home locked.

Police take statements first

A complaint filed. The Diamond Workers Union Gujarat asked police on 22 October to register a cheating offence against the owner.

No case yet. Inspector H B Patel says police will record polishers' statements, then file an offence report.

Thirty more may be owed

Twenty came forward. The union's Surat vice-president, Bhavesh Tank, says 20 of 55 polishers contacted it. He says 30 others are also owed two months' pay.

Two months each. One polisher, Monaben Mali, says she is owed ₹60,000 for August and September.

What to watch

  • Not datedA police offence report against the owner, after statements are recorded.
  • 5 to 15 November 2026The Diwali break the union expected in October, when unpaid wages bite hardest.

What would change this call

The owner reappears and pays the balance before Diwali. Then this is a payment dispute, not a closure.

Where the figures come from

Police gave the ₹14.50 lakh total. The part-payment, the headcount and the 30 further workers are the union's. The ₹10 lakh balance is Carat Capital's subtraction. No figure is from the owner, who could not be reached.
