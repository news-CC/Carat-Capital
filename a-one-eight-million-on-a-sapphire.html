Colored Gemstones

HKD 1.8 million tops Poly Auction's Hong Kong jewellery sale

A Van Cleef and Arpels sapphire and diamond necklace led the 6 October High Jewelry and Important Watches sale. Burmese ruby and cabochon sapphire earrings cleared their high estimate by more than twice.

By The Gemstones Desk

9 October 2026

What changed

Coloured stones took the top. A Van Cleef and Arpels sapphire and diamond necklace sold for HKD 1.8 million at Poly Auction's Hong Kong sale on 6 October. It narrowly beat its high estimate.

What it means

  • If you buy Burmese goods: A pair of ruby and sapphire earrings estimated to HKD 350,000 took HKD 756,000. The estimate was wrong, not the demand.
  • If you hold signed jewellery: A Van Cleef ruby suite went below its own low estimate in the same room on the same day. The name is not carrying everything.
  • If you consign jadeite: One triple-hoop necklace clearing inside its range is a thin read. The HKD 25 million lot previewed on 29 September is absent from the top five.
  • What this is not: A sale total. Poly Auction has published no aggregate, lot count or sell-through rate, and five named lots are not a market.

A sapphire necklace led

One point eight. The Van Cleef and Arpels necklace of oval-cut sapphires and circular-cut diamonds took HKD 1.8 million. Rapaport reports that as $235,488, the sale's highest price.

Margin not published. The report says it narrowly beat its high estimate but does not give that estimate, so the margin cannot be stated here.

The earrings cleared widest

Seven fifty-six thousand. Burmese ruby and cabochon sapphire earrings made HKD 756,000 against an estimate of HKD 250,000 to HKD 350,000.

Two point one six. Carat Capital's division gives 2.16 times the high estimate, which is 116% above it.

Two Burmese species. The pair carries 31.80 carats of oval-cut Burmese rubies and 31.33 carats of Burmese cabochon sapphires, with rectangular and circular-cut diamonds.

Not every lot cleared

Below the low. A Van Cleef and Arpels ruby necklace and earring set made HKD 840,000 against an estimate of HKD 860,000 to HKD 1.2 million.

Two inside range. The jadeite triple-hoop necklace made HKD 900,000 and the Gubelin sapphire earrings HKD 456,000, each inside its own published band.

A preview lot is missing

Twenty-five million absent. Carat Capital previewed a 39-bead Burmese jadeite necklace at Poly carrying an HKD 25 million high estimate on 29 September.

The report is silent. It does not appear among the five top lots of the 6 October sale. Whether it was not offered or did not sell is not stated.

What to watch

  • Within weeksWhether Poly Auction publishes a sale total, lot count or sell-through rate for the 6 October sale.
  • Within weeksWhether the 39-bead jadeite necklace previewed on 29 September reappears in a later Poly catalogue.
  • Hong Kong autumn seasonWhether Burmese ruby and sapphire lots keep clearing above estimate at other Hong Kong houses.

What would change this call

A published sale total showing a weak sell-through, which would make five strong named lots the exception rather than the read. A report that names only the top five jewels cannot distinguish a firm sale from a thin one with a good top end.

Why a top-five list is weak evidence

An auction house's own summary of its best lots is selected, not sampled. It tells you the ceiling and nothing about the floor, because unsold lots and lots that cleared below estimate are not the material a house promotes. Three specific limits apply here. There is no sell-through rate, so the proportion of the book that found buyers is unknown. There is no aggregate, so the five named prices cannot be set against the sale's size. And the figures quoted are hammer prices as reported, with buyer's premium on top, so what a buyer paid is higher than any number here. The one piece of genuine information in a list like this is internal contradiction: when a signed suite goes below its low estimate in the same room where an unsigned pair of earrings doubles its high, the useful read is about which category the room wanted, not about whether the sale was good.
