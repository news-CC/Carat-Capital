Fossil year-to-date net sales fall 2.5% over a period one week shorter

Net sales were $705.7m for the 39 weeks to 3 October, against $723.9m for 40 weeks a year earlier, Fossil's 10-Q shows. Europe fell 9.8% while Asia rose 2.6%.

By Carat Capital

15 November 2026

What changed

Year-to-date net sales fell. Fossil's net sales for the 39 weeks to 3 October fell 2.5% to $705.7m, its quarterly filing shows. The prior-year period ran a week longer.

What it means

  • If you sell Fossil brands wholesale: wholesale rose 6.3% over the period while direct-to-consumer sales fell 16.1%.
  • If you hold the stock: operating income for the 2026 period was $40.6m, against a $19.9m loss.
  • What this is not: a like-for-like decline. The 10-Q does not restate 2025 on 39 weeks, so the extra week's weight is unknown.

Europe led the decline.

Europe fell 9.8%. Europe sales were $212.5m against $235.7m, which the filing ties to the geopolitical climate in Europe and the Middle East. Americas net sales were about flat; Asia rose 2.6%, led by India.

Closures, about 220 points. Store rationalisation comprised about 220 basis points of the decline, Fossil says. It has 30 fewer stores, or 14.7%, than at the end of the year-ago quarter.

Wholesale up, direct sales down.

Direct sales fell 16.1%. Direct-to-consumer sales fell 16.1%, which the filing puts down to a smaller store base. Comparable retail sales fell 7.3%, mainly from a return to full-price selling, it says.

Traditional watches rose 2.0%. Traditional watch sales rose 2.0%. Smartwatch net sales fell 57.0% as Fossil exited the category, and jewellery fell 10.9%.

One week fewer.

The calendars differ. Fossil's 2025 fiscal year had 53 weeks, with the extra week in the first quarter, so the 2026 period has 39 weeks against 40.

Q3 matched weeks. The third quarter compared 13 weeks with 13, and net sales rose 0.4%, as reported on 11 November.

What to watch

  • Q4 2026 resultsFossil's 2026 outlook of $980m to $990m in net sales. The test: whether the fourth quarter delivers the $274.3m to $284.3m that range implies.

What would change this call

If Fossil gives a 39-week figure for 2025 and the decline disappears, the fall was the calendar, not demand, and Europe's drop reads smaller.

Fossil's 52- and 53-week year

Fossil's fiscal year periodically runs 53 weeks instead of 52. The year ended 3 January 2026 had 53, with the extra week in the first quarter, the filing says.

Carat Capital's arithmetic: 705.7 - 723.9 = -18.2; 980 - 705.7 = 274.3 and 990 - 705.7 = 284.3 for the outlook's remaining quarter, which is 13 weeks in a 52-week year.
