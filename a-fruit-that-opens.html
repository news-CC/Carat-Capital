The $21,650 lemon: Monica Rich Kosann's fruit that opens

Three pendants in the Enchanted Garden series — a cherry at $10,985, a pear at $14,500 and a lemon at $21,650 — each a hinged locket concealing garnets or diamond ‘seeds’ inside 18-karat gold and pavé.

By The Design Desk23 July 2026Brief 35 sec · Full read 2 min
What changed

The most talked-about lemon in American jewelry costs $21,650 and is available by special order only. It is the top of Monica Rich Kosann's three-piece Enchanted Garden fruit series, pendants that debuted at Couture and carry the designer's signature mechanic: they open.

What it means · The Desk’s View

Whimsy holds its value when the engineering is real, and a locket house doing trompe-l'oeil fruit is playing squarely to its own strength rather than chasing a trend. The lemon's special-order status is the tell: this is couture logic applied to the pendant case, made when wanted, never stocked.

The desk's only quibble is arithmetic; at $21,650, the seeds had better be the sweetest part.

IA lemon, by special order

The lemon wears 0.46 carats of yellow diamonds and 0.48 carats of yellow sapphires over 18-karat yellow gold, with tsavorite leaves and a white-diamond stem, and splits on its hinge to reveal white-diamond seeds.

IIThe cheaper branches

The cherry, at $10,985, is the entry point: 2.65 carats of ruby pavé with diamond accents, opening to a 0.75-carat cabochon garnet, a stone-in-fruit conceit executed with a straight face.

The pear, at $14,500, is the colorist's piece, layering a 3.50-carat yellow sapphire with orange sapphire, peridot and 1.36 carats of tsavorite, and concealing five pear-shaped garnets totaling 1.72 carats.

Kosann, a house built on lockets and the things people hide in them, calls the series an exercise in surprise, "a piece of fine jewelry that's a tad bit cheeky".

IIISculpture is the season

The fruits arrive into a market that has been rewarding exactly this kind of object.

Graff's twelve butterfly jabot pins, Pomellato's mandala capsule and the general brooch-and-objet revival this page has tracked all season point the same direction: at the top of the market, jewelry is drifting from adornment toward miniature sculpture, and houses are competing on mechanics, hinges, tremblant wings, hidden compartments, as much as on carats.
