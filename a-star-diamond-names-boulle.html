Star Diamond names Jean Raymond Boulle II chief from 1 October

He succeeds Lester Kemp, interim chief executive since November 2025, at the company's Fort à la Corne kimberlite diamond project. Rapaport reported the appointment on 15 September.

By The Diamond Desk17 September 2026Brief 20 sec · Full read 1 min
What changed

Star Diamond has a chief. Jean Raymond Boulle II becomes chief executive of Star Diamond Corp on 1 October, succeeding Lester Kemp, interim chief executive since November 2025.

What it means · The Desk’s View

If you hold Star Diamond stock: the interim label comes off after about 11 months. No strategy change is stated.

If you watch Fort à la Corne: the project keeps its operator. Nothing here touches the programme.

What this is not: a financing or a resource update. No figure accompanies the appointment.

The article2 sections · 76 words

IThe interim label comes off

A dated handover. Boulle II takes the chief executive seat on 1 October. Kemp had held it on an interim basis since November 2025.

About 11 months. That is roughly 11 months without a permanent chief executive.

IINothing else was announced

No operational change. Rapaport reported the appointment on 15 September. It carries no change to the Fort à la Corne programme, no financing and no resource figure.

The project is named. Boulle II said he looks forward to developing the Fort à la Corne kimberlite diamond project with the board and shareholders.

01What would change this call+

A programme or financing announcement in the first quarter under the new chief. A leadership change with no figure attached is only worth reporting because of what comes after it.

02What an interim chief executive signals, and for how long+

An interim appointment at a single-asset development company usually means one of three things: the board is searching, the board is waiting on a financing before it can offer a permanent package, or the strategy itself is under review and the permanent hire depends on the outcome. Which of the three applies is rarely stated and is not stated here. What the duration tells a reader is the search cost: about eleven months is long by the standards of a listed company with one project, and it ends without any accompanying change to the programme, which argues for the second or third reading rather than a simple vacancy.

03Method · the desk’s arithmetic+

Rapaport's report of 15 September 2026 was fetched directly. It gives the successor, the predecessor and his interim status since November 2025, the 1 October 2026 effective date and the project name, and states no resource, carat or stake figure; none is printed here. The eleven-month interim span is this desk's own subtraction from November 2025 to October 2026 and is written as approximate because the announcement gives no day in November. Archive test, both periods named: zero hits for Star Diamond and zero for Boulle across the 382-article archive, so this is the company's first appearance in this paper and there is no prior period to compare against. The item appeared on the Bureau's slate on 16 September and again on 17 September without being published in between, so this is a second at-bat for unpublished material rather than a repeat of anything printed.
