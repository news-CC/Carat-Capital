De Beers put rough in front of buyers at the Israel Diamond Exchange in Ramat Gan on 14 and 15 October. Hon reports it as the first such sale in the country in nine years. The Hebrew financial title dated its report 13 October Israel time. It placed the goods on the table from the following day until Thursday. No result has been published. The report does not say how many lots were offered, what they weighed, or what was asked. Neither De Beers nor the exchange has issued a figure since.…
