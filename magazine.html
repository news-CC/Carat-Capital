PBoC adds 740,000 ounces, the most of its 23-month run

China's gold reserve reached 77.47 million ounces at the end of September, about 2,409.59 tonnes. Foreign-exchange reserves fell $38.1 billion in the same month

China's central bank added 740,000 troy ounces of gold in September, its largest single month since it resumed buying in November 2024. Figures published on 7 October put official holdings at 77.47 million ounces at the end of the month, up from 76.73 million at the end of August. In tonnes the source gives 2,409.59 against 2,386.57.
