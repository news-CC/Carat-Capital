Bain puts US holiday sales above $1 trillion, up 4.5%

McKinsey finds 43% of shoppers planning to spend less on jewelry and 18% planning to spend more. Only 10% name jewelry or accessories as a top category.

Bain & Company forecasts United States retail sales for November and December rising 4.5% year on year, taking the season above $1 trillion for the first time. The comparable rise recorded in the 2025 season was 3.5%. More than half of the nominal growth, Bain says, comes from higher inflation rather than from additional goods sold.…
