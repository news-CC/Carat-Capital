$151.60 a carat in Antwerp, on the thinnest rough month in five years

Antwerp imported 1,590,026 carats of rough in July for $241,050,713, the lowest monthly volume in the fifty-five months AWDC publishes. The average paid was $15

The Antwerp World Diamond Centre publishes its rough diamond import series month by month in machine-readable form on its own site, and the July 2026 entry reads 1,590,026.12 carats for $241,050,713. Divided out, that is $151.60 a carat, against $88.63 in June, a rise of 71.05% on this desk's arithmetic. Read alone, the per-carat figure looks like a rough market that has turned hard in a single month. It is not, and the reason is in the other column.…
