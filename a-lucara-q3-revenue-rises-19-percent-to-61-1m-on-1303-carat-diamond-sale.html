Lucara Q3 revenue rises 19% to $61.1m on 1,303-carat diamond sale

Lucara raised its 2026 revenue guidance to $140m-$160m from $100m-$130m, citing two large-stone sales. The average tender price per carat fell 11%, and nine-month revenue was $123.9m against $125.2m.

By Carat Capital

15 November 2026

What changed

Lucara raised its guidance. Lucara now expects 2026 revenue of $140m to $160m, crediting the sale of the Motswedi and a 1,303-carat Type IIa diamond.

What it means

  • If you buy at Lucara's tenders: the average price per carat fell 11% on the year; volume kept revenue level.
  • If you hold Lucara paper: the raise rests on stones that may sell for more than $10m.
  • What this is not: a run-of-mine recovery. Clara's average price fell 26%, and Lucara cites pressure on smaller goods.

Lucara credits one diamond.

Revenue rose 19%. Lucara says Q3 revenue rose to $61.1m from $51.2m, primarily driven by the sale of the 1,303-carat Type IIa diamond.

HB: 79% of revenue. Revenue from HB Trading, which buys under a sales agreement, was $48.5m, against 74% of the total a year earlier. The release does not say which channel sold the large stone.

Q3 tender revenue level. Tender revenue was $11.2m, level with Q3 2025, as an 11% fall in the average price per carat met a higher volume of carats.

Clara's average fell 26%. Clara revenue was $1.4m against $2.0m, on a 26% lower average price. Lucara has given notice to end the Clara agreement; from Q1 2027 those goods go to tender.

Legacy Stones lifted guidance.

Two sales, one range. The new range reflects Legacy Stones sales, including the Motswedi and the 1,303-carat diamond, the release says. Lucara defines Legacy Stones as rough that may sell for more than $10m.

Nine months trail 2025. Revenue for the nine months was $123.9m against $125.2m, 1.0% lower on Carat Capital's division.

Q4 needs $16.1m-$36.1m. On Carat Capital's subtraction, the range leaves $16.1m to $36.1m for the fourth quarter, if guidance and the table measure the same revenue. Q4 2025 was $34.5m.

A longer quarter at Karowe.

Q3 ran 97 days. The quarter ran from 26 June to 30 September after a move to calendar-month reporting; Q2 ran 92 days. Karowe processed 829,822 tonnes of ore.

More carats sold. Lucara sold 113,227 carats in the quarter, 12% more than a year earlier, the release says.

What to watch

  • 16 November 2026, 06:00 PTLucara's results call. The test: whether it names the channel and price for the 1,303-carat stone.
  • Q4 2026 resultsFourth-quarter revenue against the $16.1m to $36.1m the range implies, and the end of open-pit mining.
  • From Q1 2027Clara's goods move to tender. The test: the tender's average price per carat with smaller goods added.

The story so far

  1. 21 August 2026Lucara sold the 2,488-carat Motswedi and published no price.
  2. 12 August 2026Q2: revenue $41.0m, guidance held at $100m to $130m.
  3. 15 July 2026Karowe recovered a 1,305.4-carat diamond on 13 July.

Go deeper

What would change this call

If fourth-quarter revenue comes in under $16.1m, the raised range fails on Lucara's own nine-month table, and the raise was one stone, not a trend.

How Lucara sells Karowe's rough

Three channels: a sales agreement with HB Trading, which pays top-up or top-down amounts when the polished price differs from the agreed value; quarterly tenders; and the Clara platform, which Lucara is leaving.

Method

Carat Capital's arithmetic: 61.1/51.2 = 1.193, the release's 19%; 123.9/125.2 = 0.9896, so 1.0% lower; 140 - 123.9 = 16.1 and 160 - 123.9 = 36.1; 48.5/61.1 = 0.794, the release's 79%. The release's guidance line reads 'Diamond revenue' and the table 'Total Revenue'; the subtraction assumes they match. Lucara does not link the two figures and this article does not either. The release names no buyer or price for the 1,303-carat stone. No second outlet was read; every figure is Lucara's.
