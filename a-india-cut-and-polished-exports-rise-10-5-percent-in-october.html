India's polished exports up 10.50% in October, down 4.50% since April

Cut and polished diamond exports reached US$1.13 billion in October, GJEPC says. The council says Diwali fell in October last year and in November this year, giving October a longer working window.

By Carat Capital

14 November 2026

What changed

Polished turned positive in October. India's cut and polished diamond exports rose 10.50% to US$1.13 billion in October, GJEPC says. From April to October they were still down 4.50%.

What it means

  • If you buy polished from India: Diwali moved to November this year, giving October a longer working window, the council says.
  • If you sell into the US: seven-month polished exports to the USA fell 29.92%, and to the UAE 12.26%, GJEPC says.
  • What this is not: a volume reading. The release gives values, not carats.

Key figures

Cut and polished, April to OctoberUS$6.95bn in dollars; +4.74% in rupees−4.50%

Polished lab-grown exports, October+81.02% in dollarsUS$170.46m

Source: GJEPC press release, 13 November 2026 · October 2026 against October 2025 · US dollar values; the release gives no carat figures

Month up, seven months down.

October: US$1.13 billion. Cut and polished exports rose 10.50% in dollars and 19.26% in rupees in October, GJEPC's release shows.

Seven months: still down. From April to October they fell 4.50% in dollars to US$6.95 billion, while rising 4.74% in rupees. GJEPC says the pace of decline has moderated.

September was negative. In September, cut and polished exports fell 5.4% year on year to US$1.29 billion, GJEPC's figures for that month showed.

Diwali moved the month.

Calendar, per GJEPC. Diwali fell in October last year, when manufacturing centres observed their festive shutdowns. This year it falls in November, which gave October a longer working window, the council says.

Prices firming, per Bhansali. GJEPC chairman Kirit Bhansali says polished prices have shown signs of firming in recent months.

Lab-grown and gold rose faster.

Lab-grown up 81.02%. Polished lab-grown exports rose 81.02% to US$170.46 million in October.

Gold value, not weight. Gold jewellery exports rose 48.20% to US$1.26 billion. Vijaykumar Mangukiya, a GJEPC panel convener, says higher values should not be equated with higher volumes.

What to watch

  • GJEPC's November figures, expected mid-December 2026 on this year's patternThe first month with this year's Diwali inside it. The test: whether polished stays positive against a November 2025 without the festival.

The story so far

  1. 18 October 2026September: cut and polished exports fell 5.4% to $1.29 billion; April to September down 7%.
  2. 20 September 2026August: polished exports fell 4.93% in value while rising 3.23% in carats.

What would change this call

If November falls by more than October rose, the October gain was the calendar, not demand.

Value, not volume

GJEPC reports exports in US dollars and rupees. A higher value can mean more stones, dearer stones or a weaker rupee, and the October release gives no carat count.
