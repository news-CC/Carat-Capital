Zijin takes control of Chifeng Gold; third-quarter profit falls 41.84%

Zijin Gold now holds about 25.85% of the Chinese miner after a new H-share issue at HK$29.82, Chifeng said on 22 October. Nine-month net profit still rose 11.05%.

By Carat Capital

24 October 2026

What changed

Control changes hands. Zijin Mining's unit Zijin Gold became Chifeng Gold's controlling shareholder on 22 October. Chifeng's third-quarter net profit fell 41.84%, which the company puts down to lower gold sales volume.

What it means

  • If you hold Chifeng: The whole board has submitted resignations, and an early board election is planned.
  • If you track Chinese gold supply: Chifeng blames volume, not price, for the quarter, and credits higher gold prices for the nine months.
  • What this is not: A loss. Nine-month net profit rose to RMB 2.285 billion.

Zijin now controls Chifeng

Two steps closed. Zijin Gold took 242 million A-shares from former controller Li Jinyang and a concert party. It then subscribed for 311 million new H-shares at HK$29.82.

A county finance bureau. Shanghang County's finance bureau becomes Chifeng's actual controller, the China Business Journal reports. Zijin Gold and concert parties hold about 25.85%.

The board resigns. All directors of Chifeng's ninth board have submitted resignations, and the board plans an early election.

Volume cut the quarter

Profit fell on volume. Third-quarter net profit attributable to shareholders fell 41.84% to RMB 553 million. Chifeng says lower gold sales volume drove every measure down.

Nine months rose. Over nine months, net profit rose 11.05% to RMB 2.285 billion on revenue up 16.69%. Chifeng credits higher gold prices.

Unaudited so far. Chifeng's third-quarter statements are unaudited, Guandian reports.

What to watch

  • Not datedChifeng's early board election and the names Zijin puts forward.
  • Not datedWhether fourth-quarter gold sales volume recovers under the new controller.

What would change this call

A fourth quarter with gold sales volume back above last year's. Then the third quarter was a dip in timing, not a lower run-rate.

How the deal was built

On 22 March, Li Jinyang and a concert party agreed to sell Zijin Gold 242 million A-shares, then 12.73% of Chifeng, for about RMB 10.006 billion. Chifeng also agreed to issue new H-shares to Zijin Gold. Both legs completed on 22 October.
