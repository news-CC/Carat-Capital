Watches

Audemars Piguet charges sellers up to 20% on its own resale site

The Watch Keepers, run by AP itself, listed 43 watches on 26 October. Buying and selling are open only to verified residents of the European Union and Switzerland.

By Carat Capital

26 October 2026 Corrected

What changed

AP resells its own. Audemars Piguet runs The Watch Keepers, a certified pre-owned site it fully owns and operates. Sellers pay a fixed, tiered commission that starts at 20% and falls to 10%.

What it means

  • If you sell pre-owned AP: On a EUR 40,000 watch the commission is EUR 7,250, or 18.1% of the price.
  • If you buy in the EU or Switzerland: Each watch carries AP's own authentication, a five-point grade and a two-year warranty.
  • What this is not: A global market. Buying and selling are open only to verified EU and Swiss residents.

AP fixes the commission.

Tiered, not negotiable. The seller commission is not negotiable, AP's FAQ says. It covers authentication, servicing, logistics, photography, listing and insurance.

Sellers set prices. Sellers choose a set price or a bidding window, with an optional reserve on a bidding window. An unsold watch can be repriced or retrieved, subject to a service fee.

Checked against AP's archives.

Archive and theft checks. AP watchmakers authenticate each watch against the brand's archives and check it has not been reported stolen. It ships with a certificate of authenticity.

Returns within 14 days. Buyers may return a watch within 14 days of receipt. Delivery covers 29 countries: the 27 EU members, Liechtenstein and Switzerland.

A 2-year warranty. Each sale carries a 2-year international sales warranty from purchase. The site listed 43 watches on 26 October.

What to watch

  • Not datedNew regions. AP's FAQ says it plans to extend buying and selling beyond the EU and Switzerland.
  • Through December 2026The listing count, 43 on 26 October. A rising count would suggest owners consigning to AP over dealers.

Corrections1 note

Clarified, 27 October 2026: a watch line said a rising listing count 'would show' owners consigning to AP over dealers. The Watch Keepers pages say nothing about who sells; a rising count could also be AP's own stock. It now says 'would suggest'. What was right as published: every figure. Raised by Carat Capital's Standards desk on 26 October 2026.

What would change this call

A cut to the 20% tier, or buying opened to the US, would put the site in price competition with dealers. At these rates it sells certainty, not price.

How The Watch Keepers works

Audemars Piguet (Marketing) SA runs the site from Le Brassus. Guests over 16 may browse; Residents over 18 who pass an identity check may buy, sell and bid. Its terms were last updated on 11 June 2026.
