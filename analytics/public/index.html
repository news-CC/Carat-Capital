How the desk counts

Readers
Distinct browsers in a day. Someone who reads on three days counts three times.
Pageviews
Every page opened, reloads included.
Visits
Arrivals from outside the paper: search, social, links, newsletters, bookmarks. Clicks between our own pages are not visits.
Reading time
Average seconds a page was on screen with the reader active.
Read-through
Share of story views that reached the end of the story.

Privacy

No cookies on caratcapital.org and no IP addresses kept. A reader is a daily-salted hash; the salt is deleted after two days, so nobody can be followed from one day to the next.

To stop counting your own reading, open caratcapital.org/?circulation=ignore once in each browser you use.
