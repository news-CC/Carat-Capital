Watches and Wonders adds 9 brands to its 2027 roster

Thirteen new exhibitors are now confirmed for the salon, which runs 5 to 11 April. Porsche Design, Berneron and Fortis are among nine named on 18 September, alongside four announced in June.

By The Watches Desk19 September 2026Brief 25 sec · Full read 1 min
What changed

Nine more brands are in. Watches and Wonders Geneva named nine further exhibitors for 2027 on 18 September, taking the count of new exhibitors to thirteen.

What it means · The Desk’s View

If you book Geneva appointments: nine more stands exist to plan around, and the week runs 5 to 11 April.

If you wholesale independents: Berneron, Alain Silberstein and Singer Reimagined move onto the main floor.

What this is not: a full roster. The organiser published additions, not a list.

INine names in one announcement

The list is published. Akhor, Alain Silberstein, Berneron, Breva Genève, Fortis, Kerbedanz, Porsche Design, Singer Reimagined and Stollenwurm were named on 18 September.

Independents dominate it. Seven of the nine are independent watchmakers; Porsche Design is the one large design house among them.

IIFour were already in

June carried the rest. Breitling, Damiani, Gallet and the revived Universal Genève were announced in June 2026, taking the new count to thirteen.

Two readings disagree. Trade reports put the 2027 field at 75 brands. The organiser's own announcement gives no total, so no roster total is printed.

The depthMethod, sources, corrections · open what you need
01What would change this call+

A published roster total. The organiser has announced additions only, and one official list would settle a count that two trade outlets currently give differently.

02What an exhibitor announcement commits to+

A salon announces exhibitors in tranches, usually from the previous summer, because stand allocation and floor plans are agreed brand by brand. An announced brand has a contract and a stand, not necessarily a launch. The count that matters commercially is stands, not names, and it is rarely published: a single group can hold one large stand for several maisons, so a roster of names and a floor plan are different documents.

03Method · the desk’s arithmetic+

The organiser's own site was read on 19 September 2026 and is the source for the thirteen new brands, the nine names announced on 18 September, the four dated to June 2026 and the 5 to 11 April 2027 dates. Time and Tide's write-up of the same announcement was read as an independent second reading and carries the identical nine names. No total roster figure is printed: Worn & Wound reports 75 brands for 2027 and the organiser's announcement carries no total, and a count this paper cannot settle between two outside sources is disclosed rather than chosen.
