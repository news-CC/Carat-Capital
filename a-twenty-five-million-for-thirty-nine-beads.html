Poly Auction puts a HKD 25m high estimate on jadeite

The 39-bead Burmese jadeite and diamond necklace could reach $3.2m at the 6 October High Jewelry and Important Watches sale in Hong Kong. More than 240 jewels are catalogued.

By Carat Capital

29 September 2026

What changed

Jadeite leads Hong Kong. A Burmese jadeite and diamond necklace carries a HKD 25m high estimate at Poly Auction's 6 October sale. The catalogue runs past 240 lots.

What it means

  • If you buy coloured stones: Jadeite is leading a Hong Kong catalogue ahead of any diamond lot. The sale also offers ruby, sapphire and Paraiba tourmaline.
  • If you value jade: A published high estimate is the nearest public mark for bead quality at this level. The 6 October result will test it.
  • What this is not: A price. This is a pre-sale estimate set by the house, not a realised result.

Thirty-nine beads and diamonds

The lot itself. The necklace comprises 39 Burmese jadeite beads with pear, rose and circular-cut diamonds. The house's HKD 25m top estimate is about $3.2m.

Coloured stones dominate. More than 240 jewels are catalogued, many with ruby, sapphire and Paraiba tourmaline. The sale is titled High Jewelry and Important Watches.

Hong Kong sets the jade mark

Burmese origin matters. Jadeite bead necklaces of Burmese origin sit at the top of the jade market. Bead matching and translucency drive an estimate at this level more than weight does.

The date is fixed. Bidding opens 6 October in Hong Kong. The house published the estimate before the viewing.

What to watch

  • 6 OctoberThe Poly Auction sale itself, and whether the necklace clears HKD 25m or falls inside its range.
  • 6 OctoberThe ruby, sapphire and Paraiba lots in the same catalogue, a broader read on Asian coloured-stone demand.
  • After the saleWhether a realised jadeite result reprices bead necklaces held in Hong Kong inventory.

What would change this call

The result. A high estimate is the house's opinion before bidding and nothing more; a withdrawal, a pass, or a sale far inside the range would each say something different about jade demand than this preview does.

Why a jade estimate is not a carat price

A diamond estimate can be reasoned back to a per-carat figure because the trade prices diamonds that way, by weight, colour and clarity against a published list. Jadeite has no such list. Value in a bead necklace comes from translucency, the evenness and saturation of colour, and above all from matching: a strand of thirty-nine beads that agree with each other in tone and diameter is worth a large multiple of thirty-nine similar beads that do not. Burmese origin carries a premium of its own, and the finest material is described in the trade by terms that are qualitative rather than measured. That is why a house publishes a range for a jade lot and why the range can be wide. It is also why the realised price, when it comes, is a more useful mark than the estimate, and why a single strand selling well can move what dealers ask for comparable material.
