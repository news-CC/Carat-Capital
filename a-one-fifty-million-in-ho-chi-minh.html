Pandora opens $150 million Vietnam plant, capacity up 50%

The Ho Chi Minh City site opened 1 October, Pandora's first outside Thailand and its fourth worldwide, with room for 60 million pieces a year. It will employ 7,000 when fully operational.

By Carat Capital

2 October 2026

What changed

The plant opened. Pandora began crafting in Ho Chi Minh City on 1 October 2026. The USD 150 million site lifts the group's manufacturing capacity by 50% and is its first production plant outside Thailand.

What it means

  • If you buy Pandora wholesale: Capacity changed, not price. No Pandora price list moved with this.
  • If you craft in Thailand: A second country now shares the volume, and the academy moves skills both ways.
  • What this is not: A diamond-growing plant. No stone is grown here; the lab-grown remarks are a separate answer.

The release gives six figures.

Six figures published. Pandora puts the investment at USD 150 million and the site's annual capacity at 60 million pieces. The facility lifts group crafting capacity by 50%.

Land and jobs follow. The release gives 7,000 local jobs when fully operational, on a 7.5-hectare plot in Vietnam-Singapore Industrial Park III. The building holds LEED Gold certification.

First plant outside Thailand.

Thailand keeps the craft. This is Pandora's first production site outside Thailand and its fourth worldwide. The company's crafting academy is to move skills between the two countries.

Power is renewable. The facility runs solely on renewable electricity and uses 100% recycled silver and gold. Pandora already crafts all its jewellery from recycled silver and gold.

Platinum plating follows. The release names the site's role in future platinum-plated jewellery as the company widens its materials. It gives no volume or launch date for that line.

Asia carries the growth case.

Asia grew ten percent. Chief executive Berta de Pablos-Barbier told CNBC the region grew 10% in the second quarter. She said Japan's business has roughly doubled in three years.

China turned. After three years of declines, Pablos-Barbier said China is returning to growth this year. She put lab-grown stones' carbon emissions around 90% below mined ones.

  • Full ramp, date not publishedWhether the 60 million-piece capacity and the 7,000 jobs arrive together; the release dates neither.

What would change this call

A ramp that slips. Every figure above is a capacity figure, not an output figure, and Pandora attaches no date to full operation: 60 million pieces a year and 7,000 jobs are both stated as the fully operational state, not as 2026. If the ramp runs long, the 50% uplift is an option the company holds rather than volume the trade can buy, and the group's reported organic growth of 3% in the second quarter is the better guide to what is actually selling. The second reading that would reverse the desk's view is a Q3 report on 4 November 2026 that carries a Vietnam start-up cost line large enough to move group margin.

What a crafting capacity figure counts

A crafting capacity figure is the number of finished pieces a plant can produce in a year at its designed run rate, not the number it will produce. It is set by the installed bench positions, the shifts the site is designed to run and the throughput of the plating and setting lines, and it is published by the company rather than measured by anyone outside it. That matters for reading the 50% in this release two ways. As a capacity number it is exact arithmetic on Pandora's own installed base: 60 million pieces added to a base the company does not state, which the division in the table puts at about 120 million. As a trading number it says nothing at all, because a plant at designed capacity and a plant at half of it both carry the same capacity figure. The practical consequence for a buyer is that this release changes what Pandora can supply and not what it will price, which is why the key figures above carry no price and the desk's view says the price list did not move.
