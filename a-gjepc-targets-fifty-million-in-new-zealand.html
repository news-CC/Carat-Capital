GJEPC targets $50m in New Zealand as trade pact takes effect

India's gem and jewellery exports to New Zealand were $25.43 million in FY2025-26, the export council says. It said on 21 October that the agreement enters into force that day.

By Carat Capital

22 October 2026

What changed

Duty-free access starts. GJEPC said on 21 October that the India-New Zealand free trade agreement enters into force that day. The council aims to double exports to $50 million within two years.

What it means

  • If you export gold jewellery to New Zealand: GJEPC says duty-free access starts now and plans buyer-seller meets.
  • If you judge the target: It needs about 40% growth a year, below last year's 50%.
  • What this is not: A large market. The whole trade is about $25 million.

Key figures

FY2025-26 exportsto New Zealand$25.43m
FY2024-25 exportsGJEPC, 27 August$16.91m
Yearly growth neededCarat Capital's arithmetic40%

Source: GJEPC release, dated 21 October 2026. FY2024-25 figure from GJEPC's 27 August 2026 release.

The pact is live

Signed in April. India and New Zealand signed the free trade agreement on 27 April 2026. It had not entered into force when the August figures came out.

First shipments named. GJEPC says Malabar Gold & Diamonds and Jewelex in Mumbai, Emerald Jewel in Chennai and Hasmukh Parekh in Kolkata shipped gold jewellery. SRK shipped cut and polished diamonds.

Silver filigree too. Padmesh Industries of Odisha shipped GI-tagged silver filigree jewellery, the council says.

Gold jewellery carries the trade

Gold led last year. In August, GJEPC put gold jewellery at $17.47 million of the FY2025-26 total, up 84%.

The target is modest. Doubling in two years needs about 40% growth a year, on Carat Capital's arithmetic. Exports grew about 50% in FY2025-26 without the agreement.

What to watch

  • Not datedGJEPC's buyer-seller meets in New Zealand, and which exporters take part.
  • MonthlyGJEPC's country figures, the first to show trade under the agreement.
  • Two years onWhether exports reach the $50 million target.

The story so far

  1. 28 August 2026Exports to New Zealand rose 50.56% to $25.46m in FY2025-26, per GJEPC.

What would change this call

A first year under the agreement with growth below last year's 50%. Then duty-free access is not what drove the trade.

How the figures are counted

GJEPC is India's government-backed gem and jewellery export council. It publishes exports by country and product from Indian trade data, on India's April-to-March fiscal year. These are the council's figures on its own trade.

Method

GJEPC release, 'India-New Zealand FTA Enters into Force; Targets US$ 50 Million in Exports in the Next Two Years', dated 'Oct 21, 2026', fetched 22 October 2026, 200, 34,653 bytes. Greps in the release: 'enters into force today' 1, 'US$ 25.43 million' 1, 'US$ 50 million' 2, 'double' 2, 'Malabar' 1, 'SRK' 1, 'Padmesh' 1. GJEPC's 27 August release, reported here on 28 August, gave $25.46m for FY2025-26, $16.91m for FY2024-25 and $17.47m for gold jewellery; the 21 October release does not say why the total moved by $0.03m. Some outside reports gave 20 October as the start date; they were not read, and the date printed is GJEPC's. The release does not give New Zealand's previous duty on jewellery, so no tariff saving is printed.
