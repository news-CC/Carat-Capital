Diamonds

De Beers and Shanghai's bourse meet on natural-diamond value

The Natural Diamond Council and the Diamond Federation of Hong Kong convened the session on 17 September, during Jewellery & Gem World. The World Federation of Diamond Bourses and De Beers sat on the panel; no figures were released.

By The Diamond Desk

21 September 2026

What changed

Five bodies met in Hong Kong. The Natural Diamond Council and the Diamond Federation of Hong Kong convened a forum on rebuilding natural-diamond value. The Shanghai Diamond Exchange, the World Federation of Diamond Bourses and De Beers joined them.

What it means

  • If you sell natural stones: The category's marketing bodies are coordinating the message, not the price. Nothing was announced.
  • If you watch China: The Shanghai exchange named retail partnership as its next step, extending a 2024 agreement.
  • What this is not: A supply or pricing decision. No volume, price or budget figure was released.

Supply is the stated premise

Falling supply framed it. The Natural Diamond Council's chief executive said natural-diamond supply is declining, and that buyers now decide more cautiously and with more information.

The ask is education. She put the category's task as consumer education on natural origin, rarity and long-term value, rather than on marriage and status alone.

China gets its own plan

Three themes, two levers. The council's Greater China managing director described three communication priorities and two collaboration drivers as the 2026 plan for that market.

Retail is the end. The Shanghai Diamond Exchange said its partnership with the council, running since 2024, now reaches from consumer campaigns through to shop counters.

Separating natural from synthetic

Bourses want a line. The World Federation of Diamond Bourses' president argued for traceability from mine to counter, and for a clear separation between natural and synthetic stones.

No number was given. No participant published a volume, a price, a budget or a date, and none is reported here.

What would change this call

A published figure. Everything here is positioning, and a single dated number from the council or the exchange, a campaign budget, a reach count or a China sell-through figure, would move this from a messaging story to a measurable one.

What these five bodies each control

The Natural Diamond Council is a marketing body funded by producers; it buys advertising and writes category messaging, and it does not set prices or supply. The Diamond Federation of Hong Kong is the local trade association and the host. The Shanghai Diamond Exchange is the only legal channel for diamond import into mainland China and therefore sees the volume that a marketing body does not. The World Federation of Diamond Bourses is the umbrella for the world's trading floors and writes the rules those floors trade under. De Beers sells rough to a fixed list of buyers. Only two of the five can move a number, and neither of them published one.
