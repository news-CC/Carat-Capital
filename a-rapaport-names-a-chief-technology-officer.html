Rapaport names Yosi Botzer chief technology officer

The former Perion Network executive takes a company-wide artificial intelligence mandate. It extends across Rapaport's businesses, including the price list the natural diamond trade discounts against.

By Carat Capital

18 October 2026

What changed

The price list gets a technologist. Rapaport has appointed Yosi Botzer, formerly chief technology officer of Perion Network, as its own chief technology officer. The mandate is company-wide and extends artificial intelligence across its businesses, including its price list.

What it means

  • If you price against the list: The company says the initiative extends to the Rapaport Price List itself. It does not say what changes, when, or whether the methodology is affected.
  • If you sell software to the trade: A pricing, news, trading and auction group has hired an ad-tech executive, not a diamond technologist. Ring Builder is named as already shipped.
  • What this is not: A change to any published price. No figure, date or methodology statement accompanies the appointment.

An outsider to diamonds

The background is ad-tech. Botzer has more than 20 years building ad-tech and data platforms. He was chief technology officer of Nasdaq-listed Perion Network.

Five businesses, one strategy. At Perion he led technical strategy across five businesses the company had acquired. Earlier he built large-scale data systems at Datorama, later bought by Salesforce.

The remit is company-wide. He will oversee how Rapaport develops technology and runs its businesses. The products it offers the natural diamond trade are also in scope.

The list is in scope

The company names it. The company says the initiative will extend to the Rapaport Price List. The report calls that list its widely used diamond pricing tool.

One product has shipped. The company has already begun putting artificial intelligence into trade products. Its recently introduced Ring Builder lets retailers create bespoke ring designs while a customer shops.

Five lines of business. Rapaport operates businesses spanning diamond pricing, research, news, online trading and auctions, all of which the mandate covers.

What to watch

  • When publishedAny methodology note attached to the price list. The list is named in scope and nothing about its construction has changed on this news.
  • OngoingFurther product releases after Ring Builder, which is the only shipped example given.
  • OngoingWhether a budget or headcount is disclosed. Neither accompanies the appointment.

What would change this call

The company saying the price list is not in scope. The one claim here that matters to a reader is that the artificial intelligence mandate reaches the Rapaport Price List, and it rests on a single trade report of a company announcement which this desk has not re-fetched from the company's own newsroom. Everything else is biography. If the list is out of scope, this article's only consequential sentence is wrong.

Why the price list is the part worth watching

The Rapaport Price List is a weekly sheet of asking prices by size, colour and clarity, and almost nobody in the natural diamond trade transacts at the number printed on it. What the trade does instead is quote a discount or a premium to it, so a stone is sold at minus twenty-eight back of list rather than at a dollar figure. That convention makes the list an index rather than a price, and it gives it a property most indices do not have: changing how it is built changes the stated terms of transactions already being negotiated against it, without anybody's price having moved. This is why an appointment with no budget and no timetable is still worth printing. The company has named the list as inside the scope of a company-wide technology mandate, and the person taking the mandate spent two decades building data platforms in advertising, an industry where pricing is set by automated auction rather than by a published sheet. None of that says the list will change. It says the group that publishes it has hired someone whose professional instinct is to automate a price, and has said the list is in his remit. The thing to watch is therefore not a product launch but a methodology note, because a change to how an index is computed is the one event here that would reprice goods rather than merely re-describe them.
