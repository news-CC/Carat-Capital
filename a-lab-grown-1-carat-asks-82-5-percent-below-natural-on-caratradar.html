Lab-grown 1-carat average asks 82.5% below natural on CaratRadar

The online average asking prices read $643 and $3,680 on 11 November. At the trade, Lepdo's expected range of lab-grown prices sits 79.9% to 88.3% below IDEX's natural round.

By Carat Capital

11 November 2026

What changed

Average gap is 82.5%. A 1-carat lab-grown diamond asked $643 on CaratRadar's 11 November averages, against $3,680 for natural. Both are online asking averages across specifications.

What it means

  • If you price a bridal counter: quote both stones from one source on one day. Across four bases the gap runs from 79.9% to 88.3%.
  • If you buy at the trade: set Lepdo's expected range of $350 to $600 against IDEX's $2,991 natural average.
  • What this is not: a trend from July. The 73% then was another source's basis.

One source, one day.

Both dated 11 November. CaratRadar averages asking prices across online listings and dates both pages 11 November 2026. Natural averaged $3,680 at one carat; lab-grown $643.

Lab-grown listings outnumber natural. The site counted 1,676,190 lab-grown listings against 939,127 natural.

An average across specifications. The averages cover every shape, colour and clarity listed at one carat. The two pools need not hold the same mix, so 82.5% is a market average, not a like-for-like quote.

The trade gap is similar.

IDEX reads $2,991. IDEX's one-carat round driver, D to K colour and IF to I1 clarity, asked $2,991.21, against $3,018.98 on 26 October.

Lepdo's expected range. Lepdo's wholesale guide expects one-carat CVD lab-grown, D to F and VS, in a range of $350 to $600 a carat in 2026.

Up to 88.3% lower. Set against IDEX's round average, that range sits 79.9% to 88.3% below natural.

One series shows widening.

BriteCo's series widened. BriteCo's appraisal records put the lab-grown discount at 26.6% in 2019 and 72.8% by 2025.

Levels, not a trend. The 82.5% is CaratRadar's asking basis, not BriteCo's appraisal basis. Set side by side, the two show levels, not a change.

What to watch

  • From 12 November 2026, dailyCaratRadar's two averages on the 05:00 ET tape. The test: whether the gap holds within a point of 82.5% over a week.
  • When Rapaport's index page answersRAPI's one-carat point, carried at 25 October. The test: whether the natural top-specification level has moved.

What would change this call

If CaratRadar's natural and lab-grown pools turn out to hold different weight bands under 'one carat', the 82.5% would mix sizes, and the IDEX-against-Lepdo band would be the better guide.

How the four bases are built

CaratRadar averages online asking prices across all listed specifications. IDEX averages trade asking prices for a defined round driver. Lepdo publishes a wholesale band for one lab-grown specification. BriteCo drew on appraisal and insurance records. None is a transaction price.
