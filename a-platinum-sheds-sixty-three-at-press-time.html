Platinum sheds $63.00 to $1,634.00, all four metals lower

A six o'clock reading of the Kitco bid puts gold at $4,117.40, silver at $60.029 and palladium at $1,119.00. All four metals sit lower than they did at five.

By The Gold & Metals Desk

7 October 2026

What changed

Platinum led the fall. Platinum's bid reads $1,634.00, down $63.00 against the mark Carat Capital printed on 6 October. All four metals are lower, and all four fell further after the five o'clock filing.

What it means

  • If you quote platinum work: The session's own range runs $1,632.00 to $1,716.00. That band is worth 5.14% of the bid you are quoting off.
  • If you hedge a gold book: Gold's fall is the mildest of the four at 0.79%, and its bid-ask spread is $2.00.
  • If you price palladium: The $40.00 bid-ask spread is twenty times gold's. Thin quoting, not direction, is the risk in that metal.
  • What this is not: A close. These are intraday bids on a live board, and both feeds read as an open session.

Platinum took the worst of it

A wide session. Platinum traded between $1,632.00 and $1,716.00 on the same board stamp. That $84.00 band is 5.14% of its own bid, the widest of the four in proportion.

Both columns agree. The fall reads 3.71% against Carat Capital's 6 October mark and 4.00% on Kitco's own day column. The direction is identical and the reference close is not.

Every mark fell after five

Four write-backs. The five o'clock filing had gold at $4,122.90, silver at $60.233, platinum at $1,648.00 and palladium at $1,125.00. All four are lower at press time.

One price, one tape. Under the One-Price Rule the press-time bid replaces the opening mark in Carat Capital's record. The figure above is the only gold number the paper carries this morning.

A second board says the same

Three inside the band. An independent CNBC feed reads silver at 60.191, platinum at 1,641.98 and palladium at 1,137.89, each inside Kitco's own bid-ask range for the metal.

Gold misses by cents. CNBC has gold at 4,117.26, fourteen cents below Kitco's bid and 0.0034% outside the band. The gap is disclosed rather than smoothed.

The session is open. Kitco's payload carries sub-second timestamps and CNBC's market-status field reads REG_MKT on all four quotes, stamped 06:06 Eastern.

What to watch

  • 08:00 Eastern, 7 October 2026The LBMA gold and silver fixes, the first settled prices of the day against these intraday bids.
  • 8 October 2026Whether platinum holds above $1,632.00, the session low on this board, or opens through it.
  • Early November 2026Chinese October reserve data, the official-sector buying that sits underneath the gold price in this table.

What would change this call

A recovery through the session high. Platinum at $1,716.00 would put it above the 6 October mark and make this morning a dip rather than a break; the low and the high are only $84.00 apart.

Which price this paper prints

Carat Capital prints one price per metal per morning, taken from the Kitco board's bid rather than its mid or its ask, and the tape carries that number for the day. Where a metals story leads the edition the board is read again at press time, and a different reading replaces the opening mark in the dated tape file and in the live tape together. The change column is always computed against the mark the paper itself last printed, looked up in the prior dated file.
