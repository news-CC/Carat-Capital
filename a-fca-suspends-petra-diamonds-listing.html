FCA suspends Petra Diamonds' listing with its audit unfinished

London trading in the shares stopped at 07:30 UK time on 2 November, after Petra missed the 31 October deadline for its annual report. Petra says restoration will not be automatic once it publishes.

By Carat Capital

3 November 2026 Corrected

What changed

The suspension took effect. The FCA suspended Petra Diamonds' listing at 07:30 UK time on 2 November, and London trading stopped with it. Petra had warned of this on 22 October.

What it means

  • If you hold Petra shares: Trading has stopped. Petra will seek a return after publishing, subject to the FCA and the exchange.
  • If you buy Cullinan goods: The notice concerns the listing and the accounts. It names no change at the mine.
  • What this is not: A finding on the accounts. Petra says only that the audit remains in progress.

Restoration is not automatic.

Audit still running. Petra says the group audit remains in progress, past the 31 October deadline. It is working with its auditors to publish as soon as practicable.

Two approvals needed. Petra intends to seek a return to listing and trading after it publishes. That needs the agreement of the FCA and the London Stock Exchange.

Both percentages fell on new shares.

Terris reported 27.04%. The Terris Fund, of Nassau, notified Petra on 22 October that it held 27.04% as of 1 July. Its previous notice, in November 2025, gave 28.30%.

Azvalor's votes rose. Azvalor Asset Management, of Madrid, held 13.93% on 16 October, against 15.02% in its December 2025 notice. Its vote count rose; its percentage fell.

Coupon paid in shares. Petra admitted 19,219,980 new shares on 1 July to pay its June note coupon. That took its share count to 354,640,773.

No word on the notes.

Notes status unstated. The notice's own boilerplate says Petra's loan notes due 2030 are listed on Euronext Dublin. It does not say whether the suspension affects them.

What to watch

  • No date set by PetraPublication of the annual report for the year to 30 June 2026. The test: whether Petra then applies for restoration, as it says it intends to.
  • After publicationThe FCA's and the exchange's decisions. The test: whether trading returns, which Petra says is not automatic.

Corrections1 note

Corrected, 4 November 2026: this page said "Azvalor's stake fell" and that the forms did not say which way either holder moved. Azvalor's vote count rose, from 48,038,053 to 49,416,725, and Terris's from 90,529,412 to 95,905,121; both percentages fell because Petra issued 19,219,980 new shares on 1 July. The page also said Terris "holds" 27.04%, a position as of 1 July, and that the notice did not mention the loan notes; it names them but does not say whether they are affected. What was right as published: every percentage and date. Raised by Carat Capital's Standards desk on 3 November 2026.

What would change this call

Publication of the annual report, followed by the FCA's and the exchange's agreement, would end the suspension. Petra says there is no certainty as to its timing.

How the deadline works

Under DTR 4.1.3R a listed company publishes its annual financial report within four months of its year end. Petra's year ended 30 June, so its deadline was 31 October.
