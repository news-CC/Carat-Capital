Lao Feng Xiang ends September with 4,809 outlets, down 107 in Q3

According to a summary of the company's notice, the chain closed 194 outlets and opened 87 in the third quarter. Main-business revenue fell 44.67% over nine months, and gross margin rose to 10.43%.

By Carat Capital

31 October 2026

What changed

Closures outran openings. Lao Feng Xiang closed 194 outlets and opened 87 in the third quarter, according to a summary of its notice. It ended September with 4,809.

What it means

  • If you supply Chinese chains: Closures hit low-yield franchises in tier-3 and tier-4 cities; about half of new franchises opened in tier-1 and tier-2.
  • If you sell gold jewellery: Comparable direct stores grew 10.02% while jewellery revenue fell 35.13%.
  • What this is not: A full set of accounts. These figures come from a summary of the notice.

Source: Guandian's summary of Lao Feng Xiang's notice of third-quarter 2026 operating data, 30 October 2026. The notice itself was not read.

Closures concentrate in small cities.

Low-yield franchises shut. Over nine months the chain closed 537 low-yield franchise stores in tier-3 and tier-4 cities, about 73% of all franchise closures, the summary says.

Bigger cities gain. It opened 90 franchise stores in tier-1 and tier-2 cities, about 49% of franchise openings. The net quarterly fall was 107 outlets.

Own stores grew, revenue fell.

Direct stores rose. Comparable directly operated stores, excluding those opened or closed in the period, grew sales 10.02%. Online platform sales rose 49.19% to RMB 466m.

Gold trading fell most. Main-business revenue fell to RMB 26.49bn. Jewellery fell 35.13% and gold trading 82.20%; the summary gives no cause for either.

Margin widens, openings planned.

Margin up. Gross margin rose 2.26 points to 10.43%. For the fourth quarter the chain plans 38 new outlets, eight of them in Shanghai.

What to watch

  • 31 December 2026The year-end outlet count against 38 planned fourth-quarter openings. The test: a count above 4,809.
  • Full-year 2026 report, 2027The year's tier-3 and tier-4 franchise closures. The test: whether the total runs past 537.

What would change this call

The company's own notice, read at the exchange, carrying a different outlet count or revenue line would change this call.

What main-business revenue counts

主营业务收入 is revenue from the company's main lines: jewellery, gold trading, pens and crafts. It is not the operating revenue line in the half-year report, so the two are not compared here.
