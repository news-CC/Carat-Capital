Every close is an exchange print. Nothing here is estimated, and nothing is anyone else's number.

What was right as published: every level and change.

Method changed, 10 November 2026: the indices are now built on each exchange's own dates, from completed sessions only. A bar from a session still open is dropped, and the headline date is the latest day on which at least 18 of 20 constituents have a completed bar. Before this, Hong Kong and Tokyo bars could sit a day ahead of Europe and New York in the same point. On the old build the CC20 fell 6.4% on 5 November; on the new build that step is 3.1%. Levels and changes printed before 10 November were on the old build.

Corrected, 9 November 2026: on 8 November this page and the homepage price desk printed a 1D change of +0.00% for all five indices. The newest point in the series was dated Sunday 8 November, created when one constituent printed a bar stamped that day, so the 1D compared Friday's values with themselves. It was not a session move. The 1D is now withheld whenever the newest point falls on a weekend. What was right as published: the index levels.

Methodology — read before quoting

Each constituent is measured in its home currency and rebased to 100 at its last close on or before 2026-01-02; an index is the equal-weight mean of those relatives, so no exchange rate and no giant market cap can speak for the group. Constituents were chosen for listed, liquid exposure to jewelry, diamonds or watches; conglomerates carry their whole business (Anglo American is in for De Beers, and carries the rest of Anglo with it). The Pit holds only three names because that is how many investable diamond miners remain — that fact is itself a finding of this desk. Closes are exchange prints retrieved from Yahoo Finance's public chart interface at computation time and spot-checked against independent sources; gold is gold, COMEX front month. Exchanges close at different hours, so the latest session differs by market; each series carries its own last print. The indices are computed with each edition, not live. They are editorial instruments of Carat Capital, not investment advice and not a tradeable benchmark — quote them with attribution: “Carat Capital indices, 2026-11-13 close.”
