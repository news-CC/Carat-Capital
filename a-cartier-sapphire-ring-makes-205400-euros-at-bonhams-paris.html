Cartier 11.5-ct sapphire ring makes €205,400 at Bonhams Paris

Bonhams says the Madagascan stone shows no indications of treatment; the ring made 3.4 times its high estimate on 29 October. A Cartier rose brooch made €83,800 against €15,000 to €20,000.

By Carat Capital

31 October 2026

What changed

The sapphire led. Bonhams says a Cartier ring with an 11.5-carat Madagascan sapphire was the top lot of its Paris sale on 29 October. It made €205,400.

What it means

  • If you buy sapphire: Two sapphires described with no indications of treatment made 3.4 and 1.8 times their high estimates.
  • If you consign signed jewels: Five of the six named lots beat their high estimates; the tourmaline did not.
  • What this is not: A sale total. Bonhams gives none, nor a share of lots sold.

Sapphires without treatment indications led.

No treatment indicated. Bonhams describes the oval 11.5-carat 'Royal Blue' stone as Madagascan, with no indications of treatment. Its estimate was €40,000 to €60,000.

Sri Lanka followed. A necklace with a colour-change Sri Lankan sapphire of about 22 carats made €46,680, 1.8 times its high estimate. Bonhams describes that stone the same way.

The preview's lead sold inside estimate.

Paraiba within range. The 81.17-carat Paraiba-type tourmaline pendant led Carat Capital's 10 October preview. It made €64,600, inside its €60,000 to €80,000 estimate.

Chaumet not listed. The preview's 12.5-carat Chaumet sapphire, estimated at €45,000 to €65,000, is not among the results Bonhams names.

Older signed pieces beat estimates.

Brooch at 4.2 times. A Cartier rose brooch in ruby, emerald and diamond, from about 1940, made €83,800. It came from the collection of the milliner Madame Paulette.

Lacloche well above. A Lacloche Frères ruby and diamond necklace from about 1912 made €32,500 against €6,000 to €10,000.

What to watch

  • 11 November 2026Sotheby's Geneva, where a 69.44-carat Ceylon sapphire listed as unheated is estimated at CHF 580,000 to 750,000. The test: a result above the high estimate.

What would change this call

A full lot list showing many lots unsold or below estimate would change this call. Bonhams names only six results.

Estimate against result

An estimate is the house's pre-sale range. Dividing the result by the high estimate shows how far bidding ran past it; a figure under 1.0 means the lot sold inside or below the range.
