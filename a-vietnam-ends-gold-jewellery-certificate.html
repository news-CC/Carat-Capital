Vietnam ends gold jewellery certificate, caps cash deals at VND 5m

Decree 390/2026 took effect on 16 October, cutting the non-cash line from VND 20 million a day. Makers with foreign contracts can seek raw-gold import licences; pending certificate files are closed.

By Carat Capital

21 October 2026

What changed

Less cash, fewer permits. Vietnam now requires non-cash payment for gold deals of VND 5 million or more a day, down from VND 20 million, from 16 October. Jewellery makers no longer need a central bank certificate.

What it means

  • If you sell gold in Vietnam: Customers buying VND 5 million or more a day must pay by non-cash means.
  • If you make jewellery there: The central bank's production certificate is no longer a condition to operate.
  • What this is not: A ban on cash. Deals under VND 5 million a day are outside the rule.

The cash line drops

A quarter of before. Gold purchases and sales worth VND 5 million or more in a day must be paid by non-cash means. The old line was VND 20 million, so the threshold falls 75%.

Signed and live together. Deputy Prime Minister Nguyễn Văn Thắng signed Decree 390/2026 on 16 October, and it took effect that day. It amends Decree 24/2012, the base rule for Vietnam's gold trade.

Jewellery making loses its certificate

The permit step goes. The decree repeals all its rules on making, processing and trading jewellery gold, both outlets report. A central bank production certificate is no longer a condition to operate.

Pending files close. Certificate applications still undecided on 16 October are terminated, and the central bank will notify the applicants.

Exporters can import. Makers with foreign processing contracts may get raw-gold import licences for that work. Foreign-invested makers can win annual licences based on capacity and export record.

Standards and reporting move

A ministry sets standards. The Ministry of Science and Technology will set national standards for jewellery and bar gold and check their quality and measurement.

Dealers and banks report. Gold businesses and commercial banks must report bar trading, gold imports and exports, and the use and stock of imported raw gold.

What to watch

  • Licence renewalsExisting gold import licences and quotas run to their terms; renewals fall under the new decree.
  • Annual licence roundThe central bank's first yearly raw-gold import licences for foreign-invested jewellery makers.

What would change this call

Guidance that restores a separate jewellery permit under another name. Then the licence change is cosmetic and only the cash line bites.

How Vietnam regulates gold

Decree 24/2012 is the base rule. As amended, the central bank inspects bar-gold production and trading, gold imports and exports, and the use of imported raw gold. Decree 232/2025 amended it before this decree did.
