GJEPC takes 89 Indian companies into 1,228 sq m in Hong Kong

The India Pavilion opened in two phases, at AsiaWorld-Expo on 14 September and the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on 16 September. Exhibitors at the fair come from 44 countries.

By The Diamonds Desk

18 September 2026

What changed

India took a hall. GJEPC's India Pavilion at Jewellery & Gem World Hong Kong holds 89 Indian companies across 1,228 square metres, the council's own count.

What it means

  • If you source from India: 89 exporters are in one pavilion, averaging 13.8 square metres each.
  • If you exhibit at Asian fairs: the fair draws exhibitors from 44 countries and expects visitors from 141.
  • What this is not: a trade figure. No order value or buyer count is published.

Small booths throughout. That is an average of 13.8 square metres a company, which is a small-booth pavilion rather than a hall of flagships.

What would change this call

A published order value. A pavilion is a cost line until someone counts what was written in it, and 89 companies in 1,228 square metres says only what India paid for, not what it sold.

What a pavilion figure is and is not

A national pavilion is floor space a trade council buys in bulk and allots to member exporters, usually at a subsidy. Its two published numbers, companies and square metres, measure the council's commitment and the average stand size, which is a real signal about who is being sent: 13.8 square metres is a two-table booth for showing parcels, not a brand installation. What it cannot measure is demand. Attendance, order value and repeat buyers are counted afterwards if they are counted at all, and no fair organiser is obliged to publish any of them.

Method

GJEPC's own news page was fetched directly on 18 September 2026 and returned 32,796 bytes; the sentence carrying both figures reads that the India Pavilion features 89 Indian companies across 1,228 sq. m. of exhibition space. The 14 and 16 September opening dates, the 44 exhibiting countries and the 141 expected visitor countries are in the same release. The page is dated 17 September 2026 and its event dates agree with its page date, so no dateline gap arises. The 13.8 square metre average is Carat Capital's division and is labelled as such in the key figures and in Table I.
