Fossil Q3 gross margin climbs to 59.8% as net sales rise 0.4%

Net income was $11.7 million against a $39.9 million loss a year earlier, in the quarter to 3 October. The company raised its full-year net sales outlook to a range of $980 million to $990 million.

By Carat Capital

11 November 2026

What changed

Margin did the work. Fossil's third-quarter gross margin rose to 59.8% from 49.0%, on net sales up 0.4%. The company raised its full-year outlook.

What it means

  • If you hold the shares: part of the margin is timing. Fossil cites lower licensed-brand minimum royalties and their earlier recognition.
  • If you distribute watches: traditional watches rose 6.4% at constant currency in the quarter while jewellery fell 10.8%.
  • What this is not: a sales recovery. Store closures cut Q3 net sales by about 190 basis points, Fossil says.

Lower licensed-brand royalties lifted margin.

Three causes named. Fossil attributes the rise mainly to lower licensed-brand minimum royalties and earlier recognition of them. Better product margins and lower tariffs also helped, it says.

Costs fell 11.1%. Operating expenses were $137.0 million against $154.1 million a year earlier.

Operating and net profit. Operating income was $25.3 million against a loss of $21.7 million. Net income was $11.7 million.

Watches grew, jewellery shrank.

Watches up 6.4%. Traditional watch sales rose 6.4% at constant currency. The Fossil brand itself rose 13.6%.

Jewellery down 10.8%. Jewellery sales fell 10.8% and leathers 20.5%, both at constant currency.

Asia led. Asia's net sales rose 15.1% at constant currency and the Americas' 0.1%. Europe's fell 5.6%.

Full-year outlook up, interest too.

Guidance raised. Fossil now expects 2026 net sales in a range of $980 million to $990 million, excluding currency effects.

Interest more than doubled. Interest expense was $8.9 million against $4.2 million, on higher debt, costs and rates. Total debt was $208.0 million.

What to watch

  • Holiday season, November to December 2026Fossil's fourth quarter. The test: whether full-year net sales land inside $980 million to $990 million.
  • Fourth-quarter results, early 2027The full-year margin. The test: how much of the 59.8% came from royalty timing.

What would change this call

If the fourth quarter's gross margin falls back towards 49% once the royalty timing reverses, the third quarter reads as an accounting quarter, not a turnaround.

What constant currency means here

Constant currency restates this year's sales at last year's exchange rates. Fossil reports it as a non-GAAP measure, with a reconciliation in the release. Reported sales rose 0.4%; at constant currency, 2.0%.
