SNB books CHF 1.2bn gold gain after a CHF 6.4bn loss in H1

The Swiss National Bank's nine-month profit was CHF 44.2bn, with CHF 43.3bn on foreign currency positions. Gold traded at CHF 112,118 a kilogram at end-September, against CHF 104,812 at end-June.

By Carat Capital

31 October 2026

What changed

Gold loss reversed. The SNB recorded a CHF 1.2bn valuation gain on gold for the first nine months. In H1 the same line was a CHF 6.4bn loss.

What it means

  • If you hold gold stock: The gain is price alone; volume was unchanged. Gold in francs rose 7.0% in Q3.
  • If you price in dollars: Dollar gold on the tape fell 7.6% from its 30 September mark to $3,874.40 at Friday's close.
  • What this is not: A purchase or a sale. No metal moved.

Price did the work.

Volume unchanged. The SNB says its gold holdings remained unchanged in volume terms. Gold traded at CHF 110,919 a kilogram at end-2025.

A third-quarter swing. From a loss at end-June to a gain at end-September, the gold line moved CHF 7.6bn. The subtraction is ours, not the bank's.

Currency positions carried the profit.

Exchange rates helped. Exchange-rate gains on foreign currency positions were CHF 29.8bn. Equity price gains were CHF 23.7bn, against CHF 22.6bn of price losses on interest-bearing paper.

What the release leaves out.

No tonnage given. The release gives no tonnage. Dividing the CHF 7.6bn swing by the CHF 7,306 rise per kilogram gives about 1,040 tonnes, on our arithmetic.

Provisional until December. The SNB says only provisional conclusions are possible on the annual result. Its allocation to currency reserve provisions is set at the end of the year.

What to watch

  • 31 December 2026Gold in francs against CHF 110,919, the end-2025 price. The test: below it, the year's gold line turns to a loss.
  • After 31 December 2026The SNB's full-year gold valuation line, set at the end of the year. The test: whether it stays above zero after October's fall in dollar gold.

What would change this call

A year-end franc gold price below CHF 110,919 a kilogram would turn the nine-month gain into a loss for the year, with volume unchanged.

How the gold line moves

The SNB reports a valuation gain or loss on gold holdings that were unchanged in volume. The line therefore moves with the franc gold price between reporting dates.

Arithmetic, ours: 1.2 − (−6.4) = 7.6; 44.2 − 25.2 = 19.0; 43.3 − 31.7 = 11.6; 112,118 / 104,812 = 1.0697 -> +7.0%; 112,118 − 104,812 = 7,306; 7.6bn / 7,306 = 1.04m kg; on the SNB's one-decimal rounding (7.55 to 7.65) the range is about 1,033 to 1,047 tonnes; the nine-month check, 1.2bn / (112,118 − 110,919 = 1,199) = 1.0m kg, agrees within its wider rounding. Components do not sum: 43.3 + 1.2 + 0.1 = 44.6 against 44.2; 31.7 − 6.4 + 0.1 = 25.4 against 25.2; no share of profit is printed.
