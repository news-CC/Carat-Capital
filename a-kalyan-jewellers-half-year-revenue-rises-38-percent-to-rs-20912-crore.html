Kalyan Jewellers half-year revenue rises 38% to Rs 20,912 crore

Profit after tax rose 48% to Rs 776 crore in the six months to 30 September, unaudited results show. Second-quarter revenue grew 31%, slower than the June quarter's 46%.

By Carat Capital

11 November 2026

What changed

Half-year profit outgrew sales. Kalyan Jewellers' first-half profit rose 48% to Rs 776 crore on revenue up 38%, unaudited results show. The six months ran to 30 September.

What it means

  • If you supply Indian jewellers: the company says the festive quarter started well, especially Navratri and Dhanteras.
  • If you hold the shares: June-quarter revenue grew 46%; September's grew 31%.
  • What this is not: a volume figure. The release gives rupee revenue and profit only.

Key figures

Half-year revenue+38% on the yearRs 20,912 cr
Half-year profit+48%, after taxRs 776 cr
Q2 revenue+31% on the yearRs 10,323 cr
Q2 profit+64%, after taxRs 427 cr

Source: Kalyan Jewellers India, press release on unaudited results for the quarter and half year to 30 September 2026, filed with the NSE 10 November 2026 · consolidated unless stated

The half grew 38%.

H1 revenue up 38%. Consolidated revenue rose to Rs 20,912 crore in the half, from Rs 15,125 crore a year earlier.

H1 profit rose faster. Profit after tax rose to Rs 776 crore in the half, from Rs 525 crore.

The second quarter slowed.

Q2 revenue up 31%. Second-quarter consolidated revenue was Rs 10,323 crore, against Rs 10,589 crore in the June quarter.

June quarter grew 46%. The June quarter's revenue rose 46%, so growth slowed by 15 points in the September quarter.

Q2 profit up 64%. Second-quarter profit after tax was Rs 427 crore.

India made most of Q2.

India Q2 up 32%. Standalone India revenue was Rs 9,007 crore in the quarter, 87.3% of the group's, on Carat Capital's division.

India profit up 62%. Standalone profit after tax was Rs 424 crore in the quarter. Kalyan has 546 showrooms in India, the US, the UK and the Middle East.

What to watch

  • 31 December 2026Kalyan's third quarter ends. The test: whether the strong festive start its executive director describes shows in revenue.
  • November 2026 to February 2027India's wedding season. The test: the company says it is upbeat about it.

The story so far

  1. 11 August 2026Kalyan opens a Tamil Nadu-only banner, Akshaya Thanga Maligai.
  2. 9 August 2026June quarter: revenue Rs 10,589 crore, up 46%; recycled gold over 46%.

Go deeper

What would change this call

If third-quarter revenue growth falls below the second quarter's 31% despite the festive start the company describes, the half reads as a June-quarter story.

What Kalyan's figures cover

Consolidated figures include the Middle East, the US, the UK and Candere, the online arm. Standalone covers India. Revenue is in rupees and includes the metal's value, so it moves with the gold price.

Method

NSE press release PDF, fetched 06:10 EST 11 November 2026, door.py 200, 1,364,227 bytes; page 2 read on the rendered page image, not OCR alone. Page text: 'consolidated revenue of Rs 20,912 crore for the first half of FY27 as against Rs 15,125 crore ... a growth of 38%'; 'Consolidated PAT for the first half of FY27 was Rs 776 crore as against a PAT of Rs 525 crore ... a growth of 48%'; 'Consolidated revenue and PAT for Q2 FY27 was Rs 10,323 Cr and Rs 427 Cr respectively, a growth of 31% and 64%'; 'Standalone revenue (India) and PAT for Q2 FY27 was Rs 9,007 Cr and Rs 424 Cr respectively, a growth of 32% and 62%'; '546 showrooms across India, USA, UK and the Middle East'. Division, Carat Capital's: 20,912 / 15,125 = 1.3826 -> +38.3%; 776 / 525 = 1.478 -> +47.8%; 20,912 - 10,323 = 10,589, equal to the June-quarter revenue Kalyan reported in August (our 9 August piece: Rs 10,589 crore, up 46% from Rs 7,268 crore); 46 - 31 = 15 points; 9,007 / 10,323 = 0.8725 -> 87.3%. The release prints no year-earlier rupee figure for the quarter, so none is derived. Quote: 'The ongoing festive quarter has started off very well', Ramesh Kalyanaraman, executive director, nine words; paraphrased on the page, not quoted.
