US JEWELRY +8.6% IN H1 — American jewelry revenue rose 8.6% over the first half and 13% in June, but the average purchase price climbed about 19–20%; tennis bracelets (+14%) and pearls led, natural-diamond units fell as prices held (Tenoris)CHINA COUNTERS RUN ON GOLD — Chow Tai Fook's quarterly retail sales rose 15.1%, Hong Kong and Macau up 44.8%, with weight-based gold jewelry up 63.7% there on softer prices; Luk Fook +32%, Chow Sang Sang +16%SILVER NARROWS THE RATIO — Silver added about 2% Friday to roughly $58 while gold held near $4,061, tightening the gold-silver ratio to about 69.5 from 70.7; the Fed meets July 28–29 with a hold widely expectedANTWERP SHORT ON GOODS — A summer shortage of polished goods in Antwerp is steering buyers toward India ahead of IIJS Premiere (Aug 6–10, Mumbai); the RapNet 0.30ct index is up 4.25% on the month as small stones lead the turnDIAVIK'S LAST CARATS — Rio Tinto recovered 33,000 carats from the shuttered Diavik mine's remaining ore in Q2; diamond output fell 97% year on year, H1 production 1.1M carats (−51%), with final sales running through year-endDE BEERS SALE GETS A CLOCK — Anglo American selected the Gareth Penny-led consortium as its leading bidder for De Beers and expects to finalize the sale by end-2026; a two-year production pause is planned at VenetiaMORE ROUGH, LESS MONEY — De Beers' Q2 rough sales fell 44% year on year to $665 million and the average realized price dropped 32% to $105 a carat, even as production rose 88%; the house reported an estimated first-half lossSWATCH HOLDS THE LINE — Swatch Group's first-half jewelry and watch sales edged up 2% to about $3.6 billion, per trade figures, as Greater China gold demand offset a soft Swiss watch secondary marketOIL LIFTS THE RISK TAPE — Crude reached six-week highs on renewed US strikes on Iran and Houthi Red Sea attacks, lifting inflation expectations; two-year Treasury yields touched a 17-month high ahead of the Fed

Up nine percent, and the average ticket did the lifting

US jewelry revenue rose 8.6% in the first half and 13% in June, but the average purchase price climbed about 19 to 20 percent. Fewer buyers, spending more: bracelets, pearls and higher-priced pieces carried the gain.
