The Wire
FOUR METALS, THIRD SESSION — At 5:58am New York gold bid $4,157.80, up $81.20 or 1.99%; silver $61.35, up $1.94 or 3.27%; platinum $1,747.00, up 1.10%; palladium $1,355.00, up 1.96%. Gold has added about $109 an ounce against the $4,048.80 this paper carried on Tuesday, the largest single-session move since the daily 5:58am mark beganTHE WAR PREMIUM COMES OUT — The bid follows reports that the United States, Iran and Oman are closing on an interim agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with an announcement targeted for Wednesday; lower oil has trimmed the odds of a September Federal Reserve increase to about 57% from about 67% a day earlier, and non-farm payrolls land FridayTHREE DOLLARS FIFTY-ONE A GRAM — Fine gold at $4,157.80 an ounce is $133.68 a gram against $130.17 on Tuesday, $3.51 added to the metal cost of every piece in a case overnight; the gold to silver ratio tightened to 67.8 from about 69.0 and platinum stands at 42.0% of the gold priceINDIA CONFIRMS IT AT HOME — October gold on the Multi Commodity Exchange rose ₹1,060 or 0.73% to ₹1,45,359 per 10 grams and September silver ₹2,494 or 1.13% to ₹2,24,109 a kilogram; Indian jewellery demand fell 19% in the first quarter and to 75.1 tonnes in the second, down 15%TWENTY PERCENT IN JUNE — Hong Kong sales of jewellery, watches, clocks and valuable gifts reached HK$5.49 billion, about $699.9 million, up 20% year on year, inside total retail of HK$31.49 billion, up 5%; first-half hard luxury rose 25% to HK$31.07 billion against 10% for all retail, a fourteenth consecutive month of growthFIFTY POINT FIVE ON FIXED PRICE — Luk Fook's year to 31 March 2026, disclosed in late June: net profit up 88.7% to HK$2.015 billion on revenue up 29% to HK$17.21 billion, gross margin 36.7%, with fixed-price jewellery sales up 50.5% against 22% for gold and platinum sold by weightEIGHTY PERCENT TO THE CONSUMER — Natural Diamond Council chief executive Amber Pepper told a GJEPC meeting in Mumbai on Monday that at least 80% of council advertising will go to consumers rather than the trade, with a Diamond Desire Index baseline due in September across India, China and the United States and a Natural Diamond Mark to be inscribed on stonesTWO THIRTY ON A MOONWALKER'S OMEGA — Joopiter's first standalone watch sale, Keeping Measure, closes on a rolling basis through 11 August; the top lot, an Omega Speedmaster engraved for Alan Bean and one of thirty made after the first moon landing, is estimated at $250,000 to $350,000 against a standing bid of $230,000SIX MONTHS EMPTY AT PANDORA — André Branch becomes president of Pandora's North America cluster on 15 August, a seat vacant since Luciano Rodembusch left in February; he arrives from the chief executive's chair at R.E.M. Beauty after two decades at Estée Lauder, L'Oréal, Diageo and Kraft Heinz, and none in jewellery
