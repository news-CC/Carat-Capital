The Wire
SEVEN-WEEK HIGH, FOURTH SESSION — At 6:03am New York gold bid $4,270.90, up $24.70 or 0.58%, its highest in seven weeks and a fourth consecutive advance; platinum $1,753.00, up $23.00 or 1.33%; palladium $1,367.00, up $20.00 or 1.48%. Against the $4,157.80 this paper carried on Wednesday, gold has added $113.10 an ounce, or 2.72%, in twenty-four hoursSILVER ALONE IN THE RED — Silver bid $61.67, down 25 cents or 0.41%, the only one of the four metals lower and the first down session after leading the complex all week with gains of 3.27% and 3.2%; the gold to silver ratio widens back to 69.3 from 67.8, undoing two sessions of tightening in oneONE THIRTY-SEVEN THIRTY-ONE A GRAM — Fine gold at $4,270.90 an ounce is $137.31 a gram against $133.68 on Wednesday and $130.17 on Tuesday, $3.63 added to the metal cost of every piece in a case since yesterday morning and $7.14 since Tuesday; platinum stands at 41.0% of the gold price, down from 42.0%PAYROLLS AT HALF PAST EIGHT — The Bureau of Labor Statistics releases July non-farm payrolls at 8:30am Friday, the first full read on American hiring since the Federal Reserve held on 29 July with three dissents; June's print was 57,000. This desk carried the same release as a binary on 2 August and has nothing to add until it landsALROSA BOOKS A LOSS — Half-year accounts for the world's largest rough producer by volume show revenue of 74.2 billion roubles, about $932.5 million, down 36%, and a net loss of 10.67 billion roubles, about $134.1 million, against a $490.1 million profit a year earlier; several open pits go to care and maintenance in 2026 and mining at Severalmaz, roughly 10% of Russian output, is suspendedTWENTY-FIVE YEARS OF TIFFANY — Pam Cloud becomes president of Blue Nile on 10 August, reporting to Signet chief operating and financial officer Joan Hilson; she spent more than 25 of her 30 years in luxury retail at Tiffany and Co., latterly as senior vice president and chief merchandising officer, and founded the pearl brand Roseate JewelryTHIRTEEN BILLION INSIDE THE PERIMETER — Deferred payment credit came under Financial Conduct Authority supervision in the United Kingdom on 15 July; the market grew from about £60 million of transactions in 2017 to more than £13 billion in 2024 with around 11 million British users, and lenders now owe affordability checks, upfront terms and Financial Ombudsman access, with six months from commencement to apply for full authorisationNINE THIRTY-FOUR FOR ONE SEVENTEEN — Onyx Partners has bid $934 million for 117 J.C. Penney stores covering 15.7 million square feet across 35 states, about $8 million a store or roughly $59 a square foot; a $947 million offer was rejected in December by Copper Property CTL Pass Through Trust, and the firm says financing is in place to close on 25 SeptemberSIXTY-SEVEN PERCENT ASKED A MACHINE — Attentive's survey of 600 United States adults finds 67% used an AI chatbot to help them shop in the past three months, rising to 80% of Gen Z and with 51% comparing brands that way; separately 62% will plan holiday purchases before November and 71% will start buying before Black Friday
