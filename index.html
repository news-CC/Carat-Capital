The Wire
FIFTEEN YEARS, NO INDIAN TAX — India's Lok Sabha passed the Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 on 6 August, proposing to exempt qualifying foreign sellers of rough diamonds from Indian income tax from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2041 where the sale takes place in a Special Notified Zone; the zones that matter are inside the Bharat Diamond Bourse in Mumbai and the Surat Diamond Bourse, and the class covers miners, brokers, aggregators, auction operators and sightholders. It still requires Rajya Sabha passage and presidential assent, with no hearing date setFIFTH SESSION, EVERY METAL UP — At 5:58am New York gold bid $4,314.50, up $75.20 or 1.77%, a fifth consecutive advance and above Thursday's seven-week high; platinum $1,772.00, up $54.00 or 3.14%; palladium $1,382.00, up $29.00 or 2.14%. Against the $4,270.90 this paper carried on Thursday, gold has added $43.60 an ounce, or 1.02%, in twenty-four hoursSILVER TAKES IT BACK, UP 4.71% — Silver bid $64.29, up $2.89 or 4.71%, the largest single-session move of the four metals and a full reversal of Thursday's 0.41% decline; the gold to silver ratio tightens to 67.1 from 69.3. This desk wrote on Thursday that silver turning red beneath a gold high was the clearest internal warning the tape had printed all week, and records that call against the print that followed itPAYROLLS AT HALF PAST EIGHT — The Bureau of Labor Statistics releases July non-farm payrolls at 8:30am Friday, after this edition closes. ADP put July private hiring at 44,000 against about 50,000 expected, JOLTS came in softer and initial claims rose to 199,000; the CME FedWatch tool has September's move at 56.9% against 63.4% a week earlier. Iran says it has reached an understanding with Oman on a proposed Hormuz shipping lane, and has been explicit that it is a temporary route rather than a full reopeningONE THIRTY-TWO AGAINST THREE THIRTY-NINE — China Gold Association half-year figures put Chinese gold jewellery consumption at 132.13 tonnes, down 33.88%, against bar and coin demand of 339.34 tonnes, up 28.42%, with total consumption up 1.23% to 511.41 tonnes; the World Gold Council put the same half at about 136 tonnes and a roughly 30% fall, a gap of some four tonnes on differing definitions that this desk carries rather than reconcilesTWENTY-TWO THIRTY-EIGHT AN ORDER — Brilliant Earth second-quarter revenue rose 6% to $115.1 million on a 2.1% fall in total orders, with average order value up 8% to $2,238 and gross margin at 57.9%, a 360 basis point sequential improvement; net income was $0.8 million against a $1.1 million loss, fine jewellery bookings grew 32%, and full-year guidance was raised to $459 million to $462 million with the third quarter guided to roughly flat salesTHE FOURTEEN PERCENT THAT ISN'T — The World Platinum Investment Council projects Chinese platinum jewellery down 14% in 2026 and global platinum jewellery down 6%, but attributes the Chinese figure to an unrepeated second-quarter 2025 stock build; excluding it, Chinese fabrication shows a small year-on-year increase. WPIC also forecasts a fourth consecutive annual market deficit for 2026 at 297,000 ouncesTWENTY-NINE NINETY-NINE AT THE CHAIR — Claire's is relaunching in-store piercing across the United States and Canada from August, its second overhaul of that service in three years, and adding lab-grown diamond and coloured gemstone studs to the starter assortment; kits begin at $29.99 with the piercing free, with 14-carat white gold lab-grown studs at 0.10 carats total weight sold as complete kitsDUBAI RETAIL SPREADS BY AED 7.25 — Khaleej Times had Dubai 24-carat gold at AED 517.50 a gram on Friday morning while this paper's own metals desk read AED 510.25 the same day and the overnight desk AED 514.00 on Thursday, a spread of AED 7.25 a gram between two readings of the same session. Both figures are printed and neither is picked. On the diamond lines NAT1 and LGD1 are carried unchanged, the lab-grown reference holding at $709 on CaratRadar for a third straight day against $536 on a second tracker

Fifteen years, no tax: what Mumbai just offered to take from Dubai

India's lower house passed a Bill on 6 August exempting qualifying foreign rough sellers from income tax between 1 October 2026 and 31 March 2041. It replaces a safe harbour that presumes profit at 1.25% of gross revenue, against corporate rates reaching about 33%.
