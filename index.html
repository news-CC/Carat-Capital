The Wire
THE FED HOLDS, THREE DISSENT — The FOMC kept its target range at 3.50–3.75% in a 9–3 vote, with Beth Hammack, Neel Kashkari and Lorie Logan pressing for a quarter-point hike; Chair Kevin Warsh's second meeting again shipped without forward guidance, and gold eased from about $4,046 to near $4,029TIFFANY AND BULGARI CARRY LVMH — LVMH's Watches & Jewellery arm booked €5.23 billion in first-half sales, up about 9% organically with an 11% second quarter and profit up 9% to €831 million — the group's best division even as overall revenue fell 3% to €38.6 billionBOUCHERON HITS A RECORD — Kering's jewelry houses lifted first-half revenue 14% to €521 million (up 20% comparable) with operating income doubling to €32 million; Boucheron reached record levels on Japan and the Quatre XS launch as group revenue slipped 3%PETRA REVENUE CAVES — Petra Diamonds' fourth-quarter revenue fell 44% to $38 million on weak tender prices and small goods; net debt rose to $322 million, the Finsch mine entered a business-rescue process and July interest was deferred to 2027GEM DIAMONDS LEANS ON GIANTS — Letšeng's high-value output pushed Gem Diamonds' average price up about 17% to roughly $1,501 a carat, led by a 191.82-carat Type IIa white and four stones over $1 million — the opposite result to Petra in the same soft marketRIO'S DIAMONDS WIND DOWN — Rio Tinto's diamond unit posted first-half sales down 27% to $118 million and output down 51% to 1.1 million carats after Diavik's March closure, swinging to a $72 million EBITDA loss with roughly $1 billion of closure costs aheadWHITE METALS COOL — Silver eased to about $57.90 from above $59 on Monday, platinum slipped to roughly $1,597 and palladium to about $1,248, the month's white-metal rally pausing into the Fed even as silver holds a 51% year-on-year gainINDIA'S GOLD SIGNAL SPLITS — The World Gold Council's July read on India shows record prices dividing the market: jewelry buyers trade down and fund purchases with old-gold exchange while bar, coin and fund investment demand stays firmerSMALL STONES STILL FIRM — Rapaport's latest comment kept the recovery in 0.30–0.69ct rounds intact on steady US demand and trimmed inventories, a rare bright line for the near-commercial goods ahead of IIJS Premiere in Mumbai next week
