TWO DUBAI RATES, 2% APART — LivePriceOfGold's Dubai board, timestamped 10 August at 10:07 UTC, reads AED 512.45 a gram for 24-carat gold and AED 470.43 for 22-carat. Goodreturns' Dubai page, dated the same day, reads AED 523.25 and AED 484.50, a gap of 2.11% and 2.99% respectively, both derived by this desk. The peg arithmetic on Kitco spot at $4,341.40 an ounce gives AED 512.59 a gram, which puts LivePriceOfGold on the raw-metal lane, AED 0.14 away, and Goodreturns AED 10.66 above it. Neither page is wrong; they publish different quantities under one label. Goodreturns' page stepped up AED 13.25 between 7 and 8 August against a metal move of AED 3.13 and has held flat for three days sinceTHE EDITOR'S READ IS THE MARK — Under the one-price rule, the tape carries this desk's 5:57am New York reading of Kitco's live board on 10 August: gold $4,341.40, up $0.10 or 0.00% against Friday 7 August's close; silver $63.99, up 52.8 cents or 0.83%; platinum $1,737.00, down $6.00 or 0.34%; palladium $1,340.00, down $17.00 or 1.25%. The 4:58am tape had read $4,345.40, $64.06, $1,739.00 and $1,342.00; every previous close matches, so this is one open session moving between two reads and the fresher verified reading has been written back. Fine gold is $139.58 a gram and the gold-to-silver ratio narrows to 67.84 from 68.415.9 MILLION SILVER PIECES — India hallmarked 5.9 million silver articles in the 2025-26 financial year against 3.2 million the year before, an increase of about 84% derived by this desk, on Bureau of Indian Standards figures disclosed 9 August. About 230 recognised assaying and hallmarking centres handle silver testing; the recognised laboratory network has gone from 147 in 2014 to 440 and government-empanelled laboratories from 24 to 350, against ten standalone bureau laboratories and roughly 270,000 samples tested. Silver hallmarking has been voluntary since 2005 and no mandatory date has been set, though the unique identification number the regime would need was introduced for silver in September 2025. The bureau names the cause as consumers moving into silver because gold has become unaffordableETSY CUTS 220 — Etsy is removing about 220 roles, roughly 12% of its marketplace workforce, mainly from product and engineering, at an estimated $35 million charge booked in the third quarter, leaving about 1,600 employees. The plan was disclosed 6 August alongside second-quarter revenue of $668.3 million, up 6.2% and ahead of a $646.1 million consensus, gross merchandise sales from continuing operations of $2.58 billion, Etsy-only GMS up 7.5%, a take rate of 25.9%, active sellers up 5.9% to 5.7 million and active buyers down 0.4% to 87.0 million. The company says the cut is neither a cost-reduction exercise nor related to artificial intelligence. Jewellery and personal accessories is one of the six largest categories by GMS in Etsy's own filingsQVC OUT IN 113 DAYS — QVC Group formally exited Chapter 11 on 7 August, 113 days after filing on 16 April, a count derived by this desk from the two dates, having removed more than $5 billion of debt. It issued $1.2 billion of take-back notes due 2032 at a 10% coupon and secured a $600 million asset-based lending facility, with the equity approved for Nasdaq trading under QVCG. David Rawlinson, chief executive since 2021, stepped down; Mike George returns as interim chief executive and board chair alongside a new eight-member board. Fine and fashion jewellery has been among the top-selling categories on the platformPANDORA'S THIRD SEAT — Pandora named Paulo Garcia chief financial officer on 6 August. He joins 1 October and takes the role 1 December, succeeding Anders Boyer, who retires 30 November after more than fourteen years and stays until 31 March 2027 to hand over. Garcia arrives from the chief financial officer's seat at Walmart de Mexico y Centroamerica, a listed subsidiary with about $50 billion of revenue and 240,000 employees, and was previously chief financial officer for Europe and Indonesia at Ahold Delhaize and before that at Unilever. With Berta de Pablos-Barbier taking office as chief executive on 1 January and Andre Branch named North America president, three of the top seats at the largest jewellery company in the world by units turn over inside twelve months, and none of the three arrives from jewelleryTHE SEVEN AUGUST SPREAD, RESOLVED — This paper printed three UAE retail gold readings for the 7 August session and declined to choose between them: Khaleej Times at AED 517.50 a gram, one bureau desk at AED 510.25 and another at AED 514.00. The peg arithmetic for that session gives AED 509.42, which places the 510.25 on the raw-metal lane and the 517.50 on the counter lane. The divergence this desk carried unresolved across three editions was a category error rather than a data error.
