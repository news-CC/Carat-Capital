Diamonds

Surat's cutters ask for the 20% their wages lost

The Diamond Worker Union says factory owners cut piece pay through a four-year slowdown and have not restored it. It also expects this year's Diwali break to run 5 to 15 November.

By The Diamonds Desk

10 October 2026

What changed

The union wants the cut reversed. Surat's Diamond Worker Union asked factory owners to restore a 20% cut to cutters' pay. It expects this year's Diwali break to run 5 to 15 November.

What it means

  • If you buy polished from Surat: A shorter closure is a capacity signal, available before any wage settlement.
  • If you employ cutters in Gujarat: The ask is restoration plus a rise, not a strike. No deadline is attached.
  • What this is not: A settlement, or an audited wage. Both figures are the union's.

Orders came back first

Three to four years. Shah describes heavy slowdown conditions across the last three to four years in the diamond trade, and says the position has now changed.

Both markets ordering. He attributes the change to large diamond orders out of international and domestic markets, which he says has restarted movement in the Surat trade.

No figure attached. No order value, carat volume or factory utilisation number is published with the claim. The recovery is described rather than measured.

The break gets shorter

Eleven days expected. The union expects a break from 5 to 15 November, or at most 20 days. The order load will not allow a longer closure.

Twenty to twenty-five. In each of the last two years the break ran 20 to 25 days. Those were declared for lack of work rather than as a holiday.

Forty-five percent shorter. Eleven days is 55.0% of a 20-day break, so 45.0% shorter. The comparison against 25 days is in the table.

The wage ask is separate

Twenty percent off. The union says factory owners cut cutters' pay by about 20% through the downturn, and that workers accepted the lower wage at the time.

Restore, then raise. Its representation is that the recovery should reach cutters: restore the cut and add an increase on top. No employer response is published.

What to watch

  • Before 5 November 2026Whether Surat's factory associations declare the Diwali break and on what dates. The union's expectation is not a notice.
  • After the breakWhether any wage restoration is announced. No deadline, forum or mechanism has been attached to the union's ask.
  • Mid-October 2026GJEPC's monthly export figures, the nearest independent read on whether the order recovery the union describes is showing up in shipments.

What would change this call

An employer or association statement with a payroll number in it, which would move the 20% from a claim to a figure. It would also read differently if the break is declared at the union's own stated maximum of 20 days, which is no shorter than the floor of the last two years and would make the vacation story a non-event while leaving the wage ask untouched.

Why Surat's holiday length is a demand indicator

Surat cuts and polishes on piece rates, and its factories close en masse for Diwali. In a normal year that closure is a holiday with a length set by custom. In a weak year it becomes a capacity valve: a factory with no order book extends the break, pays nothing for the extra days because the work is priced per stone rather than per hour, and avoids laying anybody off. That is why the duration of the Surat Diwali vacation is read in the trade as a demand signal rather than a cultural one, and why a shortening break is treated as the first visible sign of an order recovery — it appears before export figures, before price indices and before any wage settlement. The limitation is who gets to say it. The break is declared by factory owners and their associations, not by the union, so a union's expectation of 11 days is a forecast from the people doing the work rather than a notice from the people scheduling it. The two have diverged before. The wage question sits one step behind: piece rates cut in a downturn do not automatically rise when volume returns, because the rate is negotiated and the volume is not, which is precisely the gap the union is pointing at.
