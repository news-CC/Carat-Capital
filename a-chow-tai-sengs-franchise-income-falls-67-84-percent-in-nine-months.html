Chow Tai Seng's nine-month franchise income falls 67.84% on the year

Franchise income was RMB 1.076bn, 19.27% of revenue against 49.39% a year earlier. Own stores and e-commerce made 78% of revenue in the first nine months, Huaxia Times reports from the filing.

By Carat Capital

8 November 2026

What changed

Franchising shrank to a fifth. Chow Tai Seng's franchise income fell 67.84% on the year to RMB 1.076bn in the first nine months of 2026, Huaxia Times reports. That is about a fifth of revenue.

What it means

  • If you supply Chinese franchisees: franchisees are destocking, the company says, and restock through designated suppliers, Everbright Securities says.
  • If you hold the shares: own stores and e-commerce made 78% of revenue, and both grew more than 30% on the year.
  • What this is not: a third-quarter figure. These are nine-month totals, from a press report of the filing.

Wholesale fell hardest.

Gold wholesale shrank. Gold product sales to franchisees fell 84.32% on the year, to RMB 362m. The company says it actively changed its supply structure, so brand fees rose as a share.

Franchisees pay fees instead. Everbright Securities, in a note of 28 October, says brand fees rose to 48.2% of franchise income in the first half, from 15.2%.

Own stores and online gained share.

Both rose over 30%. Own-store income rose 31.46% and e-commerce 33.42% on the year. Together they made 78% of revenue, Huaxia Times reports.

A net 670 fewer stores.

Net 670 fewer. The store count ended September at 3,809, a net 670 fewer than at the start of the year.

Net franchise closures. Franchised stores fell by a net 657 to 3,424.

Small-city shops, it says. The company told investors on 28 October that closures are mainly unprofitable street shops in lower-tier cities and counties.

What to watch

  • By 30 April 2027The 2026 annual report. The test: whether franchise income keeps falling as brand fees grow.
  • MonthlyShenzhen and Shanghai counter prices for 24-carat gold. The test: whether a lower gold price brings franchisees back to buying stock.

What would change this call

Franchisees buying stock directly again. If gold prices settle and wholesale income returns, the fall reads as a gold-price effect, not a change of model.

How a franchise earns for Chow Tai Seng

Franchise income has two parts: product wholesale, where the company sells stock to franchisees, and brand-use fees. When franchisees restock through designated suppliers, the company collects only the fee, so its income falls even if counters keep selling, Everbright Securities says.
