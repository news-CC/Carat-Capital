Watches

F.P. Journe's HK$31,400,000 tops a HK$426m Sotheby's sale

The Cartier Cheich that led Sotheby's own announcement set a world record and placed second by price. Four of the sale's top six lots are F.P. Journe. The sale ran in Hong Kong on 21 September.

By The Watches Desk

6 October 2026

What changed

The record was not the top lot. An F.P. Journe Tourbillon Souverain took HK$31,400,000 at Sotheby's Hong Kong sale, 18.4% more than the Cartier Cheich the house led its announcement with.

What it means

  • If you consign independent watchmaking: Four of the top six lots were F.P. Journe, and the highest price in the room was not a listed group's brand.
  • If you read house announcements for price: The record watch and the dearest watch were different lots here. Check the lot table, not the headline.
  • If you valued a Cartier Cheich: HK$26,520,000 is 3.32 times the low estimate and 33.7% below the high.
  • What this is not: A current reading. The sale is 15 days old and nothing since has been measured against it here.

The dearest lot set no record

Eighteen percent apart. Lot 2400 took HK$31,400,000 and the Cheich HK$26,520,000. The division gives 1.184, so 18.4% more.

Different headline. Sotheby's leads its recap on the record rather than on the highest price realised in the room.

Four of six are Journe

Independent at the top. Lots 2400, 2579, 2387 and 2414 are all F.P. Journe, four of the six highest by the recap's own ordering.

Groups take the rest. Patek Philippe holds three of the top ten and Audemars Piguet one, by the same table.

Doubled, and under its high

A wide band. The Cheich was estimated at HK$8 million to HK$40 million. It realised HK$26,520,000.

Print the division. 26,520,000 ÷ 8,000,000 = 3.32 times the low; 26,520,000 ÷ 40,000,000 = 66.3% of the high, so 33.7% below it.

One document, two durations

Nine or sixteen. The recap's body calls the Cheich's contest a nine-minute bidding battle. A quoted comment in the same document says sixteen minutes.

Neither is printed. No second source carries either figure, so no duration appears in the figures above or the table.

What to watch

  • October to November 2026Christie's and Phillips Hong Kong and Geneva watch sales. The first comparable totals to set against HK$426m.
  • Next Cartier Cheich at auctionWhether HK$26,520,000 holds as the Cartier wristwatch record. The recap says the prior record was set in May 2026.
  • May 2027The anniversary of the record this one displaced, and the usual window for the house's Hong Kong luxury week.

What would change this call

A corrected recap. Two internal inconsistencies were found in this one document — two bidding durations and a currency label — and a reissued table could change the lot ordering printed here.

What a sale total counts

A house total is the sum of hammer prices plus the buyer's premium on each sold lot. It excludes lots that failed to sell, so a high total can coexist with a weak sell-through rate. Sotheby's recap for this sale publishes no sell-through percentage and none is estimated here. Currency conversions are the house's own, struck at the rate on the day of sale, which is why the HK$ and US$ columns do not move together across lots.
