Watches

Citizen and Seiko raise watch sales forecasts to ¥242.0bn and ¥236.0bn

Both forecasts cover the full year to March 2027. Citizen's watch operating profit rose 91.1% to ¥22.4 billion in the half to September, on refunds of additional US tariffs and higher net sales.

By Carat Capital

14 November 2026

What changed

Two full-year forecasts raised. Citizen raised its full-year watch sales forecast to ¥242.0 billion on 12 November. Seiko raised its own to ¥236.0 billion on 10 November.

What it means

  • If you stock Japanese watches: Citizen credits North America; Seiko credits its global brands at home and overseas.
  • If you compare margins: Citizen's watch profit includes refunds of additional US tariffs; ¥3.6 billion cut group cost of sales.
  • What this is not: the whole group. Half-year group operating profit was ¥29,228 million at Citizen and ¥31,429 million at Seiko.

Citizen: refunds and net sales.

Sales up 31.0%. Citizen's watch net sales rose 31.0% year on year to ¥117.0 billion in the six months to 30 September.

Profit up 91.1%. Watch operating profit rose 91.1% year on year to ¥22.4 billion.

Refunds, then net sales. Citizen attributes the profit rise to refunds for additional US tariffs, in addition to higher net sales. It also raised its watch profit forecast for the year to March 2027 to ¥40.0 billion.

Seiko: global brands carried it.

Sales about 24.6% higher. Seiko's watches business sold ¥122.4 billion in the half, against ¥98.2 billion. The 24.6% is Carat Capital's division.

Profit ¥26.4 billion. Watch operating profit was ¥26.4 billion, against ¥15.4 billion. Seiko credits Grand Seiko, Prospex, Presage and Astron, at home and overseas.

Profit forecast ¥39.0 billion. Seiko raised its full-year watch profit forecast to ¥39.0 billion from ¥35.0 billion.

What to watch

  • Year to 31 March 2027Each maker's second six months. The test: whether watch profit holds where no tariff refund is booked.

What would change this call

If Citizen's second half books no refunds and watch profit falls back, the 91.1% will read as a one-off.

Reported yen, not constant rates

The headline figures are in yen at each period's exchange rates; Seiko also gives some watch growth rates at constant currency. Citizen assumes 155 yen to the dollar from the third quarter.
