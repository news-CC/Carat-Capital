LBMA adds Fujian Jinyudeshang Refinery to its gold Good Delivery List

The listing took effect on 9 November. LBMA says the refiner's main feedstock is jewellery-trade material and old jewellery bought back from jewellers, and its list now includes 68 gold refiners.

By Carat Capital

15 November 2026

What changed

A Fujian refiner is in. Fujian Jinyudeshang Refinery Technology has joined the LBMA's gold Good Delivery List with effect from 9 November 2026, the association said.

What it means

  • If you buy kilobars from China: check the refiner against the LBMA list itself, not against a supplier's word.
  • If you sell old gold: LBMA says this refiner's main feedstock is jewellery-trade material and old jewellery bought back from jewellers.
  • What this is not: a capacity figure. LBMA gives no tonnage, output or price for the refiner.

The tests it passed.

Four checks, then assay. LBMA says the refiner satisfied it on ownership, history, production capability and financial standing. Independent referees assayed its bars, and its own assaying was tested.

Founded in 2009. The company was founded in September 2009 and refines precious metals and produces gold. In 2023 it gained Shanghai Gold Exchange standard gold ingot status, LBMA says.

Jewellery is the feedstock.

Old jewellery goes in. Its main source is material from clients that process and sell gold jewellery, plus old and damaged jewellery bought back from jewellers, per LBMA.

List includes 68 gold. The list now includes 68 gold and 86 silver refiners, LBMA says. Our 22 August read counted 67 gold refiners.

What to watch

  • Each LBMA noticeAdditions and suspensions take effect on the dates LBMA names. The test: whether Chinese refiners keep moving both ways, as in August.

What would change this call

If LBMA suspends the refiner, as it did Shandong Gold Smelting from 5 August, its bars stop counting as Good Delivery.

The Good Delivery List

LBMA's list of refiners whose gold and silver bars met the London market's standard when first tested. Bars from listed refiners are accepted in London settlement; LBMA maintains the list.
