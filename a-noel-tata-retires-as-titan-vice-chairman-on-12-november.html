Noel Tata retires as Titan vice-chairman on 12 November

Titan's board also approved Price Waterhouse as auditor for five years from the 2027 annual meeting, after B S R & Co.'s second term. Shareholders must still approve the appointment.

By Carat Capital

9 November 2026

What changed

Two board decisions. Titan told the exchanges on 5 November that Noel N. Tata retires as a director and its vice-chairman from 12 November, and that Price Waterhouse becomes its auditor in 2027.

What it means

  • If you hold Titan shares: the auditor change needs shareholders' approval; B S R & Co. audits the company and its subsidiaries until the 2027 annual meeting.
  • If you follow Tata governance: Titan gives only the Tata Group Governance Guidelines as the reason.
  • What this is not: a trading update. Neither letter carries a sales or profit figure.

Tata cites group guidelines.

Retirement from 12 November. The letter gives the Tata Group Governance Guidelines as the reason for the retirement, and no other. It names Tata as a non-executive, non-independent director.

Both letters, one day. General Counsel and Company Secretary Dinesh Shetty signed both letters to NSE and BSE on 5 November.

Price Waterhouse from 2027.

A vote still needed. The board approved Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP on the audit committee's recommendation, from the 43rd to the 48th annual meeting. Shareholders must still approve.

Two terms completed. B S R & Co. LLP stays on until the 2027 meeting, which completes its second consecutive five-year term, Titan says.

What to watch

  • 12 November 2026Tata's retirement takes effect. The test: whether Titan names a new vice-chairman in a further filing.
  • Titan's 2027 annual general meetingShareholders vote on Price Waterhouse. The test: whether the appointment passes as the board proposed.

What would change this call

If Titan names a different reason for the retirement in a later filing, or shareholders reject the auditor, these letters would read as the start of a contested change rather than a scheduled one.

How an Indian listed company reports a board change

Regulation 30 of SEBI's listing rules requires a listed company to tell the exchanges of a director's departure or an auditor's appointment, with an annexure giving the reason and the terms. Titan's two letters each carry that annexure.
