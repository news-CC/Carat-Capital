Senco Gold quarterly revenue up 34%; release gives two profit figures

Revenue reached ₹2,058.7 crore in the quarter to September. The results table puts profit after tax at ₹83.5 crore; the chief executive's commentary in the same release says ₹86.7 crore.

By Carat Capital

14 November 2026

What changed

Revenue up, profit unclear. Senco Gold's revenue rose 34% to ₹2,058.7 crore in the quarter to September. Its release gives two different figures for quarterly profit after tax.

What it means

  • If you track Indian jewellers: Senco credits the festive calendar: four days of Pitru Paksha fell in the quarter, against 15.
  • If you lend or supply gold: inventory rose to ₹5,487.8 crore and borrowings to ₹2,612.7 crore ahead of the festive season.
  • What this is not: a settled profit figure. The statutory results will decide which is right.

Source: Senco Gold press release, 14 November 2026 · consolidated · quarter to 30 September 2026 (Q2 FY27) · ₹ crore · statutory results not read

Revenue rose on festivals and stores.

Half up 52%. Revenue was ₹2,058.7 crore in the quarter. Half-year revenue rose 52% to ₹5,114.8 crore.

Same-store sales up 19%. Same-store sales grew 19% in the quarter, Senco says. It added a net 14 showrooms in the half, to 215.

Two quarterly profit figures, one release.

Table says ₹83.5 crore. The results table gives quarterly profit after tax of ₹83.5 crore. The chief executive's commentary gives ₹86.7 crore.

Bullet says up 78%. A summary bullet says profit grew 78%. That fits ₹86.7 crore against ₹48.8 crore a year earlier, on Carat Capital's division.

Half-year fits ₹86.7 crore. The first quarter's ₹101.1 crore plus ₹86.7 crore makes the half-year column's ₹187.8 crore.

EBIT sits above EBITDA.

Table: EBITDA down 15%. The table puts quarterly EBITDA at ₹90.2 crore, below EBIT of ₹190.0 crore.

Columns do not add. First-quarter EBITDA plus the second quarter's makes ₹303.3 crore, against a half-year column of ₹391.2 crore.

What to watch

  • Senco's statutory results for the quarter to 30 September 2026The filed profit after tax. The test: whether it reads ₹83.5 crore or ₹86.7 crore.
  • Second half, to 31 March 2027Senco's target of over 25% value growth and a 7.5% EBITDA margin. The test: whether the margin recovers from 4.4% in the second quarter.

What would change this call

If the statutory results confirm ₹86.7 crore and restate the table, the discrepancy is a typing error, and profit grew 78%.

EBIT above EBITDA

EBITDA is earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. EBIT is normally the smaller of the two. Here it is larger, which only income outside EBITDA, such as other income, can explain; the release does not say.
