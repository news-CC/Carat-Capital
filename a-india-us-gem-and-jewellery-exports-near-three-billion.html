India's US gem and jewellery exports near $3 billion, GJEPC says

Exports to the US rose nearly 6% in April to September from US$2.77 billion a year earlier, GJEPC's chairman said on 23 October. They had fallen more than 44% in April to December 2025.

By Carat Capital

25 October 2026

What changed

The US came back. India's gem and jewellery exports to the US rose nearly 6% to almost $3 billion in April to September, GJEPC chairman Kirit Bhansali said on 23 October.

What it means

  • If you buy for US stores: GJEPC is pitching for holiday and 2027 orders.
  • If you export to the US: The chairman looks to a proposed India-US trade deal for clarity; it is not final.
  • What this is not: The old level. The US took nearly $10 billion in FY2024-25; six months at almost $3 billion is well under half.

Source: GJEPC release on the India-USA Virtual Exhibition of 23 October 2026, giving chairman Kirit Bhansali's figures. Rounded as he gave them; GJEPC publishes no table with them.

Six months after the fall

Down 44% first. Exports to the US fell more than 44% in April to December 2025, Bhansali said, as tariff uncertainty hit trade.

Up 6% now. April to September 2026 came in at almost $3 billion, against $2.77 billion a year earlier.

A pitch for 2027 orders

Thirty-five exporters online. India's Consulate General in New York and GJEPC ran a virtual exhibition for 35 Indian companies on 23 October. Many are small firms.

A trade deal pending. Bhansali said the proposed India-US Bilateral Trade Agreement could bring clarity for exporters and buyers once its terms are final.

  • Mid-November 2026GJEPC's October export statement, the first month of the US holiday shipping season.

What would change this call

A GJEPC country table that puts the six months well under $2.94 billion. Then 'almost $3 billion' was rounded generously.

How the figure is reported

The numbers are the chairman's, given in a speech and carried in GJEPC's release. They cover all gem and jewellery exports to the US. The release gives no product split and no table.
