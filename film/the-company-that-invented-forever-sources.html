Simplification ledger

  • 'One room in London': the CSO was an organisation, not literally one room; 17 Charterhouse Street opened in 1979. The sights were held in London throughout.
  • 'Four of every five stones': the ~80% share is the second-half-of-century average; peak estimates run 85–90% and the share was ~two-thirds by 2000.
  • 'The county that grew chillies': Zhecheng was mainly known for chillies and cattle four decades ago; it now leads Chinese synthetic output. Its ~$10/ct floor figure is Golan's, for HPHT rough, and is attributed.
  • 'Roughly a fifteenth': compares the 2001 whole-company valuation with the implied whole from Bloomberg's reported price for 85%.
  • 'Half of the mines that produce most of its stones': Debswana (50/50) produces the large majority of De Beers' carats; Namibia and Canada supply the rest.
  • 'The largest ever dug by hand': the Big Hole's claim is a period/heritage claim, stated as 'said at the time'.

Figures declined

  • Anglo's '$4.9B carrying value after $3.5B of write-downs' (Carat Capital 17–18 July) conflicts with the verified impairment sequence ($9.2B → $2.3B); the verified sequence is used.
  • The 1980s D-flawless bottom (~$9,000) — forum-sourced, not used.
  • 'One month's salary' as an early campaign rule — used only with the label 'by the industry's own telling'.
