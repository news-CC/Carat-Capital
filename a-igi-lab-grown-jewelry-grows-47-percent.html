IGI's lab-grown jewelry line grows 47%, natural jewelry 3%

Revenue at the Mumbai-listed grader rose 31% to INR 3,980 million in the July to September quarter. Profit after tax rose 43% to INR 1,858 million on 4.35 million reports.

By Carat Capital

26 October 2026

What changed

Lab-grown jewelry led. IGI says its lab-grown jewelry segment grew 47% on a year in the quarter to 30 September. Natural diamond jewelry grew 3%.

What it means

  • If you sell lab-grown jewelry: Certified lab-grown pieces are IGI's fastest-growing segment, ahead of loose lab-grown stones at 37%.
  • If you trade natural goods: Natural loose stones grew 8% at the same lab, faster than natural jewelry.
  • What this is not: A price signal. IGI does not say whether its segment growth counts fees or reports.

Key figures

Quarter revenue+31% on a yearINR 3,980m
Profit after tax+43% on a yearINR 1,858m
EBITDA margin58.0% a year earlier61.4%
Reports issued+26% on a year4.35m

Source: IGI press release filed with NSE and BSE, 21 October 2026. Quarter to 30 September 2026 (Q2 FY27), consolidated, against Q2 FY26.

Profit outran revenue.

Revenue rose 31%. Quarter revenue rose 31% to INR 3,980 million, from INR 3,037 million a year earlier.

Profit rose 43%. Profit after tax rose 43% to INR 1,858 million.

Margins widened. EBITDA rose 39% to INR 2,445 million, a margin of 61.4%.

The half and the quarter.

The half held. For April to September, revenue rose 27% to INR 7,688 million.

Quarter on quarter. Subtracting the second quarter from the half leaves INR 3,708 million for April to June. The July quarter was 7.3% higher.

Reports rose 26%. IGI issued 4.35 million grading reports in the quarter.

Gemstones multiplied, with AGL.

Seven times. IGI says coloured gemstones grew sevenfold on a year, a figure that includes AGL. It gives no rupee amount for the line.

The chief's read. Managing director Tehmasp Printer says lab-grown diamonds have started to see higher adoption. He calls natural diamonds and gemstones a key growth lever.

What to watch

  • November to December 2026India's festive season and the US holiday ordering cycle, which IGI's chief says are ramping up.
  • Quarter to 31 December 2026IGI's next results. The test is whether lab-grown jewelry keeps outgrowing natural jewelry.

What would change this call

A quarter in which IGI's natural jewelry segment outgrows its lab-grown jewelry segment would undo the split this quarter shows.

What IGI's figures count

IGI grades and certifies stones and jewelry, so its revenue is fees, not sales of goods. It calls itself India's largest independent certifier, with a 50% market share, and runs 37 laboratories in 10 countries.
