=== POST 1: worlds-longest-gold-bar-sharjah ===
Sharjah's new gold bar measures 2.12 metres and weighs 30 kilograms, Guinness-certified.

It went on display at the 58th Watch and Jewellery Middle East Show, Expo Centre Sharjah, to 4 October. Its value, over AED 15 million, is for the record only — not for sale. Filed by Carat Capital's Gulf bureau. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: twenty-three-thirty-four-tops-hong-kong ===
A 23.34-carat yellow diamond sold for HKD 4.3 million at Phillips Hong Kong.

The top lot met its high estimate. Two other top-five lots, a ruby and a brown-yellow diamond, sold under their low estimate, Rapaport's tally of the 29 September sale shows. The sale made HKD 37.2 million from 91 lots. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
