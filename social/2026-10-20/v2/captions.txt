=== POST 1: the-flawless-diamond-missed-its-floor ===
A flawless 8.17-carat diamond missed its own floor at Christie's — two Bulgari pieces more than doubled theirs.

The D/IF round sold for $243,200 against a $250,000 low estimate, without reserve. A Bulgari necklace and earring pair made 2.56 and 3.66 times their high estimates, Rapaport reports.

=== POST 2: ninety-one-percent-more-diamonds ===
De Beers' third-quarter rough sales volume rose 91% to 8.7 million carats, while its price index fell to 65 from 81 a year ago.

Production fell 11% to 6.8 million carats as Orapa and Venetia slowed. De Beers says its $84-a-carat average mostly reflects sales mix, not the index.
