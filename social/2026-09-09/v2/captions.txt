=== POST 1: six-banks-one-exit ===
Shanghai Pudong Development Bank will stop acting as agent for individual Shanghai Gold Exchange trading after 25 September, the sixth Chinese bank to exit the same channel in 2026. ICBC and China Construction Bank closed it on 24 July; Postal Savings, Ping An and Guangfa went earlier in the year. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
