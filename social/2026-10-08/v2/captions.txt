=== POST 1: feds-charge-six-men-in-jewelry-smash-and-grabs ===
Federal prosecutors charged six New Jersey men in a string of jewelry-store smash-and-grabs, facing up to 20 years each.

DNA from broken glass at a May 2025 Paterson robbery named two of the six: Shaquan Jones and Kayron Caldwell. One robbery reached Staten Island, New York.

=== POST 2: fifty-million-for-elizabeth-bay ===
Namibia's diamond exports fell 67% in three years. A $50 million investment just restarted one of its mines anyway.

Kenzoll Capital and Namibian-registered MSF Commercials will revive Elizabeth Bay, holding 10.5 million carats onshore and offshore. De Beers sold the mine in 2019, unable to run it profitably.
