=== POST 1: pigeon-blood-reaches-jaipur ===
Jaipur cuts the world's finest rubies, and until Rapaport's report on 19 August, its own labs could not put 'pigeon's blood' on the report. Gemological Science International now issues the ruby trade's most contested colour call from Jaipur, Mumbai, New York and Bangkok — untreated stones only.
