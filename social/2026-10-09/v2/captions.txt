=== POST 1: two-minutes-thirty-million-in-gold ===
Hong Kong police recovered HK$30 million in gold after a two-minute swap for fabric softener. Two suspects, aged 27 and 41, were arrested in Tai Po and at Lo Wu border crossing. Police believe the alleged mastermind, a friend of the victim, has left Hong Kong.

=== POST 2: sixty-six-five-for-ninety-five-of-ebel ===
Movado sold 95% of its EBEL brand to a buyer group for $66.5 million. Montres Journe SA leads the group, with Chanel and Pierre Jacques, who becomes chief executive of the new EBEL entity. Movado retains a 5% stake; the sale carries EBEL's trademarks and the Villa Turque, a Le Corbusier house.
