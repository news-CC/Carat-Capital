=== POST 1: a-shop-caught-its-own-thieves ===
Two former Kanz Aldera staff allegedly swapped 470 grams of gold for fakes, then tried to extort the manager who caught them.

Mumbai's Crime Branch arrested Barman and Pathar on manager Devendra Dhanak's extortion complaint, Free Press Journal reports. Both remanded; neither has responded publicly.

=== POST 2: gjc-claims-a-record-and-prints-no-figure ===
GJC called its 10th gem show a record. It never said what the record was.

The show closed 4 October in Mumbai with 400+ exhibitors and 700+ booths, matching the pre-show release. The promised visitor and buyer counts never reappeared, and chairman Rajesh Rokde's "record sales" claim carries no figure.
