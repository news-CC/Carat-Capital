=== POST 1: silence-costs-more-than-treatment ===
A coloured gemstone's price can run ten times higher or lower on four words in its lab report: 'no indications of heating.' Most rubies, sapphires and emeralds are heated or filled to improve their look — the trade's issue has never been the treatment, only hiding it. A documented origin adds its own premium.
