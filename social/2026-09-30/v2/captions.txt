Four Daytonas sold for $49.5 million in London.

Four one-off Rolex Daytonas made a combined £37.3 million ($49.5 million) at Phillips on 28 September, benefiting the Federer Foundation and Re:wild. The set was conceived as a private sale at about $4.9 million — ten times less than the result. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: one-paisa-above-the-gold ===
Tamil Nadu orders 441,667 gold rings for newborns.

Joy Alukkas won the state tender at one paisa a ring above the cost of gold, Carat Capital's reading of the government's own release. The first work order of 128,499 rings splits 70:30 with Kalyan Jewellers, against an annual budget of ₹755.83 crore. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
