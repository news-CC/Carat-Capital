=== POST 1: one-crore-a-kilo-buys-no-certificate ===
A Bhuj agent quoted Divya Bhaskar's investigation team ₹1 crore for one kilo of 24-carat gold, no bill attached.

The bars shown were marked Metalor and PAMP; payment was to run by hawala from Jamnagar into a Bhuj angadia, collected against an Aadhaar card. No gold was bought and none has been assayed.

=== POST 2: gold-vanished-for-ten-months ===
A Wadaj workshop returned three jobs on time. The ₹87.81 lakh job after never came back.

Sanjay Shah of Labdhi Ornaments gave 646.470 grams in 17 sets on 22 December; two days later the phones were off, the shop empty. He filed a complaint; no arrest or recovery is reported.
