=== POST 1: surat-owner-vanishes-with-ten-lakh-owed ===
Marutinandan Diamonds, a cutting and polishing unit in Surat's Varachha area, has shut with ₹10 lakh of workers' wages unpaid, its union says.

Owner Kiritbhai Vagadiya paid ₹4.50 lakh in early October, promised the rest by 19 October, then stopped answering, the Diamond Workers Union Gujarat says. The union asked police on 22 October to register a cheating complaint against him. 20 of 55 polishers have reached the union so far.

=== POST 2: rolex-slips-while-vacheron-and-patek-climb ===
Chrono24's watch price index rose 0.7% in the third quarter to 1,471, Haute Time reports, even as Rolex slipped 0.2% in the same quarter.

Vacheron Constantin rose 6.7% in the quarter and 17.7% over twelve months, narrowly ahead of Patek Philippe's 17.0%. Hublot was the only tracked brand still down over the year, the report says.
