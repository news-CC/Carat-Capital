=== POST 1: france-and-botswana-say-diamond-means-natural ===
France and Botswana just told lab-grown diamond makers their stones aren't diamonds anymore.

Nicolas Forissier and Bogolo Joy Kenewendo signed the Paris Declaration on Natural Diamonds on 10 November, agreeing the word "diamond" should mean a natural stone only. Botswana's President Duma Boko, who proposed it, wants lab-grown stones called synthetic instead; a lab-grown makers' coalition calls him biased.

=== POST 2: sothebys-says-romanov-sapphire-sold-for-over-chf-4-1m ===
A Romanov sapphire bracelet sold for more than CHF 4.1 million at Sotheby's Geneva, AP reports.

The 197.80-carat stone, once worn by Dowager Empress Maria Feodorovna, had been estimated at CHF 850,000 to 1.7 million. Sotheby's own sale page showed no price as of 13 November, and neither source says whether the figure includes the buyer's premium.
