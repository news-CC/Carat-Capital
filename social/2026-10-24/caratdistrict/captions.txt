=== POST 1: the-rabbi-stabbed-on-47th-street-came-back ===
New York: The rabbi stabbed on 47th Street Wednesday was back on the same block the next day.

Yosef, 26, a Crown Heights Chabad emissary, resumed his daily rounds Thursday, helping Diamond District merchants put on tefillin, as he has done for years.

NYPD's Hate Crimes Task Force is still hunting the Russian-speaking suspect, who also pursued a second Jewish man first.
