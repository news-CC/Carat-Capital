=== POST 1: titans-profit-rose-53-percent-18-without-one-gain ===
Titan's Q2 profit before tax rose 53% to about ₹2,329 crore, but excluding a ₹536 crore customs-duty gain it rose 18% to about ₹1,793 crore. Total income rose 25% to about ₹20,533 crore; India jewellery income, including CaratLane, rose 21%, with CaratLane itself up 31%, and international jewellery, which includes Damas, grew 105%. === POST 2: monaco-legend-sells-through-at-100-percent-on-a-smaller-total ===
Monaco Legend Group's November watch sale made €22.26m and a 100% sell-through rate, the house's own figure — its April sale, bigger, made €26.47m across 288 lots at 98.3% sold. The house does not say whether its rate counts lots or value, and gives no lot count for November. A steel Rolex 6062, bought new in 1955, led at €1,858,000; a Patek Philippe 2499/100 made €1,424,000. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
