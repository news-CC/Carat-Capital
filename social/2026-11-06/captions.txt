=== POST 1: titans-profit-rose-53-percent-18-without-one-gain ===
Titan's Q2 profit before tax rose 53% to about ₹2,329 crore, but excluding a ₹536 crore customs-duty gain it rose 18% to about ₹1,793 crore. Total income rose 25% to about ₹20,533 crore; India jewellery income, including CaratLane, rose 21%, with CaratLane itself up 31%, and international jewellery, which includes Damas, grew 105%. === POST 2: letseng-sold-fewer-carats-for-73-percent-more ===
Gem Diamonds' Letšeng mine sold 21.6% fewer carats in the third quarter, at 17,899, but the average price per carat rose 73% to $1,945, lifting revenue 35% to $34.8m. Four diamonds over $1m each brought in $22.2m, including the 347-carat Lesotho Jubilee, and Gem Diamonds raised its 2026 guidance for carats recovered to 78,000-80,000 from 75,000-78,000.
