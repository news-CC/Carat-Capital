=== POST 1: vnd-5-million-a-day-the-certificate-ends ===
Vietnam now requires non-cash payment for gold purchases of VND 5 million or more a day, down from VND 20 million, and jewellery makers no longer need a central bank production certificate.

Decree 390/2026 took effect 16 October; certificate applications still pending that day are terminated outright, and foreign-contract makers may now seek raw-gold import licences.

=== POST 2: shareholders-ratified-deals-already-closed ===
Burgundy Diamond Mines' board says three 2025 rough-sale agreements worth about US$50.6 million went to a company controlled by its own director, without shareholder approval first.

Non-associated shareholders ratified the dealings on 30 April 2026, a year after they closed; Burgundy's shares have been suspended since 29 September 2025.
