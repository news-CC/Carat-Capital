=== POST 1: legal-gold-fell-smuggled-gold-rose ===
Legal gold fell 29% in India's Q3. Smuggled gold rose more than 1,500%, to 50 tonnes, the World Gold Council says.

Jewellery demand -20% to 99.9t, net imports -46% to 114.1t. India more than doubled its gold import duty to 15% this year; the WGC says higher tax pushes traders to unofficial channels.

=== POST 2: petra-buyer-the-release-wont-name ===
Petra Diamonds' Cullinan mine has signed a deal for at least $20m against future output; the buyer goes unnamed. Petra's senior lender will hold off enforcement for up to 12 months.

Q1 revenue fell to $21m at $63 a carat, down from $30m and $76. Net debt rose to $348m. Euronext Dublin suspended Petra's 2030 notes on 2 Nov, following its shares.
