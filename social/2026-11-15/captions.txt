=== POST 1: surats-wage-cut-outlasts-the-slowdown ===
Surat's diamond cutters took a 20% pay cut during a years-long slowdown, and the Diamond Worker Union says it has not been restored even as new orders return. The union's own account: this year's Diwali break is expected at 5 to 15 November, 11 days, against 20 to 25 days declared for lack of work in each of the last two years.
