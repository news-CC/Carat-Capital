=== POST 1: the-ring-carries-twelve-million ===
Christie's will offer an 8.41-carat fancy vivid purple-pink diamond ring at up to HK$95 million ($12 million) in Hong Kong this November. The ring prices at HK$11.3 million a carat, nearly double the sale's Cartier ruby brooch. Both lots publish HK$155 million ($19.7 million); neither has a low estimate yet.
