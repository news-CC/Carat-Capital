=== POST 1: gemfields-doubles-the-writedown ===
Gemfields has revised its own 2025 impairment up to $65.0 million from $35.0 million, without stating a reason. The company expects a $73.5 million net loss for the six months to 30 June, after a $125.2 million impairment at Montepuez. Auction revenue still rose 71% to $102.8 million; results are due 30 September. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: jammers-silence-the-alarm ===
A Glendale, California jeweler lost more than $1 million to burglars whose radio jammer kept the alarm silent while it stayed online. The Jewelers' Security Alliance counted violence in 27.1% of 2025 jewelry crimes, up from 16.9% the year before. A detector exists, launched June 2025, and costs a few hundred dollars. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
