=== POST 1: botswana-says-30-anglo-says-not-signed ===
Botswana's president says its De Beers stake would double to 30%, from 15% today. Duma Boko told parliament on 9 November the stake would rise once a Penny-led consortium buys Anglo American's shares. Anglo American says it would keep working toward binding transaction documents; AP reports the deal is not finalised. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: phillips-top-lot-hammers-18-9-percent-below-estimate ===
A 16.40-carat Kashmir sapphire ring hammered 18.9% below its low estimate at Phillips in Geneva. The ring sold for CHF 1,935,000 with premium on a CHF 1,500,000 hammer, 9 November; Phillips' Geneva Jewels Auction VII totalled CHF 8,069,595, with 91 of 125 lots sold. SSEF and Gübelin reports state Kashmir origin with no heat; a Bucherer diamond ring was the sale's second-highest lot, doubling its estimate. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
