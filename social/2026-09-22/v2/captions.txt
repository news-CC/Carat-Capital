=== POST 1: imports-fell-etfs-added-anyway ===
India's gold imports fell 45% to $2.3 billion in August, World Gold Council data show. Domestic prices traded below import parity, with local supply covering demand. Indian ETF holdings still rose to 121.3 tonnes, as the import discount widened from $34 an ounce in July to $78 on 11 September. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: first-gem-export-fall-in-five-months ===
Thailand's gem and jewellery exports fell 6.36% in July, ending four months of gains, GIT data show. Unwrought gold exports fell far more, down 59.22% to $411.04m, tied to a fifth month of easing gold prices. The seven-month total excluding gold still stands 9.87% ahead of last year, at $8,981.12m.
