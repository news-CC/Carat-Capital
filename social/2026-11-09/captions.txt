=== POST 1: chow-tai-sengs-franchise-income-collapses ===
Chow Tai Seng's franchise income fell 67.84% year-on-year in the first nine months of 2026, to RMB 1.076bn — while its own shops and e-commerce grew over 30%. The company says it deliberately shifted toward brand fees, which Everbright Securities says rose to 48.2% of franchise income in the first half, from 15.2%. Store count fell a net 670 to 3,809.

=== POST 2: cartiers-sister-clock-sold-22-5-percent-less ===
Cartier's Portico Mystery Clock No. 4 sold for CHF 4.8 million at Phillips in Geneva, 22.5% more than its sister clock made in May 2025. The hammer was 2.2 times the low estimate; Phillips calls it a world auction record for any Cartier timepiece. It is the only one of six Portico clocks with a hexagonal dial, last offered in November 1996.
