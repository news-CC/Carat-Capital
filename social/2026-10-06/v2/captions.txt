$438,000: a Diamond District dealer's four-year forged-check scheme ends in a guilty plea.

Arsen Canaj paid sellers with forged checks for a diamond, a Rolex and jewelry and emeralds, 2018-2021, the Manhattan DA's office said. He pleaded guilty to Grand Larceny; sentencing is 13 November.
