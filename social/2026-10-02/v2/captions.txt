=== POST 1: eighteen-jewels-and-no-watch ===
Audemars Piguet's Royal Oak Joaillerie has 18 pieces, and not one is a watch.

It opened at Paris Fashion Week on 1 October in gold and titanium, carrying the Royal Oak's bracelet links into jewelry. One cuff alone sets 506 diamonds; no retail price was published. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: boodles-takes-the-argyle-brand ===
Rio Tinto sold its Argyle Pink Diamonds brand to Boodles, a 228-year-old jeweller, with no price disclosed.

Boodles also acquired the last remaining Argyle pink-diamond inventory, Rio Tinto's release says. The mine, once over 90% of world pink supply, stopped producing in November 2020 and stays closed. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
