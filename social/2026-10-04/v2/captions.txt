=== POST 1: premium-adds-twenty-to-twenty-seven ===
A buyer's premium adds 20 to 27% on top of every hammer price at auction. Estimates exclude it, which is why the number in a sale announcement is rarely the number paid. Sotheby's tested the alternative in July: eleven lots, zero premium, the sixth run of its Gem Drop format.
