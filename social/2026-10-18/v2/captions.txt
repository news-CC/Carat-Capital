=== POST 1: what-a-sightholder-buys ===
De Beers sells rough diamonds to approved buyers, called sightholders, ten times a year in a system known as the Sight — the producer sets the price, not the buyer. Ramat Gan's bourse just ran a rare De Beers tender instead, its first in nine years, while Richemont, Signet and Botswana are all now linked to who ends up controlling it.
