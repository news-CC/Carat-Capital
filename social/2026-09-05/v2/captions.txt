=== POST 1: one-seventy-nine-became-five ===
The SEC says Lugano Diamonds and its former CEO booked over $1 billion in fictitious revenue, and a restatement cut the company's stated assets from $179 million to $5 million. The complaint, filed 31 August in California, covers conduct by Mordechai Ferder from 2021 to 2025: investor money meant for diamonds was recorded as sales instead. Ferder's lawyer says he denies the allegations. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: only-thursday-rose ===
Gold, silver, platinum and palladium all closed the week lower, and palladium's 2.63% weekly fall ran roughly 4.6 times gold's 0.57% drop. Thursday was the only session where all four metals rose; every other session saw gold fall, from Friday 28 August's close (gold $4,454.40, silver $66.253, platinum $1,819.00, palladium $1,405.00) to Friday 4 September's final close (gold $4,429.10, silver $66.091, platinum $1,816.00, palladium $1,368.00).
