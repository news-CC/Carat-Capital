=== POST 1: one-billion-pula-lands-one-tenth-of-the-pledge ===
De Beers has paid 1 billion pula — about $73 million by JCK's conversion — into Botswana's new Diamonds for Development Fund, its first contribution under the 2023 deal.

It is one-tenth of the P10 billion pledge over ten years; later payments move with De Beers' sales. Diamonds are 62.3% of Botswana's exports, which fell 29% this year.

=== POST 2: paspaley-says-service-not-stores ===
Family jewellers, not fast-growing chains, are the partners Paspaley wants in the US, its executive director says — the Australian pearl house caps new doors every year.

“We're not looking to grow at any cost,” Chris Paspaley told JCK; brands that added stores too quickly fell behind on service and stock. It sells through 14 US doors today, after opening its first US partnerships in 2024.
