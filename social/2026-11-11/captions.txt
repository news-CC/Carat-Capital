=== POST 1: never-auctioned-then-sold-for-14-6-million ===
A 24-carat pink diamond ring had never been offered at auction. Christie's changed that in Geneva on 10 November, selling it for more than $14.6 million, mid-estimate.

Christie's says the ring, believed to trace to India's Golconda mines, has stayed in one family since the 1950s. Its global head of jewellery called it "the Mona Lisa of pink diamonds."
