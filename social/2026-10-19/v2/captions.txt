=== POST 1: lugano-closes-six-of-ten-stores ===
Clients backed Middle West Partners' purchase of Lugano Diamonds, which leaves Chapter 11 with four of its ten 2025 boutiques still open.

Interim chief executive Josh Gaynor, a Bulgari veteran, is now permanent. No purchase price is disclosed, and Gaynor says plans are too early to discuss.

=== POST 2: a-court-winds-up-petras-treasury-unit ===
A UK court wound up a Petra Diamonds treasury unit over a £5.65 million tax claim — and left open what it means for the group's refinancing.

Petra says PDUK Treasury owns no mines and never received the taxed interest. Questco is running a wider strategic review begun 1 October.
