=== POST 1: movados-profit-jumped-sales-didnt ===
Movado's first-half operating income rose 409% to $21.9 million, on a gross margin of 58.4% against last year's 54.1%. Net sales rose just 6.3% to $312.2 million — margin, not volume, drove most of the gain. Net income reached $19.2 million, up 336%, and diluted EPS $0.84 against $0.20 a year earlier. === POST 2: palladium-fell-eight-times-faster ===
Every metal fell this morning: gold $4,465.40, down 0.15%, while palladium led the retreat, down 1.21%. Silver eased 0.41% to $66.57 and platinum 0.83% to $1,802.00, both between gold's and palladium's moves.
