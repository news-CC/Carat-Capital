=== POST 1: design-starts-at-the-price ===
Gold is $1,300 off its January high, and jewelers are pricing the holidays around it, JCK reports. Goldman Sachs forecasts $4,900 an ounce by year end; ING sees $4,600 — both above today's $4,254.50 spot. McKinsey found 43% of consumers expect to spend less on jewellery over the next three months. === POST 2: finished-pieces-pay-far-more ===
The AGTA told Thai officials that finished jewellery entering the US pays far more than loose coloured stones. Loose stones carry a 12.5% tariff; finished Thai jewellery carries 17.5% to 19.5%, the association says. AGTA met Thailand's vice minister for commerce, then passed the discussion to Washington; no response is reported. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
