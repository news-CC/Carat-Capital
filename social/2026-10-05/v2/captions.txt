=== POST 1: hmrc-seeks-to-wind-up-a-petra-unit ===
HMRC has filed a £5.65m winding-up petition against a Petra Diamonds unit, with a hearing listed for 14 October. It lands a day after Petra's board opened a strategic review that may include asset sales. The company has sold three of its four mines since 2024, leaving only Cullinan.

=== POST 2: caibai-takes-back-its-own-gold-at-fifteen-yuan ===
Caibai's Beijing flagship prices a gold trade-in two ways, and the difference is the receipt. With the slip the fee is 15 yuan a gram, flat; without it, the shop cuts 6% of the weight, then charges 25 yuan a gram on what remains. On 52 grams the gap is 442 yuan.
