=== POST 1: no-cartier-watch-sold-higher ===
A white-gold Cartier Cheich sold for HK$26.52 million, about $3.41 million, at Sotheby's Hong Kong on 18 September &mdash; a record for any Cartier wristwatch. The 2010 commission runs on quartz, the most ever paid for one. It beat Cartier's prior record: $2 million, for a 1991 Cartier Crash at Christie's Geneva. === POST 2: three-goals-one-carries-a-date ===
The Natural Diamond Council renamed itself The Diamond Collective on 22 September, dropping natural from its name. Chief executive Amber Pepper sets three goals: unite, protect, inspire &mdash; only the third carries a date, a fashion partnership in 2027. Critics split: one calls it strong, another doubts a name signals much. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
