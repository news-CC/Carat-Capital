=== POST 1: every-emerald-lot-sold ===
Gemfields sold every one of its 44 commercial-quality emerald lots for $29.6 million, up 16.5% on the last sale. The average realised price reached $9.72 a carat, 30.3% above November-December's $7.46 — the fourth straight rise. Sixty-three companies bid for lots from Kagem in Zambia, the widest field in five auctions, even as carats sold fell 10.5% to 3.05 million. Full story on caratcapital.org, link in bio.

=== POST 2: all-four-metals-fell-since-tuesday ===
Gold, silver, platinum and palladium all traded lower against the 9 September mark, with no tape published on 10 September. Palladium fell hardest at 3.37%, silver close behind at 3.31%, while gold held the smallest loss at 0.96%. Platinum fell 2.39% to $1,797.00 an ounce. Full tape on caratcapital.org, link in bio.
