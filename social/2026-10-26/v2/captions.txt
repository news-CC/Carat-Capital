=== POST 1: what-a-jeweler-saying-synthetic-now-risks ===
What happens when a jeweler calls a lab-grown diamond 'synthetic'?

The Diamond Producers Council told jewelers 20 October to reject CIBJO's word as biased and deceptive, warning of proceedings against deceptive use. It cites a 2016 survey in which 56% read 'synthetic diamond' as an imitation, and says its members grow more than 20 million carats a year.

=== POST 2: ap-charges-sellers-20-percent-to-resell-their-watch ===
Watch brands don't usually run their own resale market. Audemars Piguet just built one, and it charges for it.

The Watch Keepers, AP's certified pre-owned site, charges sellers a fixed commission from 20% down to 10%, not negotiable. It listed 43 watches today, open only to verified EU and Swiss residents.
