Chrono24 watch price index closes Q3 at 1,471, up 0.7%

Haute Time, citing Chrono24's Q3 Quarterly Dial, puts the index 7.5% above a year earlier. Vacheron Constantin led with 17.7% over twelve months, Patek Philippe 17.0%, while Rolex slipped 0.2% in the quarter.

By Carat Capital

27 October 2026

What changed

A fourth rise. Chrono24's ChronoPulse index of pre-owned watch prices rose 0.7% in the third quarter to 1,471 points, Haute Time reports. It was the fourth quarterly rise in a row.

What it means

  • If you price pre-owned Vacheron or Patek: Chrono24 has both brands at 52-week highs at the end of September.
  • If you hold Rolex stock: A 0.2% quarterly dip sits inside a 6.0% twelve-month gain.
  • What this is not: A dealer or auction price. The index is built from Chrono24's own marketplace sales.

Four quarters of gains.

Small, steady steps. The index rose 2.8% in Q1, 1.9% in Q2 and 0.7% in Q3, Haute Time reports. It touched a 52-week high of 1,477 points in August.

A three-year high. At 1,471 the index is 7.5% above a year earlier. Haute Time calls it the highest reading in three years.

Vacheron and Patek lead.

Two Geneva houses. Vacheron Constantin rose 17.7% over twelve months and Patek Philippe 17.0%, on Chrono24's figures. Vacheron added 6.7% in Q3, Patek 3.0%.

Both at highs. Each brand closed September at a 52-week high, Vacheron at 1,670 points and Patek at 2,086, Haute Time reports.

Nautilus carried Patek. Chrono24 says three steel Nautilus references ranked among the index's best performers over twelve months. It does not name them.

Rolex paused in Q3.

A small dip. Rolex slipped 0.2% in the quarter. It gained 2.2% over six months and is 6.0% above a year earlier.

Hublot fell. Hublot was the only tracked brand down over twelve months. Chrono24 does not publish its figure.

What to watch

  • Late January 2027Chrono24's Q4 Quarterly Dial, if its timing holds. The test: a fifth positive quarter.
  • Each quarterRolex's twelve-month gain, 6.0% now. A turn below zero would end the steady run.

The story so far

  1. 12 September 2026Rolex held about 31% of Chrono24's dollar sales volume.
  2. 16 July 2026Chrono24's June data put Cartier at the front of the pre-owned recovery.

Go deeper

What would change this call

A negative Q4, or Vacheron and Patek both falling from their September highs, would make Q3 the top of the run rather than a step in it.

How ChronoPulse is built

Chrono24's index follows secondary-market prices across 13 major brands and more than 140 references. It uses transactions on Chrono24's own marketplace, converted into euros at each day's rate, Haute Time reports.
